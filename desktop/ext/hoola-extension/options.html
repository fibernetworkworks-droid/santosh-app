Hoola extension

Send the page you are reading to your Hoola conversation - right-click > "Send to Hoola", or press Ctrl+Shift+H. Pairing works just like a PC: the phone's 6-digit code.

3 steps to add it to the browser the first time
  1. Open chrome://extensions (type it in the address bar)
  2. Turn on Developer mode (top right)
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose this folder:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Hoola\chrome-extension
