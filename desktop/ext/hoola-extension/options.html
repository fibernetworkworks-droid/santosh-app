Hoola extension

Jis page par padh rahi hain use Hoola ki conversation mein bhejiye - right-click > "Hoola ko bhejiye", ya Ctrl+Shift+H. Pairing bilkul PC jaisi: phone ka 6 anko ka code.

Pehli baar browser mein lagane ke 3 kadam
  1. chrome://extensions kholiye (address bar mein likhiye)
  2. Right-top Developer mode on kijiye
  3. Load unpacked dabaiye aur yeh folder chuniye:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Hoola\chrome-extension
