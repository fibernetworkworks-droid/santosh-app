---
name: meeting-minutes
description: Turn a meeting or call into minutes with owners and dates
---
GOAL: minutes someone can act on a week later.

1. Open with one line: date, who was there, what the meeting was FOR.
2. Decisions first: each decision as its own line, with WHO decided it.
3. Actions table: task | owner (a name, never 'team') | due date. A task
   without an owner or a date is a wish - flag it back.
4. Open questions: what could not be settled, and what it needs to be settled.
5. Numbers exactly as stated (amounts, dates, quantities) - never rounded,
   never paraphrased into different figures.
6. Keep her language (Hinglish stays Hinglish); quotes stay verbatim.
7. End with 'agla kadam': the single next thing that happens next.
