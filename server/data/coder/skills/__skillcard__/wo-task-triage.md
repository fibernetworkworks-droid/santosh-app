name: wo-task-triage
description: Task triage - email and kaam into one morning queue
---
GOAL: subah ka pehla kaam - inbox aur kaamon ko EK pakki queue me
torana, taaki din decision se shuru ho, dhundne se nahi. Syllabus:
awesome-productivity ke lists/inbox-zero/kanban patterns (generic
reference) - par door Hoola ke asli tools hain. Ye starter
morning-briefing skill ke SAATH chalta hai: briefing BRIEF deta hai
(kya hua, kya pending - dekhna); ye TRIAGE deta hai (har cheez ka
FAISLA - abhi/baad/kisiko/nahi). Pehle brief padho, phir triage
chalao - dekhna alag, faisla alag. Ek hi list, ek hi jagah, ek hi
sach - bas wahi nahi hai.

MORNING QUEUE (ek slot, pakka time - subah ek baar, poora din nahi):
1. email_triage: har mail ko bucket do - ABHI (is waqt ka jawab), BAAD
   (likhit date ke saath aage), DELEGATE (sahi bande ko, approval
   ke baad), DELETE-NAHI-SNOOZE (kuch bhi delete nahi; noise ko
   snooze/archive). Har mail se sirf ek bucket nikle - do baar
   mat socho, pehla faisla pakdo.
2. email_rule: jo pattern baar-baar dikha (newsletter, notifications),
   uska rule banao - agli baar woh mail triage me aayega hi nahi;
   rule banana one-time mehnat, har subah hai bachat.
3. email_snooze: baad wale mails din se bahar - calendar se bahar,
   dimaag se bahar. Snooze ki date likhi ho to wahi pakki date.
4. email_follow_up: jinke jawab ka intezaar hai, follow-up list pe -
   waapas aane wali cheezein kho nahi jaati. Har follow-up ke saath
   likho ki kya yaad dilana hai.
5. kaam_plan + kaam_status: is waqt ke kaam aur chalu kaamon ki status
   nikaalo, email se aaye naye kaamon ke saath MERGE karo - EK list
   me. list_add se daalo, list_done se band karo, list_show se din
   bhar dekho. Do alag jagah, do sach nahi. Merge karte waqt
   duplicate pakdo - wahi kaam email aur kaam dono me ho to ek hi
   rakho, source note kar do.

RULES: har item ke saath NEXT ACTION ek line - "Ravi ko quote
bhejna, 4 baje tak" jaisa; 3-line essay nahi. Order pakka: pehle
email buckets, phir rules/snooze/follow-up, phir kaam merge - ek hi
pass me, dobara loop mat lagao. Delegate ya owner ke
naam se kuch bhi jana = pehle ask_approval. Har bucket move ka
record printed output me - evidence hi sach hai, yaad nahi. Queue
bani to use owner ko dikha do - bina bheje, report ke roop me;
bhejna ho to pehle ask_approval.

NEVER: mail delete karna (snooze/archive only - delete kabhi nahi);
owner ke naam se auto-reply bina ask_approval; list_done bina
evidence (output dekhe bina "done" nahi); ek item me 5 actions
(aage badhne wala ek hi action likho); triage ko poora din banana -
ek slot, pakka; private data tenant se bahar.
