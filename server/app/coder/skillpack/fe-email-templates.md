GOAL: campaign/marketing emails are ASSEMBLED from proven template structures (the
awesome-emails read), filled with HER facts - subject line se CTA tak ek hi shape,
har mailbox mein pahunchti hai.

THE LANE (existing doors only):
- email_draft markdown=true likho - **bold**, *italic*, [links](url), bullets,
  headings. NEVER raw HTML (markdown render hota hai, HTML nahi).
- Ek hi mail ko list par bhejna hai -> email_bulk (mail merge; approval card uske
  tap par). Marketing publish -> kalam action=publish (card).
- Attachments: {drive_file_id} ya file_id - jab tak usne maanga nahi, attachment nahi.

THE TEMPLATE LIBRARY (structure + fill):
- OFFER/SALE: subject "Diwali sale - [dukaan naam] par [X]% tak" (<=45 chars);
  first line = preheader (inbox preview); 3 short lines offer ka saar; bullet list
  (3-5 items); ONE CTA link; last line dukaan ka naam + sheher.
- NEWSLETTER: subject "Is hafte: [topic]"; 3-4 sections, har section 2 lines +
  ek link; consistent order (top update -> baaki -> ek tip).
- APPOINTMENT/PAYMENT REMINDER: subject "Reminder: [cheez] [tareekh]"; ek line
  kya, ek line kab, ek line kya karna hai (link ya reply); warm tone, blame nahi.
- FESTIVAL GREETING: subject "[festival] ki shubhkamnayein"; 2 lines dil se;
  ek soft CTA (optional); greeting pehle, bech pan baad mein.
- PRODUCT LAUNCH: subject "Naya [product] - [ek fayda]"; problem (1 line) ->
  solution (2 lines) -> 3 bullets -> CTA; price sirf tab jab usne di ho.
- FEEDBACK REQUEST: subject "2 minute? [kaam] ka feedback"; kyun matter karta hai
  (1 line); ek link ya ek sawaal; thanks.

THE LAWS:
- Uske facts hi: price, date, naam, review - jo usne diya. Unknown = [blank]
  chhodo aur poochho. Kabhi invented number/award/guarantee nahi (kalam ka law).
- Subject <=45 chars, pehli line = preheader, ONE CTA, paras 2-3 lines.
- SPAM shape se bacho: ALL CAPS, !!!, "URGENT", fake "Re:" - nahi.
- Bulk par consent/unsubscribe line; jo list usne di hi (bought/rented list kabhi).
NEVER: OTP/password/card/Aadhaar number mail mein; misleading subject; bina
approval card bulk; bhejne ka dawa tool result se pehle.
