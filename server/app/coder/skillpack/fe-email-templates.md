GOAL: campaign/marketing emails are ASSEMBLED from proven template structures (the
awesome-emails read), filled with HER facts - one shape from the subject line to
the CTA, one that reaches every mailbox.

THE LANE (existing doors only):
- Write email_draft markdown=true - **bold**, *italic*, [links](url), bullets,
  headings. NEVER raw HTML (markdown is rendered, HTML is not).
- To send the same mail to a list -> email_bulk (mail merge; the approval card
  waits for their tap). Marketing publish -> kalam action=publish (card).
- Attachments: {drive_file_id} or file_id - no attachment unless they asked for it.

THE TEMPLATE LIBRARY (structure + fill):
- OFFER/SALE: subject "Diwali sale - up to [X]% at [shop name]" (<=45 chars);
  first line = preheader (inbox preview); 3 short lines summarising the offer;
  bullet list (3-5 items); ONE CTA link; last line shop name + city.
- NEWSLETTER: subject "This week: [topic]"; 3-4 sections, each 2 lines +
  one link; consistent order (top update -> the rest -> one tip).
- APPOINTMENT/PAYMENT REMINDER: subject "Reminder: [item] [date]"; one line
  what, one line when, one line what to do (link or reply); warm tone, no blame.
- FESTIVAL GREETING: subject "Happy [festival]"; 2 lines from the heart;
  one soft CTA (optional); the greeting first, selling only after.
- PRODUCT LAUNCH: subject "New [product] - [one benefit]"; problem (1 line) ->
  solution (2 lines) -> 3 bullets -> CTA; price only when they gave it.
- FEEDBACK REQUEST: subject "2 minutes? Feedback on [job]"; why it matters
  (1 line); one link or one question; thanks.

THE LAWS:
- Only THEIR facts: price, date, name, review - what they gave. Unknown = leave
  a [blank] and ask. Never an invented number/award/guarantee (the kalam law).
- Subject <=45 chars, first line = preheader, ONE CTA, paragraphs of 2-3 lines.
- Avoid SPAM shape: no ALL CAPS, !!!, "URGENT", fake "Re:".
- On bulk, a consent/unsubscribe line; only the list they gave (never a
  bought/rented list).
NEVER: an OTP/password/card/Aadhaar number in a mail; a misleading subject; bulk
without the approval card; claiming it was sent before the tool result.
