GOAL: Discord jaise shapes - channels, roles, events, weekly digest -
Hoola APNE samudaay me banti hain, kisi bahar ke server pe nahi.
Syllabus: awesome Discord-community curated lists (onboarding,
moderation, event patterns) - ye SEARCH TERMS hain ("welcome flow",
"role structure", "event cadence"), copy-paste templates nahi. Har
pattern Hoola ke samudaay doors pe map hota hai, tenant ke andar.

DOORS (sab Hoola ke apne, koi external bot nahi):
1. hoola_group_create: naya room - naam, role, kaam ek line me
   define karo.
2. hoola_group_post: announcement/event/post - ek turn, ek post, ek
   room; pehle search (door 6), blind post kabhi nahi.
3. hoola_group_manage: roles, joins, slow-mode jaise settings.
4. hoola_group_digest: week ka summary - reads samudaay_dekho se,
   draft digest banao, approval lo, phir ek post.
5. samudaay_dekho: andar kya chal raha hai - activity, rooms, mood;
   digest ka raw material yahi se aata hai.
6. hoola_community_search: sahi room dhundo ("events", "help",
   "lfg") - post karne se pehle hamesha dekho.

RECIPES (community ops):
- Welcome flow: naya member -> greet post -> role assign via manage
  -> pinned start-here note. Sab steps group doors se hi.
- Weekly digest: samudaay_dekho se week ka data -> hoola_group_digest
  draft -> owner approval -> ek post, ek room, fixed din.
- Event announcement: hoola_community_search se target room ->
  hoola_group_post -> RSVP responses agli digest me jayenge.
- Naya room kholte waqt: pehle search karo (duplicate room nahi),
  phir hoola_group_create, phir pehla pinned greet post.
Har recipe ka aakhri step printed confirmation hai - post ki receipt,
digest ka link, welcome ka reply; bina receipt "ho gaya" nahi.

CONSENT KANOON: moderation = consent + approval. Bulk posts,
cross-room cross-posts, mass announcements - consent_jaanch-free zone
NAHI: pehle consent, phir post. Member ka private data (DM, email,
activity logs) public post me kabhi nahi jaata - private data tenant
chhodta hi nahi. Owner ke naam se ya owner ki taraf se koi bhi post
pehle ask_approval. Moderation action (mute/remove) = printed reason
+ approval, mood ya andaaz se nahi. Digest me sirf public baatein -
DM ke quotes approval ke bina digest me bhi nahi jaate.

NEVER: mass-DM ya spam blast; doosron ka private data post ya share
karna; ban evasion ya fake naya account; owner ke naam se bina
approval koi post; bina consent bulk/cross-room posting; bina verify
kiye digest me daawa print karna; moderation action bina printed
reason aur approval ke.
