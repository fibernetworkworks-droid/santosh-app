GOAL: the first job of the morning - sort the inbox and the tasks into
ONE firm queue, so the day starts with decisions, not with searching.
Syllabus: the lists / inbox-zero / kanban patterns of
awesome-productivity (a generic reference) - but the doors are Hoola's
real tools. This starter runs TOGETHER with the morning-briefing
skill: the briefing gives the BRIEF (what happened, what is pending -
looking); this gives the TRIAGE (a DECISION on every item - now /
later / to someone / no). Read the brief first, then run the triage -
looking is one thing, deciding is another. One list, one place, one
truth.

MORNING QUEUE (one slot at a fixed time - once in the morning, not all
day):
1. email_triage: give every mail a bucket - NOW (answer at this
   time), LATER (moved ahead with a written date), DELEGATE (to the
   right person, after approval), SNOOZE-NOT-DELETE (delete nothing;
   snooze / archive the noise). Exactly one bucket per mail - do not
   think twice, keep the first decision.
2. email_rule: for a pattern that keeps showing up (newsletters,
   notifications) make a rule - next time that mail never reaches
   the triage; a rule is one-time effort and a saving every morning.
3. email_snooze: later mails go out of the day - out of the
   calendar, out of mind. When a snooze date is written, that date
   is final.
4. email_follow_up: mails waiting for a reply go on the follow-up
   list - things that must come back do not get lost. With every
   follow-up write what to remind about.
5. kaam_plan + kaam_status: pull the current tasks and the status of
   running jobs, and MERGE them with the new tasks that came from
   email - into ONE list. Add with list_add, close with list_done,
   look through the day with list_show. Not two places, not two
   truths. Catch duplicates while merging - if the same task is in
   both email and tasks, keep one and note the source.

RULES: a one-line NEXT ACTION with every item - like "Send Ravi the
quote by 4 pm"; not a 3-line essay. The order is fixed: email buckets
first, then rules / snooze / follow-up, then the task merge - in one
pass, do not loop again. Delegating, or anything going out in the
owner's name = ask_approval first. Every bucket move is recorded in
the printed output - evidence is the truth, not memory. When the
queue is built, show it to the owner - as a report, without sending
it; to send it, ask_approval first.

NEVER: delete a mail (snooze / archive only - never delete);
auto-reply in the owner's name without ask_approval; list_done
without evidence (no "done" without seeing the output); 5 actions in
one item (write only the one action that moves it forward); turn the
triage into the whole day - one slot, fixed; private data outside the
tenant.
