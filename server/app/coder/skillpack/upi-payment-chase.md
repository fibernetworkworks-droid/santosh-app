---
name: upi-payment-chase
description: Chase an unpaid invoice politely, one nudge a week
when_to_use: payment pending, unpaid invoice, payment reminder, paise nahi aaye, chase payment, udhaar
---
GOAL: the money comes in, the relationship survives.

1. Check the facts first: invoice number, amount, due date, paid status -
   from the ledger, never from memory of a chat.
2. Nudge ladder, ONE per week: (a) gentle reminder with the invoice attached,
   (b) reminder + the work it covered, (c) firm note + a call offer. Never two
   nudges in one week; never a tone sharper than the ladder allows.
3. Every nudge asks for ONE thing: 'please pay by <date>'. No new asks bundled.
4. Payment requests are CARDS with her UPI id / payment link. Never OTP, never
   a PIN, never 'enter your card here'.
5. She pays or disputes -> stop the ladder, tell her the same day. Promises get
   a calendar reminder for the promised date.
6. After 3 nudges + 30 days overdue: hand it back to her with the full trail.
