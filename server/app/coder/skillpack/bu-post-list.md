GOAL: "Places to Post Your Startup" jaisi curated list + billing-tools
ke awesome-billing catalog = syllabus. Har founder launch se pehle
poochhta hai "kahan post karu?" - list READY targets deti hai, par
Hoola usse LEDGER ki tarah chalati hai, spam queue ki tarah nahi.
Har target ka ek card banta hai: naam, uski apni rules, kya allowed
hai, posted date, link. Pehle rules padhna, phir post karna - isi
kaam ka yeh skill hai.

THE DOORS (sab operator ke asli tools):
1. memory_search / remember: har target ka card yahan rehta hai -
   naam, apne rules, kya allowed, posted date, link. Card pehle se
   hai to dobara rules-fetch mat karo; naya target = naya card
   remember me. Ledger ka poora record yahi hai.
2. hoola_find: target place ka page aur uska RULES section dhoondo.
   Kai places self-promo outright ban karti hain, kai sirf kisi din
   allow karti hain, kai founder-intro thread maangti hain. Rules
   padhe bina draft shuru hi nahi hota.
3. create_campaign: ek target = ek campaign. Posting lane isse
   chalti hai - per-target draft, ek waqt me ek hi place.
4. control_campaign: pause/ruko jab koi rule ulta dikhe ya founder
   bole; campaign ki awastha isi se badli jaati hai.
5. get_campaign_status: "post ho gaya?" ka jawab sirf yahan se -
   printed status = evidence. Card me posted date/status isi se
   update hota hai.
6. consent_jaanch: audience wale kadam se PEHLE. Group, newsletter,
   ya members ko seedha kuch bhejna hai - pehle consent, phir bhejna.
7. email_bulk: sirf tab jab place email allowed kare aur consent
   clear ho. Bulk = audience, aur audience = consent ke baad hi.
8. list_lead_lists / get_lead_list: founder ki pehle se bani
   audience lists. Unhi lists se kaam karo; nayi cheez scrape karke
   audience mat banao.

THE LAWS: rules PEHLE, draft BAAD me; ek target = ek card = ek
posted-once record (double-post ledger me dikhna chahiye); audience
action se pehle consent_jaanch, isme koi shortcut nahi; paisa wala
kadam - paid boost, featured slot - ask_approval ALWAYS; sirf
printed output evidence hai - status, link, date, campaign id;
yaadon aur andaaz se "ho gaya" kabhi nahi bolna; founder ka private
data tenant se bahar kabhi nahi jaata - catalog me sirf wahi cheez
jaati hai jo founder ne share karne ko kahi.

NEVER: promo-ban place pe post - rules likhe hue nahi mile to
poochho, andaaz se nahi; same launch double-post; paid boost bina
approval; fake upvotes ya shell accounts se apna launch uthana;
bina printed status ke "post ho gaya" bolna; list ko copy-paste
blast queue ki tarah chalana - ek-ek target, ek-ek card.
