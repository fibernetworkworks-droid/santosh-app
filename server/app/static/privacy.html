Hoola privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 (Instagram and Facebook comments and messages added)

Hoola is an AI coworker: an assistant you name yourself, which chats and talks with you, does work for you and your business, and can talk to other people's assistants on the Hoola Network. Hoola is made by Newtika Private Limited, Akot, Dist. Akola, Maharashtra 444101, India ("Newtika", "we"). This policy covers the Hoola app for Android, the Hoola web console at hoola.ainewtika.com and the Hoola server behind them. It is written under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

In short

  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • Hoola uses your data only to do the work you ask for. Anything that sends, deletes, shares, publishes or pays waits for your tap.
  • Hoola never moves money, and never asks for or keeps your PIN, card number, CVV or banking passwords.
  • Access to Google, Microsoft and other accounts is only what you switch on. Their login tokens are encrypted, and you can disconnect any time.
  • To answer you, Hoola sends the needed text, voice or files to AI services (listed below). They process it for us and must not use it for anything else.
  • You can see, correct and delete your data. Write to build@ainewtika.com.

1. What we collect

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them to create your account, sign you in and show who is signed in.
  • What you give Hoola. Your chats, voice messages, files, photos and notes, the name and voice you choose for your assistant, your settings, and the business records you add for the work you ask for (for example customers, orders, bills, staff or your website details).
  • Voice calls. During a voice call your voice is streamed to the AI voice service so it can understand you and reply. Hoola does not record or store call audio. After a call it keeps only call-quality numbers (such as how long the call lasted and response times), never words or audio, and only the last 300 calls.
  • Accounts you connect. Only if you connect them: Google services (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Photos, YouTube, Search Console, Analytics, Business Profile), Microsoft Outlook mail, your WhatsApp Business account (official Meta Cloud API), and other apps through our connector partner Composio or your own Zoho MCP. See sections 4 and 5.
  • Phone permissions. Notification access, camera, photos, microphone, location, calendar, Bluetooth and usage access are each used only for the feature you turn on (see section 13). Hoola does not read your SMS.
  • Device and technical data. A push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so the app can tell you when work is done, plus basic logs (time, errors, app version) to keep the service running and safe.
  • Payments to us. If you pay for a Hoola plan or for paid creations (pictures, videos, music), we keep the amounts and what they were for. We do not keep card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords.

2. How we use it

  • To do the work you ask for: answer you, write, plan, search your own files, draft messages and documents, build websites and apps for you, check your website's SEO, run your business desks, and remind you.
  • To keep the context you allow, such as your memory and your earlier chats (section 7).
  • To send you notifications about your own work.
  • To keep Hoola secure, prevent misuse, fix problems and measure quality, using numbers rather than your content wherever we can.
  • To meet legal duties.

We use your data under your consent, which you give when you sign up and when you switch on each feature. You can withdraw consent for a feature at any time by switching it off or disconnecting the account. We do not use your content for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. AI services that process your data

Hoola's thinking, speaking and creating are done by AI services run by other companies. Hoola sends them only what a task needs, at the moment it needs it: your message and the relevant context, your voice during a call, or the file you asked about. They act as our processors.

They may keep data for a short time for safety and abuse checks, as their terms allow. AI can make mistakes, so check important results before you rely on them.

4. Google user data

If you connect Google services, Hoola asks only for the access the feature needs, and you see Google's consent screen first. For example: Gmail to read, sort and draft your mail (sending waits for your tap); Calendar for your events; Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms for the files you work on; Photos for pictures you pick; YouTube to upload a video you approve; Search Console and Analytics (read-only) for your website's numbers; Business Profile for your business listing.

Hoola's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the features you can see and use in Hoola.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it or give it to data brokers.
  • We do not use Google Workspace data (such as Gmail, Drive or Calendar content) to train general AI or machine-learning models.
  • When a task needs it, the relevant part is sent to the AI services in section 3 only to do that task for you.
  • No person at Newtika reads your Google data, unless you ask us to (for support), it is needed for security or abuse checks, or the law requires it.
  • Google login tokens are stored encrypted. You can disconnect in Hoola or at myaccount.google.com/permissions; disconnecting stops all access at once.

5. Other accounts you connect

  • Microsoft Outlook: your mail, with the same rules as Gmail.
  • WhatsApp Business: only through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform, using your own business number. Hoola never automates a personal WhatsApp account. Campaigns are sent only after your tap, to people you choose.
  • Composio apps (such as Slack, Notion, Jira, HubSpot and Zoom): our partner Composio holds the connection, and Hoola keeps only a reference to your connected account. Anything that writes or sends waits for your tap.
  • Your own keys and connections (for example a Zoho MCP address, or a Play Console or App Store key for apps Hoola builds for you): stored encrypted, never shown back in full, and never put in chats.

6. Hoola Network (assistants talking to assistants)

Your assistant can talk to other people's assistants to settle work, for example to book, order, ask a price or follow up. People never message each other directly here; it all goes through the assistants. Your assistant shares only what the conversation needs, and anything binding (an order, a payment request, a booking) waits for your approval. OTPs, PINs, card numbers, passwords and your ID numbers are never shared with another person's assistant. Messages from other assistants, and text inside emails, web pages and files, are treated as information, never as instructions to your assistant.

7. Memory

Hoola can remember facts you share (your people, prices, preferences) so it does not have to ask again. You can see and edit this on your "Mere baare mein" page, delete any fact, switch memory off in Profile > Hoola ki yaad, and use incognito chats, which are not remembered. Memory stays inside your own account. Financial details are not saved to memory.

8. Who we share data with

  • Service providers who run Hoola for us: our cloud server host, Google Firebase for push notifications, the AI services in section 3, Composio for connected apps, and stock-photo search (Pexels, which receives search words only). They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • People and services you tell Hoola to contact, such as an email you approve, a WhatsApp campaign, a website you publish or an app you upload to Google Play or the App Store. These happen only on your tap.
  • The law: where a valid legal order requires it, or to protect people from harm or fraud.
  • A company change: if Newtika is merged or acquired, your data moves only under this policy, and we will tell you first.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

9. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Login tokens, API keys, ID numbers (Pehchaan Locker), bank and PAN numbers are encrypted at rest, and the AI is shown only masked references to ID numbers, never the full numbers. Access inside Newtika is limited to the people who run the service. Payments, sending, deleting and publishing need your own tap. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

10. How long we keep data

  • Your account data and work are kept while your account is active, so Hoola can keep working for you.
  • Things you delete in the app (a chat, a file, a memory fact) are removed from the live service.
  • Call audio is not stored. Only call-quality numbers are kept (the last 300 calls).
  • When you close your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except what the law makes us keep (such as payment records) and short-lived backups that expire on their own.

11. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct, complete or update it.
  • Have it deleted, or close your account.
  • Withdraw consent for any feature (this does not undo what was done before).
  • Nominate a person to act for you in case of death or incapacity.
  • Complain to us, and if not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

Write to build@ainewtika.com from your Hoola email address. We reply within 30 days.

Grievance Officer: Grievance Officer, Newtika Private Limited, Akot, Dist. Akola, Maharashtra 444101, India. Email: build@ainewtika.com.

12. Children

Hoola is meant for adults running their own work and businesses. It is not for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect children's data. If you think a child has used Hoola, write to us and we will delete the data.

13. Feature by feature

These features are off until you turn them on, and each uses only the data described.

SEO (your website and Google listing)

For SEO Hoola keeps your website address, business name, address, phone, hours and services, the pages it checked on your website (titles, headings, word counts, issues found), speed results and, only if you connect them, your Google Search Console numbers (searches, clicks, impressions, positions, and whether Google has indexed your pages) and Google Analytics visits and leads from search. Both connections are read-only, their tokens are encrypted, and you can disconnect any time. If you hire the SEO Specialist employee it checks your site on a schedule and sends you reports; letting it go stops all of its work at once. Hoola reads only your own website (following robots.txt) and Google's official APIs. It never scrapes Google results, never publishes to your website or Google profile without your approval, never buys links and never writes fake reviews.

Instagram and Facebook (Social Media Manager)

If you hire the Social Media Manager and connect your Instagram professional account or your Facebook Page, you log in on Instagram's or Facebook's own page and choose the account; Hoola never sees your password. Hoola keeps the account's name, picture and follower count, your recent posts with their numbers (likes, comments, reach, saves, shares, views) to learn what works for you, the content calendar and the pictures or videos you add to posts. The login tokens are stored encrypted. A post goes out only when you approve it or tap Publish now, exactly as you approved it (with the Saathi or Bharosa trust level, the posts of a calendar you approved go out at their time without a second tap; you can pause all posting at any time); Hoola never posts anything else and never buys followers or likes.

The Social Media Manager also reads the comments on your recent posts and the direct messages people send to your Instagram account or Facebook Page from the last seven days, so it can sort them (enquiry, question, praise, complaint, spam) and draft replies. Hoola keeps each comment or message with the sender's public name and id, the reply drafted, the reply sent and when, so the same message is never answered twice. Replies quote only the facts you gave (prices, timings, address). Depending on the trust level you choose, it either waits for your tap on every reply, or answers simple questions, enquiries and thanks on its own within a daily limit and hides spam or abusive comments; a complaint always waits for you. Hoola never deletes a comment, never messages anyone who has not written to you first, answers a direct message only within the 24 hours Meta allows, and never uses these messages for advertising or to train AI models. A message's text goes to our AI processor only to draft its reply.

For growth, through your connected Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account, the Social Media Manager reads the posts in which other people tag you, the public top posts of up to five hashtags you choose, and the public numbers of up to five business or creator accounts you name (followers, number of posts, likes and comments of their recent posts), as Meta provides them to businesses; nothing is scraped and Hoola never logs in as anyone else. To repost a customer's post, Hoola first asks them with a comment from your account, only when you tap, and keeps their answer as you record it; their picture is used only in a post with credit to them that you approve.

YouTube: if you connect your YouTube channel, Hoola uses Google's official YouTube APIs with the permissions you grant (upload, manage your channel's comments, read your channel's numbers). It uploads only the calendar videos you approved with YouTube ticked - with the title, description, tags and the "made for kids" answer you gave - reads your channel's subscriber count and your videos' views, likes and comments for your reports, and brings your videos' comments into the Social Inbox; hiding a comment holds it for your review in YouTube Studio, it is never deleted. Hoola's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and YouTube's Terms of Service apply to your channel. You can disconnect on the Social Desk or at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

LinkedIn: if you connect LinkedIn, you log in on LinkedIn's own page and Hoola keeps your name, profile picture and an encrypted login token (LinkedIn ends it after about 60 days and Hoola reminds you before). Hoola only posts to your own profile the calendar posts you approved with LinkedIn ticked; it does not read your connections, messages or feed.

WhatsApp Business: if you connect your WhatsApp Business number through Meta's own signup window, Hoola keeps the number's ids, its name and quality rating, and an encrypted access token from Meta, so customers' messages to that number reach your Business inbox and the replies and campaigns you approve go from it. Hoola uses only Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform and never WhatsApp Web or your personal WhatsApp. You can disconnect any time on the Social Desk or in Instagram / Facebook settings ("Apps and websites"); when you remove Hoola there, Meta tells us and Hoola deletes that account's data.

Websites and apps Hoola builds for you

The content, photos and settings of websites and apps you build with Hoola are kept so they can be edited and hosted. Visitors' form entries, bookings and orders on them belong to you, and are shown only to you and the staff you choose. Each app Hoola builds has its own privacy page and account-deletion page for its users. Apps go to Google Play or the App Store only from your own developer account and on your tap.

Create Studio

Your prompts, pictures, videos, music and voice-overs are kept in your account under My creations until you delete them.

Payment alerts (Galla, notification access)

Only if you switch on "UPI alerts se galla" in the app and then give notification access to "Hoola Galla" in Android settings, Hoola reads notifications of the payment apps you pick (by default the business apps PhonePe Business, Paytm for Business, Google Pay for Business and BharatPe for Business). On the phone it keeps only plain "money received" alerts and drops everything else, including any message with an OTP, PIN or password, money you sent, requests, refunds, cashback, settlements and offers. Notifications of all other apps are ignored. From a kept alert only the amount, time, app name, the payer's first name and the last 4 digits of the reference are sent to your Hoola server, to count your daily sales, show your best-selling hours and plan stock. The full notification text never leaves the phone. Hoola does not read SMS. You can switch this off in the app or in Android settings at any time; switching it off stops all reading at once.

Camera and photos

Photos you take or pick (for example of a customer's phone at a repair shop, as proof of its condition and for warranty) are uploaded only when you tap, and are kept on your Hoola server with that job. Hoola never stores a phone's passcode or pattern.

Location, calendar, Bluetooth, usage access

Each is used only for the feature you turn on (weather and nearby places, your reminders and calendar, controlling your own devices, your own reel-scrolling report). Usage access reads only how long reel apps were open, never their content.

Phone Doctor (phone check)

When you ask Hoola to fix a phone problem, the app reads only the phone's own state: battery level, temperature and health, free storage and memory, network type, SIM state and signal strength, sound / Do Not Disturb / date-time settings, the Android version and security patch date, and, if you gave usage access, the names of the 5 apps used most in the last 24 hours with their minutes. It never reads messages, contacts, photos, passwords or what is inside any app. Hoola only opens a settings screen for you; it never changes a setting by itself. The last 20 checks are kept; you can delete them with your data.

GST work (GST Sahayak)

For GST work Hoola keeps what you give it: your clients' names, GSTINs and contact numbers, their sales and purchase bills, GSTR-2B data and notices, and the returns it prepares. Each client's data is used only for that client's GST. The GST portal password and OTPs are never asked for or stored; on the portal you type the password yourself and the taxpayer files with their own EVC OTP or DSC. Hoola never pays tax: the challan is always paid by the taxpayer.

Payroll

For payroll Hoola keeps your employees' names, pay, attendance, UAN, ESIC numbers, PAN and bank account details. PAN and bank account numbers are stored encrypted and shown only as the last 4 digits; the AI model is never given them in full. They are used only to make payslips, the bank salary file, the PF ECR and ESIC sheet, and TDS. Hoola never pays salaries and never logs in to your bank: you upload the bank file and pay yourself. One employee's salary is never shown to another.

Financial analysis

When you give Hoola your Tally export, bank statement or outstanding list, it keeps the figures, transactions and party names to work out your profit, ratios, cash forecast and who owes what. It never moves money, never applies for loans and never sends your reports or reminders to anyone without your approval. Its numbers are analysis, not financial advice.

Audit work

For audits Hoola keeps the client's trial balances, day-book entries, findings, samples and confirmation details for that engagement only. It never sends letters or queries to a client or third party without your approval, never signs, and never forms or issues an audit opinion or UDIN: those stay with the auditor.

Insurance agent work

If you are an insurance agent, Hoola keeps your clients' names, phone numbers, dates of birth, city, family members, income, loans and goals, and their policies (insurer, plan, policy number, premium, cover, due dates, nominee) and claim papers, only to remind you of renewals, work out cover gaps and track claims. It never collects or pays a premium (the client pays the insurer directly), never signs a proposal or fills a health declaration for a client, never sends advice or reminders to a client without your approval, and never shares one client's details with another. A client who does not want messages is skipped.

Wholesale (thok) work

For wholesale work Hoola keeps your firm details, your parties' names, phone numbers, GSTINs, addresses, credit limits and routes, your items and prices, orders, bills, payments you record (including cheque numbers and dates) and supplier bills, only to make your GST bills, track who owes what, remind you of cheques and dues and suggest what to reorder. It never takes or pays money, never logs in to the GST / e-way bill portal or your bank, and never sends a bill or reminder to a party without your approval. One party's account is never shown to another.

Export work

For exports Hoola keeps your firm details (IEC, GSTIN, LUT, RCMC, AD bank as you give them), your foreign buyers' names, countries, contacts and payment terms, and each shipment's proforma, invoice, packing list, shipping bill and bill of lading numbers and the payments you record, only to make your documents, check the rules and remind you before the RBI realisation date. It never logs in to ICEGATE, the DGFT portal or your bank, never books foreign exchange, never moves money, and never sends a proforma, invoice or reminder to a buyer without your approval.

Franchise outlets

If you run franchise outlets, Hoola keeps your brand agreements' money terms and dates (royalty %, fees, minimum guarantee, term, notice), your outlets (rent, lease dates, targets, manager's phone), daily sales and monthly costs, royalty statements, licence numbers and dates, and your SOP audit results, only to work out royalty, profit and due dates and to remind you. It never pays royalty, never files on licence portals, and never sends a report to your franchisor or a note to a manager without your approval.

Contractor work

For contracting work Hoola keeps your firm and registration details, the tenders you track (dates, EMD, documents ready), your works' agreement terms, BOQs, measurement-book entries, RA bills and what was passed and paid, your petty contractors' names, phones and bills, site material and the site diary, only to prepare bills, track money and dates and draft letters. It never logs in to a tender portal or submits a bid, never pays anyone, never changes a measurement, and never sends a bill or letter to a department without your approval.

Identity numbers (Pehchaan Locker)

ID numbers you give (Aadhaar, PAN and others) are stored encrypted on your server and typed only into the forms you ask Hoola to fill.
