Hoola privacy policy

Hoola (the Santosh app and its server) is made by Newtika Private Limited, Akot, Dist. Akola, Maharashtra 444101, India. Contact: build@ainewtika.com. Last updated: 21 October 2026.

Where your data lives

The app talks only to the Hoola server you connect it to (your own or your business's server). Your chats, files, notes and settings are stored there. API keys stay on that server or in the app, never in chats. We do not sell data and do not show ads.

Payment alerts (Galla, notification access)

Only if you switch on "UPI alerts se galla" in the app and then give notification access to "Hoola Galla" in Android settings, Hoola reads notifications of the payment apps you pick (by default the business apps PhonePe Business, Paytm for Business, Google Pay for Business and BharatPe for Business). On the phone it keeps only plain "money received" alerts and drops everything else, including any message with an OTP, PIN or password, money you sent, requests, refunds, cashback, settlements and offers. Notifications of all other apps are ignored. From a kept alert only the amount, time, app name, the payer's first name and the last 4 digits of the reference are sent to your Hoola server, to count your daily sales, show your best-selling hours and plan stock. The full notification text never leaves the phone. Hoola does not read SMS. You can switch this off in the app or in Android settings at any time; switching it off stops all reading at once.

Camera and photos

Photos you take or pick (for example of a customer's phone at a repair shop, as proof of its condition and for warranty) are uploaded only when you tap, and are kept on your Hoola server with that job. Hoola never stores a phone's passcode or pattern.

Microphone, calls and voice

The microphone is used only while you talk to Hoola (a voice call you start, or the wake word if you turn it on). Voice recognition of who is speaking is optional and stays on your server.

Location, calendar, Bluetooth, usage access

Each is used only for the feature you turn on (weather and nearby places, your reminders and calendar, controlling your own devices, your own reel-scrolling report). Usage access reads only how long reel apps were open, never their content.

Phone Doctor (phone check)

When you ask Hoola to fix a phone problem, the app reads only the phone's own state: battery level, temperature and health, free storage and memory, network type, SIM state and signal strength, sound / Do Not Disturb / date-time settings, the Android version and security patch date, and, if you gave usage access, the names of the 5 apps used most in the last 24 hours with their minutes. It never reads messages, contacts, photos, passwords or what is inside any app. Hoola only opens a settings screen for you; it never changes a setting by itself. The last 20 checks are kept; you can delete them with your data.

GST work (GST Sahayak)

For GST work Hoola keeps what you give it: your clients' names, GSTINs and contact numbers, their sales and purchase bills, GSTR-2B data and notices, and the returns it prepares. Each client's data is used only for that client's GST. The GST portal password and OTPs are never asked for or stored; on the portal you type the password yourself and the taxpayer files with their own EVC OTP or DSC. Hoola never pays tax: the challan is always paid by the taxpayer.

Payroll

For payroll Hoola keeps your employees' names, pay, attendance, UAN, ESIC numbers, PAN and bank account details. PAN and bank account numbers are stored encrypted and shown only as the last 4 digits; the AI model is never given them in full. They are used only to make payslips, the bank salary file, the PF ECR and ESIC sheet, and TDS. Hoola never pays salaries and never logs in to your bank: you upload the bank file and pay yourself. One employee's salary is never shown to another.

Financial analysis

When you give Hoola your Tally export, bank statement or outstanding list, it keeps the figures, transactions and party names to work out your profit, ratios, cash forecast and who owes what. It never moves money, never applies for loans and never sends your reports or reminders to anyone without your approval. Its numbers are analysis, not financial advice.

Identity numbers (Pehchaan Locker)

ID numbers you give (Aadhaar, PAN and others) are stored encrypted on your server and typed only into the forms you ask Hoola to fill.

Money

Hoola never moves money. Payments, stock orders and messages to others wait for your own tap.

Deleting your data

You can delete items in the app, or ask the owner of your Hoola server (or us at the email above) to delete your account data.
