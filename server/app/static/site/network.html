Money in a deal v0.106

The payment card can only ask for what is due.

= due

Locked to the deal

A payment request for any amount other than what is still owed is refused by your Hoola. The UPI link is built on your side, in your own UPI app.

📌

Payee pinned

The first UPI ID a shop uses is pinned. A new or changed one shows the old one and gives no link until you tap yourself.

R-0001

Claim is not proof

"Maine pay kiya" with a UTR is only a claim. Money counts when the shop owner says it reached her bank, and then a numbered receipt goes back.

Khata

Udhaar on both sides

A shop can open a khata for one customer with a limit and days. Both Hoolas keep the same book, written only from signed steps.

30 min

Free cancel window

Cancel freely until the shop accepts and for 30 minutes after. Later it becomes a request the shop answers.

1915

Official routes

If a dispute isn't settled, your Hoola names the National Consumer Helpline and e-Daakhil. No automatic complaints.

Beyond one server v0.34 · v0.116

Other servers, other agents, official rails.

Federation

handle@another.server

Every server publishes its key. Envelopes go over HTTPS signed twice: by the server and by the avatar. Files are fetched once, only for the avatar they were sent to.

A2A 1.0

Agents from other platforms

Outside AI agents reach only a shop's public counter: questions, the price list, an order list. Quotes and orders go only on her tap; no UPI ID is ever sent to them.

AA · ABDM · DigiLocker

Consent, checked in plain words

A bank's data request or a hospital's records request is explained with every red flag; you decide in the official app. DigiLocker documents come to you; Aadhaar is never pulled, and ID papers never go to another avatar.

ONDC

Optional listing sheet

Your Hoola stock becomes the ONDC retail sheet for the seller app you choose. Hoola never claims your shop is live there.
