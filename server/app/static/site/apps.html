Questions

Good to know.

Where do I find App Studio?

Open Hoola Console on your computer and press App Development in the sidebar — the studio opens full screen. You can also go straight to hoola.ainewtika.com/console/apps.

Android and iPhone both?

Yes. One plan, one design, two real builds — an Android APK you can install right away and an iPhone app for the App Store. You choose to build one or both.

What does the plan show, exactly?

Every screen by name, every data table, every role and the phone features the app needs — camera, UPI, notifications, location. If something is unclear, the plan asks you one clear question instead of guessing.

Can it use my website's data?

Yes — if Hoola built your website, the app can share the same products, customers and logins, and orders from both land in one place. Say "An app for my business with the same data and logins as my Hoola website".

What about the stores?

Play production and App Store review are separate, guided steps with the listing pages written for you. Both always ask first — nothing is ever submitted on its own — and the store accounts stay yours.

Who owns the app and the data?

You do. The app, the data and the store accounts are yours; Hoola built them for you and watches the health. Your customers' data stays in your business.

Your app is one line away.

Open Hoola Console, press App Development, write one line. The plan and the three looks are free, and nothing is built — or sent to a store — until you say Haan.
