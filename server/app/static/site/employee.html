GST & Accounts

Your accountant that never takes a day off: every bill in one register, GST prepared and checked before the deadlines.

Does

  • One register of every sale and purchase bill
  • This month's GST dates with days left
  • Flags the same bill entered in two places
  • Rules kept with their source and the date they were checked

Needs from you

  • Your GSTIN and GST portal username
  • For filing: the OTP the GST portal sends you

Will never

  • Move money; a challan is a number you pay at your bank
  • File a return without your OTP
  • Keep your GST portal password
  • Change a month you or your CA locked

SEO Specialist

Gets your business found on Google and in AI answers. Works on your website and Google profile.

Does

  • Full audit of your website every week, up to 500 pages
  • Watches your site every hour for downtime and dropped pages
  • Reads Search Console and Analytics
  • Checks speed on mobile

Needs from you

  • Your website address
  • Google sign-in for Search Console

Will never

  • Buy links or fake reviews
  • Publish anything without your OK
  • Make mass AI pages
  • Promise a ranking

Social Media Manager

Runs your Instagram and Facebook Page like a good agency: plans the month, writes the posts, learns what works.

Does

  • Reads your last 50 posts and their numbers
  • A strategy with themes, rhythm and hashtags
  • A 30-day calendar of ready posts
  • Indian festivals and local moments planned in

Needs from you

  • Instagram Business account or Facebook Page
  • One login on Instagram's own page

Will never

  • Buy followers or likes
  • Post anything without your OK
  • Invent prices, offers or claims
  • Touch an account you did not connect

CAD Engineer

Turns your words and sizes into a real 3D part: STEP and STL files plus a dimensioned drawing.

Does

  • Designs the part from your sizes in mm
  • Builds and checks it: one valid solid at the right size
  • STEP, STL, four views and a drawing PDF
  • Changes it on your words: 'make the holes 10 mm'

Needs from you

  • What the part is and its main sizes in mm

Will never

  • Call a design certified or safe without an engineer
  • Sign or stamp drawings
  • Use cracked CAD software
  • Design weapons or export-controlled parts
