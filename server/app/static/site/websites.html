Questions

Good to know.

Where do I find Web Studio?

Open Hoola Console on your computer and press Web Development in the sidebar — the studio opens full screen. You can also go straight to hoola.ainewtika.com/console/web.

Do I need to know code?

No. You write what you want in plain words. If you do read code, the Code view shows every file as it is written, and you can ask for changes in plain words too.

What are the three looks, really?

Three complete designs of your whole site — different layouts, type and sections, not colour swaps. You click through each one like a real site, then say "Build in this look". The other two stay around and you can borrow pages from them.

Can I use my own domain and keep my old site's place in Google?

Yes. Bring your domain or buy one here; DNS and SSL are set up for you. When your Hoola site goes live, your old addresses move to it with 301 redirects, so old links and search keep working.

What is free?

The brief, the plan, the three looks, the live build and the 12 launch checks. You pay for the domain, hosting and care — on a price card you read before you say Yes.

Who owns the website?

You do. The code and the content are yours, on your domain, and you can export any time. Hoola hosts it and watches it, and hands it over whole if you ever ask.
