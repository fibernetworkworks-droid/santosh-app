# DIGITAL / SURVEY / SUSTAINABILITY & CERTIFICATION SIDE OF THE BUILDING INDUSTRY — COMPLETE JOB LIST (India primary, international noted)

## 0. SOURCING NOTES AND HARD LIMITS HIT (read first)

**NCO-2015 codes.** The official National Classification of Occupations 2015 volumes (DGT, Ministry of Labour & Employment) are **not retrievable online from DGT or NCS** — `ncs.gov.in` hosts no NCO-2015 document (verified by fetch). What IS verifiable is the NCO-2015 code each NSQF Qualification Pack declares in its "Aligned to NCO/ISCO/ISIC Code" field. I downloaded and text-extracted the actual QP PDFs; those codes are quoted below as **verified**. Where no QP or NQR file cites a code, I write **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND** rather than guess. NCO-2015 is structurally aligned to ISCO-08 at the 4-digit level (the 8-digit form is `<ISCO 4-digit>.<Indian 4-digit>`, e.g. `2165.0200`), so I give the ISCO-08 4-digit family as the fallback and label it as such.

**ISO 19650 primary text.** iso.org returned HTTP 403; the standards are paywalled. Role definitions below are taken from the **IMI Framework** (the renamed UK BIM Framework, "brought to you by BSI and nima" — the logo file is still `nima-IMI-Framework-Logo` and the logo link text still reads "UK BIM Framework"; the site does not announce the rename): https://imiframework.org/ . That page lists the series as **BS EN ISO 19650-1:2018** (concepts and principles), **-2:2018** (delivery phase), **-3:2020** (operational phase), **-4:2022** (information exchange), **-5:2020** (security-minded BIM), plus PAS 1192-6:2018 and BS 8536.

**Verbatim role definitions from that page:**
- *Appointing party:* "Receiver of information concerning works, goods or services from a lead appointed party." (may be called client / owner / employer)
- *Lead appointed party:* coordinates information between the delivery team and the appointing party; sits in both the Project Team and a Delivery Team.
- *Appointed party:* "Provider of information concerning works, goods or services"; a lead appointed party should be identified for each delivery team.
- *Information manager / information management function:* **NOT DEFINED on that page** — NOT FOUND. (ISO 19650-2 allocates an *information management function* as a set of tasks to be performed by a named party, not as a job title. I could not verify the clause text behind the paywall, so treat "Information Manager" below as an industry job title, not a standard-defined role.)
- EIR / BEP / MIDP / TIDP / responsibility matrix / CDE: **the terms do not appear on the IMI Framework landing page** — NOT FOUND there. Guidance is behind https://app.morta.io/project/a690e3a3-aa56-4a14-bb59-887f9c743b31/process/0e0db609-09a6-423d-a2c6-5093e8314f14 and https://imiframework.org/resources/ .

### A1. BIM Manager (India: "BIM Manager", "BIM Lead", "Digital Delivery Manager")
- **NCO-2015:** 2142.9900 (verified, CON/Q2108, NSQF 6.5).
- **Does:** Owns the organisation's or project's BIM strategy; authors/owns the BIM Execution Plan; sets modelling standards, naming conventions, LOD/LOI schedules; picks and administers the CDE; runs the coordination cycle; resources and trains the modelling team; signs off model issues to the client.
- **Deliverables:** BEP (pre- and post-appointment), BIM standards/manual, LOD-LOI matrix, responsibility matrix, MIDP, model-issue/transmittal records, federated model releases, training plan.
- **Employs:** Architecture/engineering consultancies, EPC and large contractors (L&T, Tata Projects, Shapoorji), developer in-house digital teams, metro/airport/rail PMCs, offshore BIM service companies (Pune, Bengaluru, Ahmedabad, Coimbatore) delivering to UK/US/Gulf clients.
- **THE GATE:** **None legally.** No Indian statute, Council of Architecture rule or ECI/IEI licence gates the title. ISO 19650 certification (BSI "ISO 19650 Practitioner", buildingSMART Professional Certification) is **contractual, never statutory**. The only place a gate bites is procurement: UK public work applies the Construction Playbook, which cites the IMI/UK BIM Framework (https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6312222de90e075880923330/14.116_CO_Construction_Playbook_Web.pdf), and Gulf/Singapore/Scandinavian clients mandate ISO 19650 compliance in the contract. **Who signs what:** the BEP is signed by the **lead appointed party**, not by an accredited individual.

### A2. BIM Coordinator (India: "BIM Coordinator", "Discipline Coordinator")
- **NCO-2015:** 2142.9900 (verified, CON/Q2103 "BIM Coordinator – Design", NSQF 6).
- **Does:** Runs the weekly/fortnightly coordination cycle for one or more disciplines; federates discipline models; runs clash tests; chairs clash resolution meetings; assigns and tracks issues to resolution; checks models against the BEP before upload to the CDE.
- **Deliverables:** Federated model, clash detection reports (Navisworks clash XML/HTML, Solibri .smc/BCF), BCF issue files, coordination meeting minutes, model-health/QA check reports, sign-off against the TIDP.
- **Employs:** Same as A1, plus MEP subcontractors (Voltas, Blue Star, Johnson Controls), façade and precast subcontractors.
- **THE GATE:** **None.** Autodesk/Nemetschek certifications are vendor marketing. No legal signature.

### A3. BIM Modeller / BIM Technician (India: "BIM Modeller", older title "CAD Draughtsman")
- **NCO-2015:** 2142.9900 (verified, CON/Q2110 BIM Modeler NSQF 4; CON/Q2109 BIM Technician – Object Creation NSQF 5). Note the QP's own job description: "A BIM Modeler is responsible for creating and managing digital BIM models of architectural, structural, Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP), or landscape elements…"
- **Does:** Builds geometry and attaches data to it; builds and maintains the parametric family/object library; produces sheets and schedules from the model; applies the naming and classification standard.
- **Deliverables:** Discipline models (.rvt/.pln/.ifc), object/family libraries, drawing sheet sets, schedules/quantity tables, model exports to IFC.
- **Employs:** Consultancies, offshore BIM production houses (this is India's single largest digital-construction employment pool), contractors' VDC cells.
- **THE GATE:** **None.** Diploma in civil/architectural draughtsmanship is customary; NSQF 4/5 certification is voluntary.

### A4. Information Manager (ISO 19650) (India: usually folded into "BIM Manager" or "Document Controller")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.** Nearest ISCO family 2142 / 2166. No Indian QP exists for it.
- **Does:** Performs the ISO 19650-2 *information management function* on behalf of the appointing party or lead appointed party — establishes the information standard and production methods, configures and audits the CDE workflow states (WIP → Shared → Published → Archived), issues and logs information containers, checks that each exchange answers the EIR, maintains the information model and the responsibility matrix.
- **Deliverables:** Information standard, information production methods & procedures, CDE workflow configuration, container naming/status/revision register, information-exchange audit records, MIDP/TIDP assembly, ISO 19650-5 security-minded elements where -5 applies.
- **Employs:** Appointing parties (metro corporations, airport operators, data-centre and pharma owners), PMC firms, lead designers. Internationally: UK/EU/Gulf public clients, where the function is contractually named.
- **THE GATE:** **No statutory gate anywhere.** ISO 19650 is a standard, not a licence; conformity is asserted contractually or via third-party certification (BSI; buildingSMART Professional Certification). **IMPORTANT:** the role is a *function*, and the IMI Framework landing page does **not** define "information manager" — NOT FOUND. ISO 19650-2 clause text unverified (paywall, HTTP 403 from iso.org).

### A5. CDE / Common Data Environment Manager (India: "CDE Administrator", "Document Controller – Digital")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND** (document-control work maps to ISCO 4415/3341; the digital variant has no code).
- **Does:** Administers the CDE platform (ACC/BIM 360, Bentley ProjectWise, Trimble Connect, Asite, Aconex/Oracle, Viewpoint); sets permissions and folder/container structures; enforces the four approval states; manages revision/status codes; runs transmittals and audit trails; handles ISO 19650-5 access segregation.
- **Deliverables:** CDE structure and permission matrix, transmittal records, revision/status registers, audit logs, archive/handover snapshots at project close.
- **Employs:** PMCs, main contractors, owners' digital teams, CDE vendor implementation partners.
- **THE GATE:** **None statutory.** Vendor administrator certification is contractual. Where the project handles defence/critical-infrastructure data, Indian data-localisation and the DST Geospatial Guidelines' storage rule bite (see F-family note: finer-than-threshold geospatial data "must be stored and processed only in India").

### A6. Clash Detection / Coordination Engineer
- **NCO-2015:** 2142.9900 (within CON/Q2103 scope).
- **Does:** Hard/soft/clearance clash rule-set authoring; running batch clash tests; triaging false positives; measuring constructability and maintenance access; sequencing MEP in congested plant rooms and shafts.
- **Deliverables:** Clash matrices (which discipline vs which, tolerance), clash reports with viewpoints, BCF issue packages, resolved/open trackers, zero-clash sign-off certificates.
- **Employs:** MEP consultancies, EPC contractors, VDC cells.
- **THE GATE:** **None.**

### A7. 4D Planner / 5D Cost-BIM Specialist
- **NCO-2015:** 2142.9900 (verified: CON/Q2102 "4D BIM Planner – Planning and Monitoring" NSQF 6; CON/Q2107 "5D BIM Planner – Cost Planning and Monitoring" NSQF 6).
- **Does:** 4D — links the model to the P6/MSP schedule, builds construction sequence simulations, tests logistics, crane and temporary-works sequencing, does planned-vs-actual progress visualisation. 5D — links model quantities to rate libraries and BOQ items, runs cost loading, earned value, change-order impact.
- **Deliverables:** 4D simulation video/model, schedule-linked model (Synchro/Navisworks TimeLiner), look-ahead visuals, progress S-curves against model, 5D cost-loaded model, model-derived BOQ and bill variance reports, cash-flow curves.
- **Employs:** EPC and infrastructure contractors, PMCs, claims consultancies (4D is heavily used in delay/disruption forensic work).
- **THE GATE:** **None.** Note the adjacency: a *Quantity Surveyor* (ISCO 2149) in Commonwealth practice may need RICS/IIQS membership for contractual certification, but the 5D BIM specialist as such is ungated.

### A8. Digital Twin Manager (India: "Digital Twin Lead", "Asset Information Manager")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.**
- **Does:** Defines Asset Information Requirements; governs the transition from PIM (project information model) to AIM (asset information model) per ISO 19650-3:2020; integrates BMS/IoT/SCADA telemetry with the as-built model; maintains the asset register and its linkage to CMMS/EAM (Maximo, SAP PM); runs performance dashboards against the model.
- **Deliverables:** AIR, AIM, asset register (COBie or equivalent), O&M data sets, twin integration architecture, live performance dashboards, handover data drop (ISO 19650-3 operational-phase exchanges).
- **Employs:** Airport and metro operators, REITs and large landlords (Embassy, DLF, Brookfield), data-centre and pharma owners, facilities-management majors, OEM/platform vendors (Siemens, Honeywell, Johnson Controls, Bentley iTwin).
- **THE GATE:** **None.** CON/Q2104 "7D BIM Creator – BIM & IoT Integration" (NCO-2015/2142.9900) is the nearest Indian qualification and it is **deactivated**.

### A9. Computational / Parametric Designer (India: "Computational Designer", "Dynamo/Grasshopper Specialist")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND** (nearest ISCO 2142/2161/2512 software developer).
- **Does:** Writes Dynamo/Grasshopper graphs and Python/C# add-ins; automates repetitive modelling and documentation; generative/optioneering studies; façade panelisation and rationalisation; links analysis engines (Ladybug/Honeybee, Karamba) into the design loop; builds in-house tooling against the Revit/ArchiCAD/Rhino APIs.
- **Deliverables:** Scripts and graphs, custom plug-ins and add-ins, optioneering matrices, rationalised geometry sets, automated drawing/schedule pipelines, panel-and-fabrication data.
- **Employs:** Design-driven practices, façade consultants and fabricators, large consultancies' computational-design groups, Indian GCCs of Arup/Buro Happold/WSP/Jacobs.
- **THE GATE:** **None.**

### A10. Authoring-Tool Specialist — Revit / ArchiCAD / Tekla / Civil 3D / MicroStation
- **NCO-2015:** 2142.9900 where it sits in the BIM QP family.
- **Does:** Deep platform configuration — templates, shared parameters, worksets, view filters, annotation standards, families, Tekla custom components, Civil 3D corridor/subassembly authoring; upgrades and data migration; interoperability troubleshooting.
- **Deliverables:** Project templates, shared parameter files, title-block/annotation libraries, platform configuration documents, migration and upgrade reports, exchange configuration (IFC mapping tables).
- **Employs:** Consultancies, reseller/implementation partners, steel detailing houses (Tekla), infrastructure design firms (Civil 3D/MicroStation).
- **THE GATE:** **None.** Vendor certification only.

### A11. BIM Standards Author — EIR / BEP writer (India: "BIM Consultant", "Digital Strategy Consultant")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.**
- **Does:** Writes the client-side Exchange Information Requirements, Organisational Information Requirements, Asset Information Requirements, Project Information Requirements; writes the information standard; reviews tenderers' pre-appointment BEPs; writes the responsibility matrix; defines LOD/LOI by stage and discipline; writes IDS validation rule sets; writes the measured compliance/audit protocol.
- **Deliverables:** EIR, OIR/AIR/PIR, information standard, information production methods & procedures, responsibility matrix, MIDP template, IDS files, model audit protocol, maturity assessment.
- **Employs:** Appointing parties, government digital-construction cells, PMCs, independent BIM consultancies.
- **THE GATE:** **None statutory.** This is the role most often required by contract to hold third-party ISO 19650 certification. In India there is **no national BIM mandate**; the Niti Aayog / CPWD and metro-sector requirements are procurement conditions, not law — **NOT FOUND: any Indian statutory BIM mandate.**

# FAMILY B — SURVEY, MEASUREMENT AND REALITY CAPTURE

**THE CONTROLLING INDIAN LAW FOR THIS WHOLE FAMILY** is the **DST Guidelines for acquiring and producing Geospatial Data and Geospatial Data Services including Maps, 15 February 2021**, which replaced the Survey of India's National Map Policy. Verbatim from the Khaitan & Co analysis of the Guidelines (https://khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2021-02/Ergo_19022021.pdf), which I text-extracted:

> "there shall be no requirement for prior approval, security clearance, license or any other restrictions on the collection, generation, preparation, dissemination, storage, publication, updating and/or digitization of geospatial data and maps within India (subject to the 'negative lists'). The Guidelines also envisage a self-certification requirement."

> "Indian Entities are permitted to acquire, collect, generate, prepare, disseminate, store, share, publish, distribute, update, digitize and/or create geospatial data, including maps, of any spatial accuracy within the territory of India by using any geospatial technology, subject to the negative lists."

> "**Only Indian Entities are permitted to conduct terrestrial mobile mapping survey, street view survey and surveying in Indian territorial waters.**"

> "Only Indian Entities are permitted to create and/or own maps / geospatial data of spatial accuracy / value finer than the threshold value. The same must be stored and processed only in India."

> Foreign companies and foreign-owned/controlled Indian companies "can license the digital maps / geospatial data of spatial accuracy / value finer than the threshold value from Indian Entities only for the purpose of serving their customers in India… only through Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) which do not allow maps / geospatial data to pass through the foreign company or its servers."

"Indian Entity" is defined by reference to **Explanation to Rule 23 of the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-Debt Instrument) Rules, 2019**. The Guidelines "specify certain threshold values for spatial accuracy, gravity anomaly, and vertical accuracy" — **the numeric threshold values are NOT FOUND**: dst.gov.in's PDF returned 404, the Trilegal note omits them, and egazette.nic.in is blocked from this session. Do not quote a figure.

Follow-on policy: **National Geospatial Policy 2022** (DST is the nodal department; replaced the National Map Policy 2005; targets nationwide high-resolution topo survey and a high-accuracy DEM by 2030) — https://www.indiaenvironmentportal.org.in/reports-and-documents/national-geospatial-policy-2022 . Survey of India remains the national mapping agency and its topographic data is treated as a common good with open access where security permits.

### B1. Land Surveyor (India: "Surveyor"; revenue-side title "Amin" / "Bhoomi Abhilekh surveyor")
- **NCO-2015: 2165.0200 — VERIFIED.** CSDCI QP **CON/Q0902 "Surveyor" v4.0, NSQF Level 5.5**, aligned to NCO-2015/2165.0200; entry requirement is a UG qualification in a relevant field, e.g. completion of 3rd year of a Civil Engineering degree. https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/nsdcproddocuments/qpPdf/CON_Q0902_v4.0.pdf ; NQR listing QG-5.5-CO-03966-2025-V2-CSDCI, https://www.nqr.gov.in/qualification/file/QF_Surveyor_v%204.0_L%205.5_English.pdf . **Assistant Surveyor** is a separate qualification aligned to **NCO-2015/2165.9900** (NQR QG-02-CO-03965-2025-V2-CSDCI). ISCO-08 family 2165 "Cartographers and surveyors" — examples: aerial surveyor, cadastral surveyor, cartographer, hydrographic surveyor, land surveyor, mine surveyor, photogrammetrist; **excludes** quantity surveyor (2149), building surveyor (3112), marine surveyor (3115) — https://www.openriskmanual.org/wiki/ISCO_Unit_Group_2165_Cartographers_And_Surveyors
- **Does:** Boundary demarcation, cadastral/plot measurement, subdivision, area computation, title-support survey, encroachment identification, deed-plan preparation; works from revenue records (7/12, RoR, village map, tippan).
- **Deliverables:** Survey plan/plot plan, area statement, boundary demarcation report, subdivision sketch, tippan/measurement sheet, coordinate schedule, encroachment plan, affidavit-grade survey certificate.
- **Employs:** State revenue/land-records departments, licensed private surveyors in independent practice, developers, title-insurance and due-diligence practices, law firms, PMCs, banks (mortgage verification).
- **THE GATE — THIS IS THE REAL ONE IN THIS FAMILY. ANSWER: THERE IS NO ALL-INDIA SURVEYOR LICENCE. LICENSING IS STATE REVENUE-DEPARTMENT LAW, AND WHERE A STATE HAS IT, AN UNLICENSED SURVEY IS NOT USABLE FOR A REVENUE-RECORD PURPOSE (mutation, subdivision, RoR update).** **NOT FOUND: any central statute licensing land surveyors in India** (there is no Indian analogue of the UK RICS-chartered-plus-statutory or the US state Professional Land Surveyor licensure; the Institution of Surveying & Mapping and IEI confer *membership*, not a licence).
  - **Maharashtra, concrete and current:** the **Maharashtra Land Revenue Code, 1966 has been amended to allow private players** to carry out land measurement; the state approved ~200 **licensed private surveyors** at district level, covering "subdivision of land, fixing boundaries, conversion of agricultural land for non-agricultural use and 'gunthewari' plots"; candidates undergo a two-week training programme run by the **Department of Land Records** followed by an **examination**. https://theprint.in/india/maharashtra-govt-to-deploy-200-private-surveyors-to-speed-up-land-measurement-work/2875223 ; https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/maharashtra-to-appoint-200-licensed-private-surveyors-to-clear-pending-land-measurement-cases . **The final licensing rules / GR text is NOT FOUND**, so the precise statement "a private licensed surveyor's measurement is binding for mutation" is **NOT VERIFIED** — only that the Code was amended to permit them and that licensing + training + exam is the route.
  - **Who may sign what:** for a record-of-rights consequence, the signature that counts is the **District Inspector of Land Records / Taluka Inspector of Land Records (or the state-licensed surveyor acting under that Code)** — a private unlicensed survey is evidence, not record. For a *construction* setting-out or quantity purpose, no licence is required at all.
  - **International contrast:** US — Professional Land Surveyor (PLS/PS) licensure is a state-by-state statutory requirement and a plat must bear the licensed surveyor's seal. UK — no statutory licence; RICS chartered status (MRICS) is market, not law. Australia/NZ/Canada — statutory registration of cadastral surveyors, mandatory for plans lodged at the land registry.

### B2. Licensed / Registered Surveyor (India: "Licensed Surveyor", "Panel Surveyor", "Government Approved Surveyor")
- **NCO-2015:** 2165.0200 (same code; the licence is a legal status, not a separate occupation).
- **Does:** B1's work, but with the statutory standing to produce survey documents that a revenue authority, municipal authority or court will accept; often also empanelled for municipal plot-area certification, land-acquisition measurement and court-commissioner measurement.
- **Deliverables:** Same as B1 plus the signed-and-sealed/licensed survey certificate; court-commissioner measurement reports; land-acquisition award measurement sheets.
- **Employs:** Independent practice (the licence is personal), state revenue departments as empanelled surveyors, courts as commissioners, land-acquisition authorities, metro/highway acquisition cells.
- **THE GATE:** **YES — the licence IS the gate, and it is per-state.** See B1. Additionally the **DST Geospatial Guidelines 2021** overlay: no survey licence or security clearance is needed from the central government for *geospatial data production* by an Indian Entity, but **only an Indian Entity may survey in Indian territorial waters or do terrestrial mobile mapping / street view** (quoted above) — so a foreign-controlled survey firm is barred from those three activities regardless of any state licence.

### B3. Topographic Surveyor (India: "Topo Surveyor", "Detail Survey Engineer")
- **NCO-2015:** 2165.0200 / 2165.9900.
- **Does:** Total-station and GNSS/RTK detail survey; establishes primary and secondary control; levelling and benchmark transfer; produces contours, spot levels, drainage patterns, existing-feature plans, cross/longitudinal sections; utility and tree surveys; river and reservoir cross-sections.
- **Deliverables:** Topographic survey drawing (DWG/DGN), contour and TIN/DTM surface, coordinate and level schedule with control register, L-sections and cross-sections, traverse closure and adjustment computation, survey report with instrument calibration certificates and datum statement (WGS84/UTM or local grid, GTS benchmark reference).
- **Employs:** Survey firms, infrastructure consultancies (highways, metro, water), contractors, mining and industrial owners, Survey of India.
- **THE GATE:** **None for construction-purpose topo survey.** Instrument calibration traceability is the practical gate. Geospatial Guidelines apply as above. If the output is tied into the national geodetic framework for an official map, Survey of India's datum/standards govern.

### B4. Setting-Out Engineer (India: "Survey Engineer – Site", "Layout Engineer")
- **NCO-2015:** NOT FOUND for the specific role; 2165.0200 / 3112 (civil engineering technician) family.
- **Does:** Transfers design geometry to the ground — grid lines, column centres, pile positions, pile cut-off levels, formwork and kerb lines, alignment and chainage for linear works, verticality and plumb checks on cores and high-rises, as-constructed position checks, machine-control/automated-machine-guidance data preparation.
- **Deliverables:** Setting-out data sheets and coordinate files (points/alignments), setting-out records countersigned by the engineer, verticality and level check reports, non-conformance records for out-of-tolerance work, machine-control models (LandXML/TIN), as-set-out survey.
- **Employs:** Main contractors and subcontractors on every site; infrastructure contractors (where the role is largest — alignment work); piling specialists; precast erectors.
- **THE GATE:** **None.** The signature that matters is contractual: the Engineer/consultant countersigns the setting-out record before concrete. (International: on UK/EU works the as-set-out record is a contract deliverable under the specification, not licensed.)

### B5. Quantity Take-Off from Model (India: "Quantity Surveyor – BIM", "5D QS", "Billing Engineer")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND** for the BIM variant; ISCO-08 places **quantity surveyor at 2149** (expressly excluded from 2165).
- **Does:** Extracts quantities from the model against a measurement standard (IS 1200 series for Indian works, CESMM/NRM/SMM7 internationally, POMI in the Gulf); reconciles model quantities to the BOQ; model-based variation and interim-payment measurement; auditing model completeness for measurement (what is NOT modelled and must be measured manually).
- **Deliverables:** Model-derived BOQ, quantity reconciliation statement (model vs 2D vs site), measurement basis note (which standard, what is excluded), interim payment quantities, variation quantification, cost-loaded model.
- **Employs:** QS consultancies, contractors' billing/commercial departments, developers, claims and arbitration experts.
- **THE GATE:** **No statutory gate in India.** Where a contract requires an *independent certifier* of measurement, the signature usually must be a **chartered engineer** or an **RICS/IIQS member** by contract. Under RERA the related certificate IS gated — see B11.

### B6. Laser Scanning / Point Cloud Technician (India: "3D Laser Scanning Engineer", "Reality Capture Technician")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.** Nearest 2165.9900.
- **Does:** Plans scan locations and target/registration strategy; operates terrestrial laser scanners (Leica RTC360/BLK, Faro, Trimble X7) and SLAM/mobile units (NavVis, GeoSLAM); registers scans and reports registration error; georeferences to project control; cleans, decimates and segments point clouds; delivers clouds and/or Scan-to-BIM models; does deviation analysis against design.
- **Deliverables:** Registered point cloud in **E57** (open, ASTM E2807) / **LAS/LAZ** (open, ASGPRS/ISO) and vendor formats (RCP/RCS, ZFS, FLS, E57 being the interchange one), registration report with per-scan and global error, panoramic imagery, Scan-to-BIM model with stated accuracy, deviation/heat-map reports (as-built vs model), ortho-images and 2D sections from cloud.
- **Employs:** Reality-capture service firms, refinery/plant and brownfield engineering consultancies, heritage documentation units (ASI contractors, heritage conservation practices), retrofit and MEP coordination teams, metro and rail asset owners, insurance/forensic surveyors.
- **THE GATE:** **None.** But the **DST Geospatial Guidelines** bite hard on **mobile/SLAM scanning on streets**: "Only Indian Entities are permitted to conduct terrestrial mobile mapping survey, street view survey" — a foreign-owned/controlled entity may not do street-level mobile mapping in India. Inside a private building envelope this is not a mapping survey; outdoors along a public right of way it is.

### B7. Drone / UAV Survey Pilot (India: "Drone Pilot", "UAV Survey Pilot", "Remote Pilot")

Adjacent verified codes: **UAS Developer → NCO/ISCO 2149.0100** (https://nqr.gov.in/qualification/file/UAS%20Developer%20Qualification%20File%20Final.pdf); **Junior Engineer Drone (R&D) → NCO-2015/8212.0400** (https://www.nqr.gov.in/qualification/file/QFile_Junior%20Engineer%20Drone%20%28R%20&%20D%29.pdf); **Drone Film Making → NCO-2015/7321.1200**. **No NQR qualification titled "Drone Pilot" was found.**

- **Employs:** Drone service companies, mining and quarry owners (monthly volumetrics), solar and transmission EPCs, highway and canal projects, municipal property-tax and slum-mapping programmes, SVAMITVA-type land-records programmes, real-estate marketing, insurance.
- **THE GATE — YES, AND IT IS NAMED. THE DRONE RULES, 2021 (MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION, G.S.R. 589(E), 25 AUGUST 2021), AS AMENDED BY THE DRONE (AMENDMENT) RULES, 2022 (G.S.R. 108(E), 11 FEBRUARY 2022) AND 2023 (G.S.R. 715(E), 27 SEPTEMBER 2023).** Rule-by-rule (rule numbers and headings verified against the consolidated text at https://www.legitquest.com/act/drone-rules-2021/8A40 ; amendment text at https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-10/Drone%20%28Amendment%29%20Rules%2C%202022%20dated%2011%20Feb%202022_0.pdf ):
  - **Rule 5 — Classification by all-up weight including payload:** Nano ≤ 250 g; **Micro > 250 g and ≤ 2 kg**; (Small, Medium, Large above).
  - **Rule 6 / Rule 8 / Rule 9 — Type certificate:** no person may operate a UAS in India unless it "conforms to a type certificate or is exempted from the requirement of a type certificate"; the DGCA issues the type certificate "on the recommendation of the Quality Council of India or an authorised testing entity." **Rule 13** gives exemptions.
  - **Rule 14 / Rule 15 — Registration and UIN:** "No person shall operate an unmanned aircraft system without first registering it on the digital sky platform"; on Form D-2 "the digital sky platform shall verify the details and issue a unique identification number to the applicant."
  - **Rule 34 — Remote pilot certificate.** As originally made, the trainee "shall be issued a remote pilot certificate through digital sky platform" and the DGCA issued the licence within fifteen days. **The 2022 Amendment substituted "certificate" for "licence" throughout, OMITTED sub-rule (4) of rule 34, and in rule 35(e) substituted "authorized remote pilot training organization" for "Director General"** — i.e. **since 11 Feb 2022 the Remote Pilot Certificate is issued by a DGCA-authorised Remote Pilot Training Organisation (RPTO) and enlisted on Digital Sky; the DGCA no longer issues a separate licence.**
  - **Rule 36 — Exemption:** "No remote pilot licence [now: certificate] shall be required for" operating a **nano** UAS, or "a **micro** unmanned aircraft system **for non-commercial purposes**." **A COMMERCIAL SURVEY IS NOT NON-COMMERCIAL, SO A PAID SURVEY FLIGHT NEEDS AN RPC AT ANY WEIGHT ABOVE NANO.** A sub-250 g nano needs no RPC even commercially — but still needs registration/UIN under rule 14/15.
  - **Rules 19–24 — Airspace:** Rule 19, the Central Government may publish an airspace map "segregating the entire airspace of India into red zone, yellow zone and green zone" on Digital Sky. Green zone = up to "a vertical distance of 400 feet or 120 metre" above land/territorial waters. Yellow zone = "within which unmanned aircraft system operations are restricted and shall require permission" from the concerned ATC authority. Red zone = "operations shall be permitted only by the Central Government." **Rule 21** mandatory pre-flight verification of zonal restrictions; **Rule 22** prior permission for red and yellow, **none required in green**; Rule 23 dynamic zoning; Rule 24 temporary red zone.
  - **Rule 44 — Insurance** (third-party).
  - **WHO MAY SIGN WHAT:** the RPC is held by the **individual remote pilot** and issued by an **authorised RPTO** (rule 34/35 as amended); the **UIN attaches to the aircraft**; **zone permission is granted via Digital Sky by the Central Government (red) or the concerned ATC authority (yellow)**. A survey deliverable is not "certified" by the pilot's RPC — the RPC legalises the *flight*; the *survey* accuracy statement is signed by the surveyor/photogrammetrist.
  - **Layered on top:** the DST Geospatial Guidelines (above) mean a **foreign-owned/controlled Indian company cannot own finer-than-threshold output** and must keep such data stored and processed only in India.
  - **International contrast:** EU — EASA Regulation (EU) 2019/947 Open/Specific categories, remote pilot online training + A1/A3 or A2 certificate of competency, SORA for Specific. US — FAA 14 CFR Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate, plus LAANC airspace authorisation. UK — CAA A2 CofC / GVC with an Operational Authorisation.

A Maharashtra REAT ruling held the carpet area in the registered Agreement for Sale prevails over allotment letters and drafts — https://livelaw.in/pdf_upload/maha-reat-543089.pdf .
  - **NOT FOUND:** any reference in RERA or state RERA rules to **IS 3861** (the Indian Standard on method of measurement of plinth, carpet and rentable areas) as the mandated measurement method. Do not claim one.

### C5. ECBC Compliance Consultant / Third-Party Assessor (India: "ECBC TPA", "Certified Energy Auditor (Building)", "ECBC Expert")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.**
- **Does:** Checks the design against the Energy Conservation Building Code (ECBC 2017, BEE/Ministry of Power) and the state's adopted/notified version; envelope compliance (U-values, SHGC, WWR, roof and wall assemblies), lighting power density and controls, HVAC equipment efficiency and controls, service water heating, electrical power distribution; chooses the compliance path (prescriptive / whole-building performance); for residential, **Eco-Niwas Samhita** (Part I 2018 — building envelope, metric **RETV**, Residential Envelope Transmittance Value; Part II 2021 — code compliance plus electro-mechanical and renewable-energy systems); signs the compliance document for plan approval and the compliance certificate at occupancy.
- **Deliverables:** ECBC/ENS compliance report and forms, envelope calculation workbook (U/SHGC/RETV/WWR), LPD schedule, HVAC equipment efficiency schedule, whole-building simulation report where the performance path is used, signed compliance certificate at plan-approval stage and at occupancy, as-built compliance verification.

- **THE GATE — YES, AND IT IS A STATE-NOTIFIED EMPANELMENT OR A BEE CERTIFICATION. NAME BOTH:**
  - **Legal basis for the auditor cadre:** the **Energy Conservation Act, 2001** confers rule-making power over "the qualifications, criteria and conditions subject to which a person may be accredited as an energy auditor and the procedure for such accreditation" (inserted by the 2010 amendment); the **Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022** inserts a definition of "energy auditor" as "any individual possessing the qualifications prescribed under clause (m) of section 14" — https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_parliament/2022/The%20Energy%20Conservation%20(Amendment)%20Act,%202022.pdf .   - **Haryana (HECBC 2022):** compliance documents may come from a **Certified Energy Auditor (Building)** or a **BEE-empanelled ECBC Expert/Design Professional or HAREDA-empanelled ECBC design professional** — the latter accepted **"in place of Certified Energy Auditor (Building) until that cadre is established."** That is the key practical fact: **BEE has not yet created a separate Certified Energy Auditor (Building) cadre**, so states accept empanelled ECBC experts in the interim.

There is no single national answer.**

- **Deliverables:** Water balance diagram and statement, RWH design drawings and calculations, STP/ETP process flow diagram, mass balance and equipment sizing, discharge quality compliance statement against CPCB/SPCB norms, O&M manual, IGBC/GRIHA/LEED water-credit submissions, CGWA NOC application support, plumbing drawings and schedules.
- **THE GATE — THERE ARE REAL ONES, BUT THEY SIT ON CONSENTS, NOT ON THE CONSULTANT:**
  - **Rainwater harvesting is legally mandatory in most Indian states/cities** via the building bye-laws (e.g. Delhi, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad) for plots above a stated size, and plan approval is withheld without it. **The specific bye-law and plot threshold is city-by-city — NOT FOUND: a single national rule.**
  - **Groundwater abstraction needs a NOC from the Central Ground Water Authority** (or the state ground water authority) in notified areas.
  - **STP/ETP discharge needs Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate from the State Pollution Control Board** under the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974; monitoring reports for consent compliance must come from a **recognised/NABL-accredited laboratory** (see D7). **The signature that counts is the occupier's, supported by the recognised lab's test report** — not the design consultant's.
  - **Environmental Clearance** under EIA Notification 2006 (building and construction projects above the notified built-up-area threshold) requires the water/wastewater scheme in the EMP, signed off by the proponent and appraised by SEIAA/EAC. **Accredited EIA consultant organisations under the NABET/QCI scheme are required for EIA report preparation** — that IS a gate on the EIA consultant, distinct from this role.
  - **No credential gates the water consultant's design itself.**

### C10. Waste Management Consultant (India: "C&D Waste Consultant", "Solid Waste Consultant")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.**
- **Does:** Construction & demolition waste management plan per the **Construction and Demolition Waste Management Rules, 2016** (MoEFCC); pre-demolition audit and material-recovery estimate; segregation and storage plan; recycled-content specification; operational-phase municipal-solid-waste segregation, organic waste converter sizing, e-waste and hazardous-waste routing; diversion-rate tracking.
- **Deliverables:** C&D waste management plan, waste inventory and diversion log with weighbridge/disposal receipts, pre-demolition audit report, recycled-content specification and tracking, OWC/MSW room design, diversion-rate report, LEED MRc5 Construction and Demolition Waste Management / IGBC and GRIHA waste-credit submissions.
- **Employs:** Developers, contractors, municipal corporations, demolition contractors, recyclers, sustainability consultancies.
- **THE GATE:** **No credential gate on the consultant.** The statutory duties fall on the **generator** under the C&D Waste Rules 2016 — a generator above the notified quantity must submit a waste management plan and obtain approval from the local authority, and must pay the relevant charges; **hazardous waste handling requires an authorisation from the SPCB** under the Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 2016. **Who signs:** the **generator/occupier**, approved by the **local authority / SPCB**.

# FAMILY D — TESTING, COMMISSIONING AND HANDOVER

### D1. Commissioning Authority / Commissioning Manager (CxA) (India: "Commissioning Manager", "Cx Consultant")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.** CSDCI **CON/Q2106 "Project Soft-Landing Executive"** (NSQF 4.5, **NCO-2015/2142.9900**, verified, deactivated) is the nearest Indian qualification and covers the soft-landings end of this work.
- **Does:** Runs the **Commissioning Process** per **ASHRAE Guideline 0-2019, The Commissioning Process**, which "describes a Commissioning Process capable of verifying that a facility and its systems meet the Owner's Project Requirements"; facilitates and documents the **Owner's Project Requirements** ("generally a collaborative effort between the Owner and Commissioning Authority (CxA)"); reviews the Basis of Design and the design against the OPR; writes the commissioning plan and the pre-functional checklists and functional performance test scripts; witnesses and signs tests; manages the issues log; verifies training and O&M handover; plans seasonal/deferred testing. **ASHRAE Guideline 1.1 is the HVAC&R-specific companion — note Guideline 1.1-2025 is published and SUPERSEDES Guideline 1.1-2007** (https://www.ashrae.org/technical-resources/standards-and-guidelines/titles-purposes-and-scopes ; https://www.ashrae.org/technical-resources/bookstore/commissioning ).
- **Deliverables:** OPR document, design review comments, **commissioning plan**, pre-functional checklists, functional performance test procedures and signed results, integrated systems test results, commissioning issues log, training records, systems manual, **final commissioning report**, seasonal testing report, **commissioning certificate**, O&M handover acceptance.
- **Employs:** Independent Cx firms, owner's representatives/PMCs on data centres, hospitals, pharma, airports, labs and large offices; MEP consultancies' Cx arms; large contractors (though owner-side independence is often contractually required).
- **THE GATE:** **No statutory licence in India.** But **LEED imposes a hard, specific one** and it is the gate most often quoted: under LEED v4 **EA Prerequisite Fundamental Commissioning and Verification**, the CxA **must be engaged by the end of the design development phase and have documented commissioning process experience on at least two building projects with a similar scope of work** — the experience "does not have to be on LEED projects" ("it is the commissioning experience that matters, not if it was for LEED or not"), and non-LEED experience must be documented "through issues, logs and/or Cx checklists for all energy systems." Reviewers have enforced the **"similar"** test strictly, rejecting a submission where the CxA's prior projects were not of the certified building's type — https://leeduser.buildinggreen.com/credit/NC-v4/EAp1 ; https://leeduser.buildinggreen.com/node/44514 . Project specifications also commonly impose independence: one owner's specification states "the owner does not allow the Commissioning Authority and Commissioning Agent to be the same organization or person" — **that is a project rule, not an ASHRAE requirement** — https://www.dasny.org/sites/default/files/rfp-documents/2023-06/Section%20019113%20-%20General%20Commissioning%20Requirements_0.pdf . Market credentials: **AABC CxA, BCxA CCP (ACP), ASHRAE CPMP, NEBB BSC** — none legally required. **Who signs what:** the **CxA signs the commissioning report and certificate**; the **owner accepts the OPR**; the **contractor signs pre-functional checklists**; **LEED requires the CxA's declaration of eligibility in the submission**.

### D2. Testing & Commissioning (T&C) Engineer (India: "T&C Engineer", "Pre-commissioning Engineer")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND**; ISCO 3113/3115 (electrical/mechanical engineering technicians) family.
- **Does:** The hands-on contractor-side testing — flushing, hydrostatic and pneumatic pressure tests, air and water balancing, chiller and AHU performance tests, pump and fan curve verification, DG load bank tests, transformer and switchgear tests (IR, HV, relay coordination, protection settings), cable HiPot, earthing resistance, lift and escalator tests, fire-alarm and sprinkler/hydrant tests, fire-pump churning and flow tests, BMS point-to-point and sequence verification, cause-and-effect matrix testing, integrated fire and life-safety test, lightning protection, UPS and battery capacity tests.
- **Deliverables:** Test and inspection plan, pre-commissioning checklists, test reports per system with instrument calibration certificates, air/water balancing report (TAB), cause-and-effect test record, integrated systems test record, snag/punch lists, statutory test certificates (lift, pressure vessel, electrical), commissioning data sheets, O&M manuals, spares and tools handover list, warranty certificates.
- **Employs:** MEP contractors and subcontractors, OEMs (Voltas, Blue Star, Daikin, Schneider, ABB, Siemens, Otis, Kone, Johnson Controls, Honeywell), T&C specialist firms, data-centre and pharma specialists.
- **THE GATE — YES FOR SPECIFIC STATUTORY SYSTEMS, AND THE SIGNATORIES ARE NAMED BY OTHER LAW, NOT BY A Cx STANDARD:**
  - **Electrical installations:** energisation above the prescribed voltage requires approval/inspection by the **state Electrical Inspectorate** under the **Central Electricity Authority (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations, 2010** / Electricity Act 2003; drawings and test results must be submitted by a **licensed electrical contractor and a supervisor/wireman holding a state-issued licence/competency certificate**. **The signature is the state Electrical Inspector's.**
  - **Lifts and escalators:** licensing and inspection under the **state Lifts and Escalators Act** (e.g. Maharashtra Lifts, Escalators and Moving Walks Act, 2017) — a **lift licence from the state Lift Inspectorate** is required before use.
  - **Pressure vessels and boilers:** inspection and certification under the **Boilers Act, 1923** / Indian Boiler Regulations by a **state Inspector of Boilers or a competent person/Inspecting Authority**.
  - **Fire systems:** a **Fire NOC / fire safety certificate from the State Fire Service** is required before occupancy, generally referencing **NBC 2016 Part 4** and the state fire rules.
  - **None of these is gated by a commissioning credential; each is gated by a distinct statutory inspectorate or licensed contractor/supervisor. NOT FOUND: a national T&C engineer licence.**

### D3. Building Performance / Measurement & Verification (M&V) Analyst
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.**
- **Does:** Designs and runs M&V per **IPMVP** (Options A/B/C/D — retrofit isolation, whole facility, calibrated simulation) and **ASHRAE Guideline 14**; sets the baseline period and adjustments; installs and validates metering; regression modelling against weather and occupancy; computes avoided energy use and savings uncertainty; verifies ESCO/EPC guaranteed savings; ongoing/monitoring-based commissioning; fault detection and diagnostics from BMS trends; portfolio benchmarking and EUI tracking.
- **Deliverables:** M&V plan, baseline model with statistical fit (CV-RMSE, NMBE per Guideline 14 acceptance criteria), metering plan and meter verification records, **savings verification report** with uncertainty, ESCO payment certificate basis, ongoing commissioning reports, FDD issue lists, EUI/benchmark dashboard, LEED EAc3 Advanced Energy Metering and EBOM performance documentation, ENERGY STAR / BEE star-label data submissions.
- **Employs:** ESCOs (EESL and private), consultancies, large portfolio owners and REITs, BEE programmes, utilities' DSM cells, international consultancies.
- **THE GATE:** **No Indian statutory licence.** **But the ESCO money gate is real: under an Energy Performance Contract, savings certification typically must come from a BEE Certified/Accredited Energy Auditor or an agreed independent M&V agent named in the contract, and under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001 designated consumers must have their energy audit conducted by an accredited energy auditor** (the accreditation route is the **BEE (Certification Procedures for Energy Auditors and Energy Managers) Regulations, 2010** under the s.13/14 rule-making power; the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022 inserts the "energy auditor" definition tied to the prescribed qualifications). Market credentials: **AEE CMVP** (Certified Measurement & Verification Professional) — not legally required. **Who signs what:** the **accredited energy auditor signs the mandatory energy audit of a designated consumer**; the **named M&V agent signs the savings verification that releases ESCO payment**.

### D4. Air-Tightness Tester (India: "Blower Door Tester" — a thin market)
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.**
- **Does:** Whole-building and zone pressurisation/depressurisation testing to **ISO 9972** (or ASTM E779 / ASTM E3158 for large buildings); measures air changes per hour at 50 Pa (ACH50) and air permeability (m³/h·m² at 50 Pa); guarded-zone and multi-fan tests for large buildings; leakage localisation with smoke, thermography and anemometry; duct leakage testing (ASTM E1554 / SMACNA); cleanroom and containment leak testing; smoke-control and stair-pressurisation testing.
- **Deliverables:** Air-tightness test report with ACH50/q50, pressure-vs-flow data and regression, calibration certificates for fans and manometers, envelope leakage location schedule with thermal images, duct leakage class result, remedial recommendations, pass/fail against the specified target, passive-house/certification test certificate.
- **Employs:** Specialist testing firms (few in India), façade consultants, Passive House and net-zero projects, cleanroom and pharma validation firms, data-centre commissioning teams, researchers; internationally this is a mature trade because codes require it.
- **THE GATE:** **NO Indian requirement — this is the clearest "no gate because no rule" case in the family. NOT FOUND: any ECBC 2017 or Eco-Niwas Samhita mandatory air-tightness test.** Internationally it IS mandatory: **UK Building Regulations Approved Document L requires air-permeability testing of new dwellings and buildings, carried out by a tester registered with an approved competent-persons scheme (ATTMA/BINDT or iATS)**; several US energy codes (IECC) and Passive House certification require a tested ACH50 limit; so the *gate exists abroad and is on the tester's scheme registration*. For Passive House, the test must be performed to ISO 9972 and witnessed/documented for the certifier.

### D5. Thermography Technician (India: "Thermographer", "IR Survey Engineer")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.**
- **Does:** Infrared surveys — building envelope thermal-bridge and insulation-defect mapping, moisture-ingress and water-leak detection, flat-roof surveys; electrical thermography of panels, busbars, terminations and transformers; mechanical thermography of bearings, motors, steam traps; solar PV module hot-spot and string-fault surveys; sets emissivity, reflected temperature, ambient and delta-T conditions and reports qualitative vs quantitative findings with severity.
- **Deliverables:** Thermographic survey report with paired IR/visual images, anomaly register with severity classification and recommended action, delta-T tables, envelope thermal-bridge map, roof moisture map, PV string/module fault list, camera calibration certificate, survey conditions log.
- **Employs:** Predictive-maintenance and condition-monitoring firms, insurers and insurance surveyors, facility management majors, solar O&M firms, electrical safety auditors, forensic/building-pathology consultants.
- **THE GATE:** **No Indian statutory requirement. NOT FOUND.** The recognised competence framework is **ISO 18436-7 (Condition monitoring and diagnostics of machines — Requirements for qualification and assessment of personnel: Thermography)** with Category/Level I–III certification, and ASNT **Level I/II/III in Infrared and Thermal Testing** under **ASNT SNT-TC-1A** or **ISO 9712**. These are **required by contract and by insurers** (an electrical thermography report accepted for an insurance premium credit or a statutory electrical safety audit usually must come from a Level II thermographer), **not by Indian law**. Where thermography forms part of a statutory electrical safety audit, the audit itself is signed by the **approved/empanelled electrical safety auditor or chartered electrical engineer** per state Electrical Inspectorate rules.

### D6. Acoustic Tester / Acoustic Consultant (India: "Acoustic Consultant", "Noise & Vibration Consultant")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.**
- **Does:** Room-acoustics design and measurement (reverberation time per **ISO 3382**, speech transmission index); airborne and impact sound insulation measurement of partitions and floors (**ISO 16283 / ISO 140** series, rated to **ISO 717** as Rw/DnT,w/L'nT,w); mechanical services noise (NC/NR/RC criteria in occupied spaces); environmental and boundary noise surveys against the **Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000** limits by zone and day/night; vibration measurement and isolation design (metro-induced ground-borne noise and vibration, generator and chiller isolation); construction-noise monitoring; auditorium and studio design; façade sound insulation design.
- **Deliverables:** Acoustic design report and specification, measured RT/STI results, sound insulation test report with single-number ratings, services noise compliance measurement, environmental noise survey report with Leq/L10/L90/Lden and zone compliance statement, vibration survey (VDV/PPV), isolation design and product schedule, LEED/WELL acoustic-credit documentation, construction-noise monitoring logs.
- **Employs:** Acoustic consultancies, architecture practices, metro and infrastructure projects (mandatory noise and vibration assessment in the EIA), developers near highways and airports, studios/auditoria/hospitals, SPCB monitoring contractors.
- **THE GATE — YES, A SPECIFIC ONE, AND IT IS A LAB GATE NOT A CONSULTANT GATE:** environmental noise monitoring submitted for **SPCB consent compliance or an EIA** must come from a laboratory **recognised under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 / notified as an Environmental Laboratory, and in practice NABL-accredited for the relevant parameters** (see D7). The **Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000** set the limits and vest enforcement in the designated authority; the **test report is signed by the authorised signatory of the recognised/accredited laboratory**. **Design-stage acoustic consultancy itself is ungated. NOT FOUND:** any Indian mandatory sound-insulation testing requirement for completed buildings (contrast: **UK Approved Document E requires pre-completion sound testing or Robust Details registration for new dwellings**, performed by a UKAS-accredited or ANC-registered tester).

### D7. NABL-Accredited Lab Testing Coordinator (India: "QC Engineer – Lab", "Materials Testing Coordinator")
- **NCO-2015: NOT FOUND.**
- **Does:** Plans the project's testing regime against the specification and the relevant IS codes (concrete cube/cylinder strength, slump and flow, cement, aggregates, steel reinforcement tensile/bend/rebend and chemical, soil CBR/proctor/triaxial, bitumen, bricks and blocks, water quality, welds by NDT, paint and coating DFT, façade water penetration and structural performance); samples, identifies, seals and dispatches; selects and audits the laboratory; reviews and accepts test reports; manages non-conformances and retesting; maintains the test register for handover and for structural audit.
- **Deliverables:** Test and inspection plan (ITP), sampling register, **accredited laboratory test reports** with NABL scope reference and authorised signatory, concrete cube strength statistical analysis, non-conformance reports and retest records, materials approval register, mix design approval documents, structural audit test package, QA/QC dossier at handover.
- **Employs:** Contractors' QA/QC departments, PMCs, owners' quality cells, independent inspection agencies, testing laboratories, highway and metro authorities.
- **THE GATE — YES, AND THIS IS THE BEST-DOCUMENTED LAB GATE IN INDIAN CONSTRUCTION:**
  - **NABL is the accreditation body under the Quality Council of India and accredits testing and calibration laboratories against ISO/IEC 17025** — https://nabcb.qci.org.in/ and https://qcin.org/
  - **National highways:** MoRTH circular dated **22 April 2022** states that executing agencies involved in NH projects **"may mandatory insist that private laboratories used for third-party testing have NABL accreditation"** and that **"all labs, including BIS labs, should get NABL accreditation for their products and tests"** — https://morth.gov.in/sites/default/files/comprehensive_compendium_circular/3100.15-22.04.2022%20NABL%20Laboratory_accreditation.pdf
  - **CPWD:** the **CPWD Works Manual 2014, clause 53.20** and subsequent office memoranda govern the use of outside private laboratories; a circular records that unauthorised testing in private labs leads to substandard material and is "in contravention to the proviso of CPWD 2014 manual clause 53.20," and that a private lab is approved **only for the specified tests and projects** — https://www.hrmsfci.in/aps/CivilEngg/circular/Adobe%20Scan%2024-Jul-2021%20(1).pdf
  - **State PWD:** a 2013 state PWD order required contractors to have **20% of construction material tested by accredited labs**, and the refusal to consider a non-accredited laboratory was litigated — https://future.indiankanoon.org/doc/81850017/
  - **NABL temporary site laboratory recognition scheme — announced 30 January 2024:** recognition of temporary site testing laboratories at construction sites, covering **fine aggregates, coarse aggregates and concrete**; **mobile testing facilities are NOT included**; it is **voluntary and "an independent quality assurance scheme, which is not covered under APAC & ILAC MRA"** — i.e. **it is NOT ISO 17025 accreditation**; competence is assessed through on-site assessments and proficiency testing with possible unannounced assessments; **recognition lasts for the project duration, maximum 5 years**; practice threshold reported as projects above ~50,000 sq ft constructed area; **NABL–CREDAI MoU signed November 2024** — https://temporarysitelab.qci.org.in ; https://housing.com/news/nabl-launches-recognition-scheme-for-temporary-site-laboratories/ ; https://egov.eletsonline.com/2024/11/nabl-and-credai-sign-mou-to-strengthen-quality-assurance-in-construction/
  - **WHO MAY SIGN WHAT:** a test report is signed by the **authorised signatory named in the laboratory's NABL scope of accreditation**, and is valid only **for parameters inside that scope** — a NABL-accredited lab signing a test outside its listed scope produces an unaccredited report. **BOTTOM LINE, STATED PRECISELY: NABL accreditation is NOT a blanket national legal requirement for every construction test report. It is mandatory where the tender, the CPWD Works Manual, the state PWD code, or the MoRTH circular makes it so — which covers most central and state government work and NH projects — and it is the universal requirement for a report relied on in a structural audit, an insurance claim or litigation.** For **environmental** parameters the parallel gate is recognition as an **Environmental Laboratory under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986** plus NABL accreditation.

# WHAT IS ALREADY MACHINE-READABLE HERE

This family is the most digital, so the honest answer splits three ways: **(1) genuinely open, standardised, parseable data; (2) open container, non-standardised content — the data is readable but its meaning is not; (3) locked in vendor formats or in PDF, which is the default for every regulatory deliverable in India.**

## 1. Genuinely open and structured — a program can read it and act on it

| Format | Standard | Carries | Who produces it in this list |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IFC** (.ifc, .ifcXML, .ifcZIP) | **ISO 16739-1** (IFC4); IFC2x3 still dominant in practice | Building object geometry + type + properties + relationships, spatial structure, systems | A1–A11 (all BIM roles) |
| **IDS** (.ids) | buildingSMART **Information Delivery Specification** — XML; each Specification has exactly three parts: **metadata, applicability, requirements**; applicability filters by IFC class or finer criteria; property URIs can resolve into **bSDD** | The *requirement* itself, machine-checkable: which objects must exist, which classifications, properties, values, units | A11 (BIM standards author) writes it; A2/A6 run it. https://technical.buildingsmart.org/projects/information-delivery-specification-ids/ ; https://www.buildingsmart.org/?p=25444 ; developed openly at github.com/buildingSMART/IDS |
| **bSDD** | buildingSMART Data Dictionary, REST API | Standardised definitions, units, relations, classifications — resolvable property URIs | A11; anyone authoring properties |
| **BCF** (.bcf, .bcfzip; BCF-API) | buildingSMART Collaboration Format | A coordination *issue*: viewpoint, camera, clipping, GUIDs of the clashing elements, comments, status, assignee, due date | A2, A6, E3 (VR review issues) |
| **COBie** | Construction-Operations Building information exchange (an IFC MVD, delivered as spreadsheet/XML/IFC) | Asset handover data: facility, floor, space, zone, type, component, system, spare, resource, job, document, attribute | A8, A10, D1 |
| **gbXML** | Green Building XML schema | Thermal zones, surfaces, constructions, spaces, loads — the BIM→energy-model bridge | C6 (energy modeller) |
| **IDF / epJSON** | EnergyPlus input | Full simulation input, including every construction, schedule and system object | C6 |
| **LandXML** | LandXML.org schema | Survey points, alignments, profiles, cross-sections, surfaces (TIN), parcels — the survey/civil interchange and the format machine-control systems eat | B3, B4 (setting-out + AMG), B8 |
| **E57** (.e57) | **ASTM E2807** | Registered point clouds with per-scan pose, intensity, colour, and metadata | B6 |
| **LAS / LAZ** | ASPRS LAS specification (LAZ = lossless compression) | Point clouds with classification codes, returns, intensity, GPS time — the LiDAR lingua franca | B6, B7, B8 |
| **GeoJSON** | **RFC 7946** | Vector features + attributes, WGS84, web-native | B9 |
| **GeoPackage** | **OGC GeoPackage** (SQLite) | Vector + raster + tiles + attributes in one queryable file | B9 |
| **GeoTIFF / COG** | OGC GeoTIFF; Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF | Orthomosaics, DSM/DTM rasters with CRS and geotransform | B7, B8 |
| **CityGML / 3D Tiles / glTF** | OGC | Semantic 3D city models; streamable 3D for web/XR | B9, A8, E3 |
| **WMS / WFS / WMTS / OGC API** | OGC | Live spatial services | B9 |
| **ISO 19115 / ISO 19139** | Geographic metadata | Lineage, accuracy, CRS, constraints — the thing that makes a survey dataset legally interpretable | B3, B8, B9 |
| **IFC-SPF property sets + classification (Uniclass, OmniClass, MasterFormat)** | — | Lets a rule engine find "all external walls" reliably | A11 |
| **EPD data in EN 15804 machine-readable form (ILCD/EPD-XML, ECO Portal)** | EN 15804, ISO 14025 | Stage-wise GWP per declared unit | C8 |
| **ifcOWL / Brick / Haystack ontologies** | W3C LBD / Brick / Project Haystack | BMS point tagging and semantic queries over operational data | A8, D3 |

## 2. Open container, meaning NOT standardised — readable bytes, unreadable semantics

- **IFC in practice.** The format is open, but property naming, classification and LOI are project-specific. An IFC file from a Revit MEP model and one from a Tekla structural model do not agree on what a "fire rating" property is called or where it lives. **This is exactly the hole IDS was created to fill, and it only closes if someone authors the IDS.** Without an IDS, "we exchanged IFC" guarantees nothing checkable.
- **COBie.** The schema is fixed; the *content* is not. "Type.Name," "Component.SerialNumber" and "Space.Name" are routinely filled with project-local conventions, so two COBie drops cannot be compared or merged without mapping work.
- **Point clouds.** E57/LAS carry geometry and intensity faithfully. **They carry no semantics at all** — a point cloud does not know it is a wall. Classification codes in LAS cover terrain/vegetation/building at a coarse level, nothing building-interior. Segmentation to objects is ML or manual work.
- **LandXML.** Excellent for alignments and surfaces; weak and inconsistently implemented for parcels, cadastral attributes and Indian revenue-record semantics (survey number, hissa, tippan).
- **Energy models.** IDF/gbXML fully describe a model but say nothing about the *compliance claim*. Two modellers can build ECBC-compliant-looking models with incomparable assumptions; the baseline rules live in prose (ASHRAE 90.1 Appendix G, ECBC Chapter-level rules), not in data.

## 3. Locked in vendor formats, or worse, in PDF

- **Authoring natives:** `.rvt` / `.rfa` (Autodesk — proprietary, no published spec), `.pln`/`.gsm` (Graphisoft), `.dgn` (Bentley), `.db1` (Tekla), `.dwg` (Autodesk — reverse-engineered by the ODA, not an open standard; `.dxf` is published but lossy and geometry-only). **Every Indian regulatory submission is a DWG or a PDF.**
- **Reality capture natives:** `.rcp`/`.rcs` (Autodesk ReCap), `.fls`/`.lsproj` (Faro), `.zfs` (Zoller+Fröhlich), `.tzf` (Trimble) — E57 is the escape hatch and it is often the lossy one (loses vendor-specific scan metadata).
- **Clash and coordination:** `.nwd`/`.nwf`/`.nwc` (Navisworks) and `.smc` (Solibri) are closed. **BCF is the only open escape, and it carries the issue, not the model.**
- **Schedule:** `.xer`/`.xml` (Primavera P6) is semi-open; `.mpp` (MS Project) is closed. 4D links are usually stored in the Synchro/Navisworks file, not in an open format.
- **THE BIG ONE — EVERY INDIAN COMPLIANCE AND CERTIFICATION DELIVERABLE IS A SIGNED PDF OR A SCAN.** Specifically: the ECBC/ENS compliance certificate signed by the TPA or Certified Energy Auditor (Building); MahaRERA Forms 1/2/3/4; the carpet-area statement and Architect Certificate; the licensed surveyor's tippan and survey plan (often a scanned manual drawing); the NABL laboratory test report; the lift licence, Fire NOC, Electrical Inspectorate approval, SPCB consent; the commissioning report and certificate; IGBC/GRIHA/LEED submissions (uploaded documents, reviewed by humans, with the scorecard as the only structured artefact); the EIA report; the drone flight permission (Digital Sky is an API-mediated platform, which makes the *permission* structured, but the survey output it authorises is not). **The official Drone Rules 2021 gazette and the thc.nic.in copy are scanned images with no text layer — I could not text-extract them (pdftotext yielded 1,513 bytes of nothing usable from an 8 MB file). The law governing India's most digital survey technique is itself not machine-readable.**

## 4. Where a rule CAN be checked automatically against a model — and where it cannot

**Fully automatable today (deterministic, geometry + property driven):**
- **IDS validation.** Does every IfcDoor have a FireRating? Is every IfcSpace classified? Are units correct? This is exactly what IDS was built for and open-source validators exist. **This is the single highest-leverage automation in this whole family.**
- **Clash detection.** Hard clash, clearance clash, duplicate-element detection — pure geometry, fully deterministic. Rule sets are authored once (A6) and run forever.
- **Model health and standards compliance.** Naming conventions, worksets, orphaned elements, element counts by category, LOD proxies, missing classification, units, coordinate system and shared-site checks, model file size and view counts.
- **Area and quantity checks.** Gross/net/carpet area computed from space boundaries against a stated rule; FAR/FSI and ground-coverage arithmetic; BOQ quantity extraction against a measurement basis; **carpet-area computation per RERA s.2(k) is arithmetically specifiable from the model: net usable floor area, minus external walls, minus service shafts, minus exclusive balcony/verandah, minus exclusive open terrace, PLUS internal partition walls.** That is a checkable formula — and this is where an automated check has real commercial value given the 3% statutory variation cap.
- **ECBC envelope prescriptive rules.** **This is the most automatable regulatory check in Indian building law.** Window-to-wall ratio by orientation, assembly U-value against the climate-zone table, SHGC against the table, roof and opaque-wall U-value, skylight area and U/SHGC, lighting power density by space type against the table — all are *table lookups against quantities the model already holds*. Likewise **Eco-Niwas Samhita Part I RETV**, which is a closed-form weighted calculation over envelope components and orientations: a model plus the climate zone plus the component properties gives the number with no judgement. (I did not verify the numeric RETV limit from the BEE PDF — a secondary source gives RETV ≤ 15 W/m² and roof U ≤ 1.2 — treat those figures as unverified and read the limits off https://beeindia.gov.in/sites/default/files/Eco%20Niwas%20Samhita%202021%20Part-%202.pdf and the 2018 Part I.)
- **Code geometry checks generally.** Minimum room dimensions and heights, staircase riser/tread and width, corridor widths, travel distance to exit, door widths, accessibility clearances and turning circles, parking counts and bay dimensions, setbacks — all are geometric predicates against the NBC 2016 and the local development control regulations. (Singapore's CORENET and Norway's ByggSøk have done versions of this for years; **India has no equivalent automated plan check — NOT FOUND**.)
- **Point-cloud deviation analysis.** As-built vs design surface deviation, verticality, flatness (FF/FL), slab thickness, clearance verification — fully deterministic once the cloud is registered to project control.
- **Survey closure and accuracy.** Traverse misclosure, bundle-adjustment residuals, GCP/checkpoint RMSE against a stated tolerance, registration error per scan — all are numbers with pass/fail thresholds.
- **M&V baseline statistics.** CV-RMSE and NMBE against ASHRAE Guideline 14 acceptance criteria — deterministic.
- **Drone pre-flight.** Zone classification against the Digital Sky airspace map, UIN validity, RPC validity, 400 ft/120 m ceiling — **this is already automated in India via Digital Sky and NPNT**, which is the one place where an Indian construction-adjacent regulation is enforced by machine rather than by signature.
- **Document completeness.** Does the handover set contain every container the MIDP promised, at the right revision and status? Pure metadata query against the CDE.

**Needs judgement — and will keep needing it:**
- **Whether a clash matters.** A pipe through a non-structural ceiling void that will be re-routed anyway versus a duct through a shear wall. Severity, cost and sequence consequence are human calls; the detection is not.
- **Whether a model is fit for its purpose.** IDS tells you the property exists. It cannot tell you the value is *right*, or that the geometry represents the real intent, or that the model is buildable.
- **ECBC/LEED whole-building performance path.** The baseline is a *constructed hypothetical* — system type mapping, baseline fan power, unmet load hours, exceptional calculation methods. Appendix G is rules plus interpretation, and reviewers disagree with modellers constantly. Not automatable.
- **Every rating credit with a narrative.** Innovation credits, regional priority, integrative process, site selection, community connectivity, "demonstrate equivalence" paths — judged by a reviewer. The *scorecard* is structured data; the *decision* is not.
- **Commissioning.** A functional performance test is a physical experiment with a pass/fail criterion; whether the system "meets the Owner's Project Requirements" per ASHRAE Guideline 0-2019 is a professional opinion about whether the OPR was even the right OPR. The checklist is automatable; the verdict is not.
- **Thermography and acoustics.** Anomaly severity classification, whether a delta-T is a defect or an expected load pattern, whether a reverberation time serves the room's purpose — all judgement over measured data.
- **Cadastral and boundary determination.** This is the hardest one and it is not a data problem. A boundary is a **legal** fact determined from title documents, revenue records, possession, adjoining owners' consent and, in dispute, a court — not from coordinates. A survey instrument produces coordinates; **only a licensed surveyor under the state's land revenue code (and ultimately a revenue officer or court) determines where the boundary IS.** No amount of RTK accuracy changes that, which is precisely why the licence gate in B1/B2 exists.
- **Carpet area in the hard cases.** The formula is automatable; the *inputs* are contested — is that duct a "service shaft," is that a "balcony" or usable area, is a cupboard inside or outside. The Haryana REAT appointed a human architect/engineer to measure, and MahaRERA caps variation at 3% precisely because the measurement is arguable at the margin.
- **LCA scope decisions.** System boundary, functional unit, allocation method, data-quality judgement, scenario choice for stage B and C. ISO 14044 requires a **critical review by a panel** for public comparative assertions for exactly this reason.
- **Whether a deliverable is complete enough to pay for.** Always human.

**The structural gap worth naming:** the automatable side of this family is almost entirely the **technical** rules (IDS, clash, areas, ECBC envelope, RETV, survey closure), while the **legal** gates — who may sign the ECBC compliance certificate, who may certify carpet area, who may survey a boundary for record, whose test report counts, who may fly — are all gates on a **person's accreditation or licence**, recorded as a **signature on a PDF**. Nothing in the current Indian stack binds a credential to a deliverable in a machine-checkable way. Digital Sky, where a flight permission is granted and bound to a UIN and an RPC through an API, is the sole counterexample — and it is the only one.
