### THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT LEGAL FACT FOR SOFTWARE
India has **no statutory licensing of engineers at all**. MBBL 2016 cl. 6.3.6 says so in terms:

> "Presently, the legislation for the profession of architecture is applicable in the country in the form of Architects Act 1972… **For other professions and professionals like engineers, structural engineers, landscape architects, urban designers, building supervisors, electrical engineers, developers/promoters there is no legislative frame available/applicable.** Therefore, for appropriate qualifications, competence and responsibilities… shall be applicable as per Appendix 'E'."

So the gate for everyone except the architect is **(a) registration/licensing with the individual municipal authority or development authority**, and **(b) membership of a private body (IE(I), ITPI, ISSE) that the bye-law names**. The gate varies city by city. The architect's gate is national (COA). Everyone else's is local — which means software must resolve the gate **per ULB**, not per country.

Second fact: **Architects Act s.37 protects the TITLE, not the FUNCTION.** The Supreme Court in *Mukesh Goyal* (2020) held s.37 "does not prohibit the practice of architecture by unregistered individuals" but does prohibit them using "the title and style of architect". The *function* gate is created separately by each bye-law (who may sign a submission drawing). Also note the Council's Aug-2026 public advisory that architects registered under the Act "do not require any additional registration with local bodies" (https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/council-of-architecture-warns-against-unregistered-individuals-misusing-architect-title-issues-public-advisory) — this **conflicts in practice** with city registers (MCGM, Surat) that do register architects; treat as contested.

## 1. ARCHITECTURE

**A1. Architect / Registered Architect — Indian market title: "Architect", "Principal Architect", "Architect on Record (AR)"**
1. NCO-2015: **2161.0100 "Architect"** (group 2161 Building Architects; also 2161.9900 "Building Architects, Other") — unverified per §0.
2. Day to day: takes the client's brief and the plot's bye-law envelope (FAR, setbacks, height, ground coverage, parking) and produces the building's form and plan; then runs the sanction submission through the ULB and coordinates structural/MEP consultants onto one set of drawings.
3. Deliverables: concept/option drawings, submission (sanction) drawing set, GFC/working drawings, door-window-finish schedules, details, tender drawings + specifications, site instructions, **supervision certificate**, **completion certificate** (architectural aspects), RERA Form 1 (percentage of physical completion) in most states.
4. Employers: architectural consultancy (own or employed), developer in-house, government (CPWD/PWD/Chief Architect posts), contractor design-build cells, self-employed.
5. **THE GATE — the strongest in the Indian industry:**
 - **Title:** Architects Act 1972 s.37(1): *"no person other than a registered architect, or a firm of architects shall use the title and style of architect"* — criminal penalty under s.37(2). Registration = COA under s.23–25.
 - **Signing:** MBBL Appendix E, item 1, architect competence: *"(a) Prepare and sign all plans, sub-division/layout plans and information connected with building permit **except engineering services of multi-storeyed/special buildings**; (b) …development permit of area up to 1 hectare for metro-cities and 2 hectare for other places; (c) Issuing certificate of supervision for development permit [same limits]; (d) Issuing certificate of supervision and completion of all buildings pertaining to architectural aspects."* Qualification cell: *"Registered with valid membership of the Council of Architecture, India as prescribed under Architect's Act 1972."*
 - **Delegated sanctioning power** (MBBL Appendix E-1): an **Empanelled Architect** — COA-registered, ≥5 years' experience, empanelled 2 years at a time, security deposit + professional indemnity bond + professional liability insurance — is *"authorized person to sanction building plans of residential buildings up to 15 mt. in height and for plot size up to 500sqm, forming part of an approved lay-out plan"*, retains 50% of the application fee, files quarterly returns, and can be **struck off the panel and referred to COA** for a violation. This is the one place in Indian building law where a private professional literally *is* the approving authority.
 - **RERA:** s.4(2)(l)(D) — withdrawal from the 70% designated account only after *"an engineer, an architect, and a chartered accountant in practice certify that the withdrawal is in proportion to the percentage of completion of the project."* Named professionals cannot be swapped without intimating the Authority.
 - **Government buildings** (MBBL App. E-1 cl.5): plans *"shall be sanctioned by the Chief Architect/ Senior Architect of the concerned Department of the Government."*
 - International: UK — Architects Act 1997 s.20, ARB register, title only; US — state licence + **seal**, and the seal may not be applied to work the architect did not prepare or directly supervise (49 Pa. Code §9.142).

**A2. Project Architect** — NCO: subsumed in 2161.0100. Runs one project's drawing production and consultant coordination day to day; owns the drawing register, RFIs and revision discipline. Deliverables: coordinated GFC sets, RFI/ submittal responses, site visit reports. Employer: consultancy / developer / PMC. **Gate:** none of its own — signs **under** the registered architect's stamp. (This is the single most automatable architecture job precisely because it has no gate.)

**A3. Design Architect / Concept Architect** — NCO 2161.0100. Produces massing, plan parti, elevation language, materials palette; typically hands over at end of concept/schematic. Deliverables: option studies, 3D massing, design intent report, area statement. Employer: design-led consultancy, international practice's India arm. **Gate:** none for the design work; the resulting submission still needs an architect's stamp. Common Indian pattern: a foreign "design architect" + a COA-registered **Architect of Record** who stamps. Software should model these as two separate jobs.

**A4. Architectural Assistant / Junior Architect / Architectural Draughtsman** — NCO **3118.0100 "Draughtsperson, Architectural"** (also 3118.0301 "Draftsman", 3118.9900). Draws what the architect decides: plans/sections/elevations to scale, schedules, detail sheets; increasingly Revit modelling. Deliverables: drawing sheets, schedules, models, area statements. Employer: consultancy, contractor, developer. **Gate: none.** Note the Indian diploma route — MBBL "Supervisor" explicitly accepts *"Diploma in Civil engineering / **Architectural Assistantship** from recognized institute with 2yrs working experience"* or *"Civil Draftsmanship from I.T.I with 5yrs experience"*.

**A5. Specification Writer** — NCO: **NOT FOUND** (no NCO-2015 entry located). Writes the clause-by-clause material/workmanship specification bound to the tender BOQ and the IS codes. In India this is usually **not a separate job** — it is absorbed by the architect or the quantity surveyor/billing engineer; separate spec writers exist mainly in large PMCs and in the UK/US (NBS, MasterSpec, CSI MasterFormat). Deliverables: specification document, product/approved-makes list, Schedule of Finishes. **Gate: none.** Liability runs through whoever stamps the tender set.

**A6. Interior Architect** — NCO: nearest is **3432.0100 "Interior Designer"** (group 3432 Interior Designers and Decorators). Does interior spatial planning where structure/services are moved, so the work crosses into base-build. **Gate:** cannot be called "interior *architect*" unless COA-registered (s.37 protects "architect"; the Act's own carve-outs are for "landscape architect" and "naval architect" — nothing for "interior architect"). This is a real, overlooked exposure for Indian firms.

**A7. Liaison / Approvals Consultant ("liaisoning architect", "approval consultant")** — NCO: **NOT FOUND**. Assembles and walks the sanction file through the ULB, fire, airport (AAI NOC), environment, CFO and utilities; tracks objections. Deliverables: application forms, NOC file, compliance affidavits, sanctioned set. Employer: developer, self-employed. **Gate:** none for liaison itself, but the drawings in the file must carry the stamp of the architect/engineer/structural engineer as the bye-law requires. MBBL lists the NOC chain: Chief Fire Officer, Airport Authority of India, Chief Controller of Explosives, Civil Aviation, Authority land-use NOC.

**A8. Heritage / Conservation Architect** — NCO 2161.0100. Documents and specifies repair of listed fabric; appears before heritage conservation committees. Deliverables: condition survey, measured drawings, conservation plan, material specification. Employer: specialist consultancy, ASI/state archaeology, trusts. **Gate:** no separate statutory licence found; approval authority is the Heritage Conservation Committee under the city's bye-laws, not the individual.

**A9. Architectural Visualiser / 3D Artist** — NCO **2166.0401 "Designer (CAD)"** or 2166 Graphic & Multimedia Designers. Produces renders, walkthroughs, VR. **Gate: none.**

**A10. Client-side Design Manager** — NCO nearest **1323.0100 "Manager, Construction / Manager Construction Projects"**. Sits inside the developer, writes the brief, appoints and polices consultants, runs design freeze and value gates. Deliverables: brief, consultant scope matrices, design freeze registers, decision logs. **Gate: none.**

**A11. Plan-scrutiny / Building Permission Engineer or Architect (regulator side)** — NCO 1323/2142/2161 depending on post. Checks submitted drawings against FAR/setback/bye-laws in AutoDCR-type systems and raises objections. Employer: ULB / development authority. **Gate:** the *post* is reserved — after *Mukesh Goyal*, a public body "cannot promote or recruit individuals who do not hold a degree in architecture recognised by the Architects Act to a post that uses the title or style of 'architect'".

## 2. STRUCTURAL

**S1. Structural Engineer — Indian titles: "Structural Engineer", "Structural Engineer on Record (SER)", "Registered Structural Engineer (RSE)", "RCC Consultant"**
1. NCO: **2142.0300 "Civil Engineer, Structural"** (group 2142 Civil Engineers).
2. Day to day: fixes the structural system and grid with the architect, models and analyses the frame for gravity + wind + seismic per IS 456/800/875/1893/13920, and issues sized members with reinforcement.
3. Deliverables: **Structural Design Basis Report (SDBR)** (4 parts, Form 6 of the MHA Expert Committee Report), load calculations, analysis output, GFA/structural drawings, bar bending schedules, **certificate of structural safety of design (Form 3 / Form 14)**, retrofit schemes.
4. Employers: structural consultancy, architectural firm's in-house, developer, contractor, PEB fabricator, government.
5. **THE GATE:**
 - MBBL cl. 6.1: *"For any building under the jurisdiction of these regulations **structural design/retrofitting shall only be carried out by a Structural Engineer on Record (SER) or Structural Design Agency on Record (SDAR)**."*
 - MBBL Appendix E item 3 qualification: *"Graduate in Civil engineering… or Corporate Member (Civil) of Institution of Engineers (India), minimum 3 years experience in structural engineering practice"* (relaxed to 2 yrs with an M.Tech in structural engineering, 1 yr with a doctorate). Competence: *"…competent to prepare the structural design, calculations and details for all buildings and undertake their supervision. In case of buildings having special structural features, as decided by the Authority, they shall be designed **only by Structural engineers**."*
 - MBBL 6.1.6: *"SER or SDAR shall give a certificate of structural safety of design as per proforma given in Form-3 and Form 14… at the time of completion."*
 - Municipal registration is the real operative gate: Mumbai requires *"a structural engineer duly registered with MCGM"* (DCPR 2034 cl.49) and MCGM publishes the register of RCC structural engineers; Surat registers a **"Structural Designer"** needing *degree/AMIE(Civil) + 5 yrs responsible structural design experience, or M.E.(Structures) + 2 yrs, or doctorate + 1 yr*, whose scope is *"preparing and submitting structural details for all building types and special structures"*.
 - **No national licence. No chartered-engineer requirement in law.** IE(I) corporate membership is a private body's membership that the bye-law borrows.
 - **Note the engineer-vs-structural-engineer split** (MBBL App. E item 2 vs 3): a plain registered **Engineer** may prepare structural drawings/calcs only *"on plot up to 500 m² and up to 5 storeys or 16 m in height"*. Above that, SER only. This threshold is the exact line software must respect.

**S2. Engineer (generalist, registered) — "Engineer on Record (ER)", "Licensed Engineer"** — NCO **2142.0200 "Civil Engineer, Building"** / 2142.0100. Does the small-building whole job: plans, structural drawings, service plans, supervision. **Gate:** MBBL App. E item 2 — *"Graduate in Civil Engineering… Registered with valid membership (Civil) of the Institution of Engineers, India"*; may *"Prepare and sign all building plans, structural drawings and service plans"*, structural design limited to **≤500 m² plot and ≤5 storeys/16 m**, and may issue supervision + completion certificates *"including structure and building services"*. Surat variant: diploma holders admitted with 5 years' experience but *"limited to low-rise buildings"*.

**S3. Structural Designer / Analyst (employed)** — NCO 2142.0300. Builds the ETABS/STAAD/SAFE model, runs load combinations, sizes members, iterates on deflection and drift. Deliverables: model, analysis report, member schedules. **Gate: none — signs under the SER.** Highly computable (see §9).

**S4. Structural Detailer / RCC Draughtsman / Rebar Detailer** — NCO **3118.0500 "Draught person, Structural"**. Converts sized members into placing drawings and **bar bending schedules**; checks lap/development length, cover, congestion. Deliverables: placing drawings, BBS, cutting lists. **Gate: none.**

**S5. Steel Detailer / Connection Designer (fabrication drawings)** — NCO 3118.0500 / 3118.0401. Produces shop and erection drawings, bolt/weld connection design to IS 800, part marks. Deliverables: shop drawings, erection drawings, CNC/NC files, bill of steel. **Gate:** none statutory in India; connection design is often delegated to the fabricator but **the SER's stamp still covers the frame** — the delegation boundary must be explicit, and it is a frequent source of dispute.

**S6. Seismic Retrofit / Rehabilitation Specialist** — NCO 2142.0300. Evaluates an existing building's seismic resistance and designs strengthening (jacketing, shear walls, bracing, FRP). **Gate:** MBBL 6.1.3: *"Prior to seismic strengthening/retrofitting of any existing structure, evaluation of the existing structure… shall be carried out by a RSE/RSDA"* — i.e. retrofit is reserved to registered structural engineers, referencing IS 4326, IS 13935, IS 15988:2013. Deliverables: vulnerability assessment, retrofit design + drawings, safety certificate.

**S7. Proof Consultant / Peer Reviewer — "Proof Checking Consultant", member of the Structural Design Review Panel (SDRP)"** — NCO 2142.0300. Independently re-checks another engineer's design and certifies it. **Gate:** MBBL 6.1.5: *"The Competent Authority shall create a Structural Design Review Panel (SDRP) consisting of senior SER's and SDAR's whose task will be to review and certify the design prepared by SER or SDAR whenever referred by the competent authority"*, and *"The Reviewing Agency shall submit addendum to the certificate or a new certificate in case of subsequent changes in structural design."* **Table 6.1 — the mandatory proof-check matrix (quote):**

| Type of structure | Submission from SER/SDAR | To be proof-checked |
|---|---|---|
| Load-bearing buildings up to 3 storeys | SDBR | **Not to be checked** |
| Buildings up to 7 storeys (RCC/steel framed) | SDBR; Preliminary design | Both to be checked |
| Buildings > 7 storeys (RCC/steel framed) | SDBR; Preliminary design; Detailed design + structural drawings | All to be checked |
| **Special structures** (stadium, assembly hall, water tank, TV tower, chimney) | SDBR; Preliminary; Detailed + drawings | All to be checked |

Plus: *"In seismic Zone II, buildings & structures greater than 40m in height will require proof checking by SDRP."* This is **codified, thresholded, machine-decidable** — the ideal thing for software to compute; the *check itself* is not.

**S8. Geotechnical Engineer — "Geotechnical Engineer on Record (GER)", "Soil Consultant"** — NCO: **NOT FOUND** (no "Geotechnical Engineer" entry located in the NCO transcription; nearest 2142.0100 Civil Engineer, General). Plans and interprets the borehole/SPT/plate-load programme, gives bearing capacity, settlement, pile capacity, liquefaction and excavation-support advice. Deliverables: **geotechnical investigation report**, bearing capacity/safe load tables, pile load test reports, foundation recommendation, dewatering/shoring advice. Employer: specialist geotech firm, soil testing lab, large consultancy, government. **Gate:** no licence; but GER is a **named appointment** the owner must make — MBBL 6.3.8: *"The Owner/Developer shall appoint Town Planner on Record (TPR), Architect on Record (AR), Engineer on Record (ER), Structural Engineer on Record (SER), Structural Design Agency on Record (SDAR), **Geotechnical Engineer on Record (GER)**, Construction Engineer on Record (CER), (CMAR), and Quality Auditor on Record (QAR)… Proper written agreement(s), in standard format(s), shall be entered upon."* DUAC records that the 2016 Delhi bye-laws newly "clearly laid down" the competence of **Geotechnical Engineers** (https://duac.org.in/u/BuildingByeLaws). Lab side: NABL accreditation for the testing laboratory, not the engineer.

**S9. Structural Audit Consultant** — NCO 2142.0300. Inspects an ageing building and certifies its condition. **Gate:** city-specific and genuinely mandatory in Maharashtra (periodic structural audit of buildings beyond a stated age, by a municipality-empanelled structural engineer) — **exact provision NOT retrieved in this run; verify against MCGM circulars and the Maharashtra Municipal Corporation Act s.353B before relying on it.**

**S10. Temporary Works / Formwork / Shoring Designer** — NCO 2142.0300 / 2144.0500. Designs falsework, props, deep-excavation shoring, crane bases. Deliverables: temporary works design + calcs, prop layout, de-shuttering schedule, permit-to-load. Employer: contractor, formwork vendor. **Gate:** none in India (in the UK this is a CDM-2015 designer duty). Collapses here are frequent and liability sits with the contractor.

**S11. Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) Designer** — NCO 2142.0300. Designs the proprietary portal frame/purlin/cladding system to the client's loads. **Gate:** the **customer's SER must still accept and stamp** the frame for the sanction file; the PEB vendor's own certificate is not a bye-law certificate.

**S12. Post-Tensioning / Specialist Slab Designer** — NCO 2142.0300. PT flat slab design, tendon layout, stressing sequence. Deliverables: tendon drawings, stressing records, friction/elongation calcs. **Gate:** delegated design under the SER.

**S13. Construction Engineer on Record (CER) / Construction Management Agency on Record (CMAR)** — NCO **1323.0100 "Manager, Construction"**. Supervises the actual building so that it is built as designed. **Gate — a hard one:** MBBL 6.1.7.1: *"All construction **except load bearing buildings upto 3 storeys** shall be carried out under supervision of the Construction Engineer on Record (CER) or Construction Management Agency on Record (CMAR)"*; 6.1.7.2: *"CER/CMAR shall give a certificate of structural safety of construction as per proforma given in Form-13."* MBBL also fixes the liability split: *"Structural safety of a building is the responsibility of the 'SER/SDAR' for proper design and the 'CER/CMAR' for proper construction"* (6.3.7).

**S14. Quality Auditor on Record (QAR) / Quality Audit Agency on Record (QAAR)** — NCO nearest 2152.0601 "Quality Engineer" / 3112. Audits the construction quality plan. **Gate:** MBBL requires a QAR/QAAR **in seismic zones IV & V** (cl. 6.1.7.x, as extracted). Deliverables: quality assurance plan, audit reports, non-conformance registers.

## 3. MEP / BUILDING SERVICES

**M1. HVAC Design Engineer — Indian title "HVAC Consultant", "Mechanical Design Engineer"**
1. NCO: **2144.1200 "Mechanical Engineer, Air-Conditioning"** (group 2144).
2. Day to day: builds the room-by-room heat load, selects the system (VRF/chiller/AHU), sizes ducts/pipes/equipment, lays out shafts and plant rooms with the architect, and writes the equipment schedule.
3. Deliverables: heat load calculations (per ISHRAE/ASHRAE), equipment selection + schedules, duct/pipe layouts and sizing, psychrometrics, ventilation & smoke-extract design, specifications, BOQ, ECBC compliance figures, testing & commissioning (T&C) protocol.
4. Employers: MEP consultancy, architectural firm's services arm, developer, HVAC contractor (design-build), OEM.
5. **THE GATE: none in India for HVAC design.** No licence, no stamp. The only adjacent statutory hooks: **ECBC / state energy conservation building rules** compliance (certified by the ECBC-compliance route the state adopts; BEE-accredited **Energy Auditor** for audits), smoke-management provisions that go into the **Fire Officer's** NOC scrutiny, and the Ozone-Depleting Substances Rules on refrigerants. MBBL App. E does not list a mechanical/HVAC professional at all — it ends at Plumber, Electrician, Fire Consultant.

**M2. Electrical Design Engineer (LV/HV)** — NCO **2151.0100 "Electrical Engineer, General"** / **2151.0200 "Designer, Electrical Engineering"** / 2151.0300. Load list, transformer/DG/panel sizing, single-line diagram, cable sizing + voltage drop, earthing and lightning protection, lux design. Deliverables: SLD, load schedule, panel schedules, cable schedules, earthing layout, lighting layout + lux calcs, DG/UPS sizing, specifications, BOQ.
**THE GATE — real and often missed:**
 - MBBL App. E item 9, **Electrician**: *"As prescribed by the concerned electricity company."* (So the ULB defers to the DISCOM/state licensing board, which licenses **Electrical Contractors, Supervisors and Wiremen** under state Electrical Licensing Board rules.)
 - **CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations 2010, Reg. 43 "Approval by Electrical Inspector":** no electrical installation/apparatus **above 650 V** may be energised without the written approval of the Electrical Inspectorate; Reg. 32 for generating units. Form-A is the statutory application. Some states (e.g. Maharashtra) have notified 11 kV and allow **notified chartered engineers to self-certify** installations up to that voltage (Reg. 5A/43 as amended 2015).
 - So: a consultant may *design* freely; **energising requires the Electrical Inspector's approval and the work must be executed/certified by a licensed electrical contractor/supervisor.** Software may produce the design; it may not produce the completion/test certificate.

**M3. Plumbing / Public Health Engineer (PHE) — Indian title "PHE Consultant", "Plumbing Consultant"** — NCO **2142.0900 "Civil Engineer, Public Health"** (design level); also **2142.0301 "Plumbing Site Engineer"**, **3118.0201 "Plumbing Draftsman"**, **3123.0302 "Plumbing Supervisor"**, 7126.x for the trade. Water demand and storage calculations, pump head/sizing, hot and cold distribution, drainage and venting falls, rainwater harvesting, STP/WTP sizing, external services. Deliverables: water balance, demand/storage calcs, pump and pipe sizing, plumbing and drainage layouts, riser diagrams, RWH design, STP/ETP process design, specifications, BOQ.
**THE GATE:** design is ungated, **execution is gated.** MBBL App. E item 8, **Plumber**: *"licensed by the concerned Authority through examination of candidates — Certificate of training from ITI, with min. 2yrs experience of execution of sanitary and plumbing works… Knowledge of working drawings and dimensioned sketches."* Competence: *"Execution/supervision of sanitary works up to 500 sq mt plot size and 4 storeyed buildings."* Rainwater harvesting and dual plumbing are mandated by many ULBs, and STP/ETP discharge consent comes from the **State Pollution Control Board** (Consent to Establish/Operate) — a separate statutory gate on the *project*, not the person.

**M4. Fire Protection Engineer / Fire Fighting Systems Designer** — NCO **2142.0501 "Fire Protection Systems Design Engineer"**. Hydrant/sprinkler/pump sizing per IS 15105 / IS 3844 / NBC Part 4, detection and alarm zoning, pressurisation. Deliverables: hydraulic calculations, pump room and tank sizing, sprinkler/hydrant layouts, detection and alarm schematics, pressurisation/smoke-extract calcs, fire NOC drawing set, T&C protocol. **THE GATE — the hardest MEP gate in India:**
 - MBBL App. E item 10, **Fire Consultant**: *"As prescribed by Chief Fire Officer, Town/City Fire Service."*
 - MBBL 11.2: the Authority **refers the plans to the Chief Fire Officer**, who *"shall examine these plans"* and *"shall issue the No Objection Certificate"*; the Authority *"shall ensure that clearance from Chief Fire Officer has been obtained"*; and MBBL records the practice of the NOC being issued *"on the basis of undertaking given by the Fire Consultant / Architect"*. Occupation stage too: *"the work shall also be subject to the inspection of the Chief Fire officer"* and a completion report from him "from the fire protection point of view".
 - **Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act 2006** — the sharpest example of a statutory signing monopoly: the fire certificate may only be issued by a **"Licensed Agency"** = *"a person or an association of persons licensed by the Director, or… the Chief Fire Officer or the nominated officer, for undertaking or executing fire prevention and life safety [measures]"*; s.3(3): *"The owner or occupier… shall furnish to the Chief Fire Officer… **a certificate in the prescribed form issued by a Licensed Agency** regarding the compliance of the fire prevention and life safety measures"* — Form A (works executed) and Form B (half-yearly maintenance, January and July). The 2025 amendment adds Forms A-1/B-1 and requires that *"Form A, Form B, Form A-1 or Form B-1… shall be issued under signatory of the person having requisite qualification as mentioned in rule 12"*; the Directorate's guidelines require the agency's **digital signature to match the one on its licence**, forbid any *"addition or deletion of any word(s) as given in the Form B"*, and licences can be **class-restricted** (an agency "shall not issue Form A or Form B… for certain system classes"). **Software must never generate this certificate.**

**M5. ELV / Low-Voltage & Smart Systems Designer (BMS, CCTV, access control, PA, DAS, structured cabling)** — NCO nearest **2152.x Electronics Engineers** / 2153 Telecommunication Engineers / 7422.0204 "Infrastructure Engineer". I/O lists, point schedules, head-end layouts, camera coverage and pixel-density plans, cable schedules, network topology. Deliverables: point/IO schedules, riser and containment layouts, architecture diagrams, device layouts, specification, BOQ, SAT scripts. **Gate: none.** CCTV placement intersects privacy/DPDP Act obligations but there is no design licence.

**M6. Vertical Transportation / Lift Consultant** — NCO: **NOT FOUND** for the consultant (nearest 2144.0100). Runs traffic analysis (handling capacity %, interval), picks car/speed/door and machine room arrangement, writes the specification and witnesses tests. Deliverables: traffic analysis report, shaft/pit/overhead dimension drawings, machine room layout, specification, vendor comparison, T&C witness report. **Gate:** the **state Lifts & Escalators Act** regime — erection and operation of a lift require permission/licence from the state Electrical Inspector / designated authority, and periodic inspection; the installer, not the consultant, holds that. **Exact section not retrieved — mark "verify the applicable state Lifts Act".** NCO has only operator-level entries (8343.1800 "Liftman").

**M7. MEP Coordinator / Services Coordination Engineer** — NCO nearest 1323.0100 / 2144.0100. Resolves clashes between duct, pipe, tray and structure; fixes shaft and ceiling void zones; owns the composite services drawing. Deliverables: coordinated composite drawings, clash reports, ceiling void sections, builder's work (BWIC) drawings, sleeve/insert schedules. **Gate: none.** Near-fully computable (clash detection).

**M8. Energy / ECBC Modeller & Energy Auditor** — NCO **3113.0300 "Energy Auditor"**; design-side modeller NOT FOUND. Builds the whole-building energy model (eQUEST/EnergyPlus/IESVE), demonstrates ECBC/state-rule compliance, runs daylight and shading studies. Deliverables: energy model, ECBC compliance report, envelope U-value/SHGC compliance tables, LPD/EPI calcs. **Gate:** an **Energy Auditor accredited by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency** is required for statutory audits of designated consumers under the Energy Conservation Act 2001; ECBC compliance certification route is state-specific. Design modelling itself is ungated.

**M9. Medical Gas / Specialist Hospital Services Engineer** — NCO nearest 2144.0100. Designs manifolds, pipeline, alarm and vacuum systems to HTM/NFPA 99/IS. **Gate:** none in India for design; NABH accreditation drives the standard commercially.

**M10. Water & Wastewater Treatment Process Designer (STP/ETP/WTP)** — NCO nearest 2143 Environmental Engineers. Sizes process units, mass balance, blower/pump selection. Deliverables: process flow diagram, mass balance, unit sizing, P&ID, discharge-compliance statement. **Gate:** the **SPCB Consent to Establish/Operate** is the statutory permission, granted to the occupier, not the designer.

## 4. INTERIORS & FINISHES

**I1. Interior Designer** — NCO **3432.0100 "Interior Designer"** (group 3432 Interior Designers and Decorators; 3432.0200 "Decorator", 3432.9900). Selects and specifies loose furniture, fixtures, equipment and art; runs the procurement schedule against lead times and budget. Deliverables: FF&E schedule (room-by-room, with image, spec, qty, rate, lead time), cut sheets, mock-up approvals, procurement tracker, art/accessory schedule. **Gate: none.** This job is **mostly structured-document production** — the single most automatable interiors job.

**I3. Lighting Designer (architectural)** — NCO **2151.0400 "Illumination Engineer"** (design-side architectural lighting designer as a distinct job: NOT FOUND). Designs the luminous effect and sets lux/uniformity/glare and colour temperature, picks fittings and controls, does mock-ups. Deliverables: lighting layout, lux/UGR calculations (Dialux/AGi32), luminaire schedule, control/scene schedule, focusing instructions, mock-up report. **Gate: none in India.** (Emergency/escape lighting levels feed the fire NOC, which is the CFO's gate.)

**I4. Kitchen / Commercial Catering Consultant** — NCO **3432.0501 "Bathroom and Kitchen Designer"** for the domestic/retail job; the commercial catering consultant: NOT FOUND. Designs the back-of-house flow (receipt → store → prep → cook → wash → waste), sizes equipment to covers, specifies hoods and grease management. Deliverables: kitchen layout, equipment schedule with utility loads (the MEP consultant's primary input), hood/exhaust sizing, drainage and floor-trap plan, BOQ. **Gate:** none for design; **FSSAI licensing** attaches to the operator, and the kitchen's hood/suppression is inside the CFO's fire scrutiny.

**I5. Joinery / Millwork Designer (shop-drawing side)** — NCO nearest 2163.0400 / 3118.x. Converts the designer's detail into made-to-make shop drawings with hardware, edge and grain-direction, and a cutting list. Deliverables: shop drawings, hardware schedule, cutting/nesting list, finish samples. Employer: joinery fabricator, fitout contractor. **Gate: none.**

**I6. Signage & Wayfinding Designer** — NCO nearest 2166.0501 "Graphic Designer". Sign family, message schedule, sign location plan. Deliverables: sign type drawings, message schedule, location plans, fabrication details. **Gate:** none, except statutory signage (exit/fire signage per NBC Part 4 and accessibility signage) which is checked by the CFO/accessibility audit.

**I7. Accessibility / Universal Design Consultant** — NCO: NOT FOUND. Audits against the **Harmonised Guidelines and Standards for Universal Accessibility in India (2021)** and the **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016**. Deliverables: accessibility audit report, compliance checklist, remedial drawings. **Gate:** none for the consultant; the RPwD Act obligation is on the establishment, and the bye-law checklist is enforced by the ULB. ## 5. LANDSCAPE & URBAN

**L1. Landscape Architect**
1. NCO: **NOT FOUND.** The ISCO-08 group 2162 "Landscape Architects" returned **zero** rows in the NCO-2015 transcription available to me — i.e. NCO-2015 may simply not carry landscape architects, which is itself a finding worth verifying against the official Vol II-A.
2. Day to day: designs the external realm — grading and levels, planting, hardscape, water, external lighting, irrigation — and coordinates it against podium structure and services.
3. Deliverables: landscape masterplan, grading/levels plan, planting plan + plant schedule, hardscape layout and details, irrigation design, soil/drainage sections, external lighting layout, BOQ, maintenance manual.
4. Employers: landscape consultancy, architectural practice, developer, government (horticulture departments), self-employed.
5. **THE GATE:** **not a protected title; not COA-registrable as such** — and note that the Architects Act's prohibition "does not affect the use of designation as 'Landscape Architect' or 'Naval Architect'". The only gate is **municipal registration + a project-size trigger.** MBBL App. E item 5: qualification *"Bachelor or Master's degree in landscape architecture or equivalent from recognized Indian or foreign university"*; competence *"…competent to carry out the work related to landscape design for building/development permit for **land areas 5 hectares and above. In case of metro-cities, this limit of land area shall be 2 hectares and above**"*; and under the Town Planner's entry: *"for land layouts for development permit **above 5 hectare** in area, **landscape architect shall also be associated**"*. So above the threshold, their involvement is **mandatory**, below it merely "may be considered".

**L2. Urban Designer** — NCO: **NOT FOUND** (2164 holds only Town Planner/Traffic Planner). Shapes the public realm between buildings: street sections, block and plot structure, frontage and height controls, massing codes. Deliverables: urban design framework, block/plot plans, street sections, design codes/guidelines, 3D massing, public realm strategy. **Gate:** MBBL App. E item 6: *"Master's degree in Urban Design or equivalent"*; competence — *"urban design for city areas more than 5 hectares and campus area more than 2 hectares… also competent to carry out the work of urban renewal for all blighted/congested areas."* Mandatory above those sizes where the bye-law is adopted.

**L3. Town / Country Planner** — NCO **2164.0100 "Town Planner"** (group 2164 Town and Traffic Planners). Prepares layouts and sub-division plans, development plans and zoning proposals; certifies layout supervision. Deliverables: land sub-division/layout plan, area and density statements, zoning and land-use proposals, development plan reports, layout supervision certificate. Employer: Town & Country Planning Organisation / state T&CP departments / development authorities, planning consultancy, developer. **THE GATE:** MBBL App. E item 4: qualification *"Graduate or Post-graduate degree in Town and country planning with valid **Associate Membership of the Institute of Town Planners, India**"*; competence — *"(a) Preparation of plans for land sub-division/layout… for **all areas more than 1 Hectare**; (b) Issuing of certificate of supervision for development of land of all areas."* Note the **clean split with the architect**: architect may do layouts **up to** 1 ha (metro) / 2 ha (other); **above that, the town planner**. MBBL 6.3.8 also makes "Town Planner on Record (TPR)" a named appointment. Goa's T&CP publishes annual registers of registered architects/engineers/planners (https://tcp.goa.gov.in) — concrete evidence that state registration, not a national licence, is the operative gate.

**L4. Master Planner** — NCO 2164.0100. Does the same work at city/region/large-estate scale: structure plan, phasing, infrastructure capacity, density distribution. Deliverables: master plan drawings, phasing plan, infrastructure demand assessment, DCR/development control recommendations, reports. **Gate:** as L3; statutory master plans are notified by the state under the relevant Town & Country Planning Act — the *plan* is gated, not the planner.

**L5. Traffic / Transport Planner & Consultant** — NCO **2164.0200 "Traffic Planner"**. Does trip generation and modal split, junction capacity, parking demand and supply, access/egress geometry, construction traffic management. Deliverables: traffic impact assessment (TIA), junction capacity analysis, parking demand/provision statement, access geometry drawings, signage/marking plan, TMP. **Gate:** no personal licence; the **TIA is a condition of approval** imposed by the ULB/traffic police/road authority in many cities, and NOC from the road-owning authority is a separate project gate. Highly computable (IRC codes; trip rates).

**L6. Environmental Planner / EIA Consultant** — NCO nearest 2143 Environmental Engineers. Prepares the environmental clearance application for buildings above the EIA Notification 2006 thresholds. Deliverables: Form 1/1A, EMP, baseline monitoring, EIA/EMP report, clearance compliance reports. **THE GATE — organisational, not personal:** EIA reports for prior environmental clearance must come from a consultant organisation **accredited by NABET/QCI** under the MoEFCC scheme, and a named **Functional Area Expert** signs each chapter. **Exact OM/notification text NOT retrieved — verify at parivesh.nic.in / QCI NABET before relying.**

**L7. Horticulturist / Arborist** — NCO nearest 2132/6210. Species selection, soil, tree survey and transplantation. **Gate:** tree felling/transplantation requires **Tree Authority permission** under state Preservation of Trees Acts — a project gate.

**L8. Irrigation Designer** — NCO nearest 2142.0400 "Civil Engineer, Irrigation". Hydrozones, emitter and pipe sizing, controller schedule. Deliverables: irrigation layout, hydraulic calcs, controller programme. **Gate: none.**

Note on legal status of the COA scales: a 2015 government communication records that the Conditions of Engagement are *"neither approved by the Parliament under the Architects Act, 1972 nor by the Central Government"* — they are prescribed by the Council under the **Architects (Professional Conduct) Regulations 1989**. Treat the fee percentages as professional guidance, not law (different secondary copies give irreconcilable percentages, so **do not hard-code them**). The **India-specific extra stage that RIBA/AIA have no equivalent for is the sanction/NOC stage** (A7 liaison + fire NOC + AAI + SPCB) — any software built on RIBA or AIA stages alone will model the Indian project wrongly.

## 9. WHERE THE WORK IS JUDGEMENT vs WHERE IT IS CALCULATION OR DOCUMENT PRODUCTION

Three columns per family: **(C) arithmetic/codified — computable and verifiable**; **(D) document assembly — automatable, reviewable, low liability**; **(J) irreducible professional judgement carrying liability — the part that must stay with a named, gated human.**

**Architecture**
- **(C)** FAR/ground coverage/setback/height/parking/open-space arithmetic from the bye-law; area statements and BUA/carpet/RERA carpet computations; travel distance and exit-width counts; staircase and riser/tread geometry; accessibility dimensions and counts; daylight factor and shadow studies; room-by-room area schedules; drawing register, revision and sheet-numbering discipline; bye-law deviation and compounding-fee computation (MBBL Appendix F gives literal rate tables).
- **(D)** The entire submission set's **non-design** content: forms, affidavits, NOC checklists, title and ownership annexures, area tables, schedules of doors/windows/finishes, specification clauses, tender preliminaries, O&M dossiers, minutes, the compliance matrix.
- **(J)** The parti — which plan among the compliant ones; what the building *means* in its place; proportion, light, material and detail quality; whether a client brief is internally contradictory and which half to keep; whether a deviation is worth arguing to the authority or designing out; **and, decisively, the act of stamping — s.37 title + the supervision and completion certificates. A certificate is a personal assertion of fact backed by a career; it is not a document that can be "produced".**

**Structural**
- **(C)** Nearly all of it, and it is the most computable family in the industry: load take-down, combinations per IS 875 parts 1-5 / IS 1893; analysis and member design per IS 456 / IS 800 / IS 13920; deflection, crack-width, drift and soft-storey checks (MBBL 6.1.4 even gives the literal alternative rule: *"columns and beams of the soft storey shall be designed for 2.5 times the storey shears and moments"* or shear walls for *"1.5 times the lateral storey shear"*); bar bending schedules and cutting lists; **and the proof-check trigger itself — Table 6.1 is a pure lookup on storeys × framing × seismic zone × height, plus "Zone II and >40 m".**
- **(D)** The SDBR (Form 6) — four structured parts, a form; design calculation reports; drawing issue sheets; the Form-3/13/14 *templates*.
- **(J)** Choice of structural system and load path; what counts as a "special structural feature" (MBBL leaves it to the Authority's and the engineer's judgement); modelling idealisation — which stiffness, which restraint, which cracked-section assumption, what to ignore; detailing in congested zones; **the condition assessment of an existing building for retrofit (the single least computable act in structural engineering — reading a real, damaged, undocumented building)**; and signing the **certificate of structural safety**. MBBL fixes personal liability: design on SER/SDAR, construction on CER/CMAR.

**MEP / Services**
- **(C)** Heat load; duct and pipe sizing and pressure drop; water demand and storage; pump head; drainage falls and venting; cable sizing and voltage drop; short-circuit and earthing calcs; lux/UGR; sprinkler and hydrant hydraulics; lift traffic analysis (handling capacity and interval are formulae); ECBC envelope and LPD compliance tables; equipment schedules derived from the calcs.
- **(D)** Schedules (equipment, panel, cable, valve, point/IO), specifications, BOQ, T&C protocols and witness forms, O&M manuals, as-built registers.
- **(J)** System *selection* against the client's operating reality — who maintains it, what the power situation is, what the tenant will actually do; redundancy and failure-mode choices; plant room and shaft strategy, which is a spatial-political negotiation with the architect and structure, not a calculation; **fire strategy, where a defensible case must be made to a named officer** — and that officer's NOC, plus (in Maharashtra) the **Licensed Agency's Form A/B**, are the two things in this family that software must never generate.

**Interiors & Finishes**
- **(C)** Quantities and areas, finish take-off, lux calcs, FF&E quantity and cost roll-up, lead-time scheduling, joinery cutting/nesting optimisation.
- **(D)** Overwhelmingly this family's work: FF&E schedules, finish schedules, material boards, vendor comparison sheets, shop-drawing registers, snag lists, handover books. **FF&E and green-building documentation are the two most automatable jobs in this report.**
- **(J)** Taste, and the coherence of a material palette under real light; whether a sample is the sample; the judgement that a fitout has crossed into base-build (structure, exits, compartmentation) **and therefore re-attached a gate.**

**Landscape & Urban**
- **(C)** Grading/cut-and-fill volumes, drainage and stormwater runoff, irrigation hydraulics, planting quantities, open-space and tree-count compliance, **and the threshold triggers themselves — the 1 ha / 2 ha / 5 ha tests in MBBL Appendix E that decide WHO MUST BE APPOINTED are pure arithmetic and the highest-value thing software can compute in this family.**
- **(D)** Plant schedules, maintenance manuals, layout submission sets, development-permit annexures, design codes and guideline documents.
- **(J)** Whether a place works; species choice against a real microclimate and a real maintenance budget; the public-realm trade-offs in urban design; and in planning, the **weighing of competing public interests** — density against infrastructure against livelihood — which is the most explicitly political judgement in the whole industry and is why the statutory plan is *notified* by the state, not calculated.

**Specialist Consultants**
- **(C)** Façade thermal/condensation and anchorage loads; acoustic Rw/Dnt,w build-up arithmetic and RT; CFD and wind-tunnel post-processing; traffic trip generation and junction capacity per IRC; QS quantity take-off and rate analysis (**the most mechanisable large job in Indian construction consultancy**); clash detection; accessibility and code compliance matrices; energy models; proof-check and appointment-threshold triggers.
- **(D)** Credit documentation (green rating), compliance matrices, TVRA report templates, BEP and standards documents, waterproofing detail libraries, claim and delay-analysis assembly, valuation report scaffolding, tender documents.
- **(J)** Everything that is a *bet about an uncertain future*: will this façade leak in twenty monsoons; is this acoustic assumption true once the ceiling is actually built; is this soil parameter representative of the whole plot from six boreholes; is this claim arguable; is this valuation defensible before a tribunal. Plus the two judgements that are purely interpersonal: **arguing a deviation to a regulator, and telling a client that what they want is not possible.**

**THE OPERATIVE RULE FOR SOFTWARE, in one line:** in India, software may do **computation, drawing production, document assembly, compliance checking and gate-detection** across every job above — but **five classes of instrument must be produced by a named, registered human and must never be auto-generated or auto-signed**: (1) anything stamped as an **architect's** plan/supervision/completion certificate (Architects Act s.37 + COA; and the Empanelled Architect's *sanction* itself); (2) the **SER/SDAR structural design safety certificate and SDBR**, and the **proof-checker's certificate** (MBBL 6.1, 6.1.5, 6.1.6); (3) the **CER/CMAR construction-safety certificate (Form-13)** and the registered supervisor's supervision memo; (4) the **Chief Fire Officer's NOC** and any **Licensed Agency Form A/B/A-1/B-1** (Maharashtra Fire Act s.3(3), rule 12, digital signature bound to the licence); (5) the **statutory certificates of others' professions** — the RERA s.4(2)(l)(D) architect/engineer/CA certificates, the **IBBI Registered Valuer's** report, the **Electrical Inspector's** Reg. 43 energisation approval, and the **NABET-accredited EIA** report. The safe product shape is: compute everything, draft everything, **detect which gate applies at which threshold**, and hand the signature to the gated human with the evidence already assembled.
