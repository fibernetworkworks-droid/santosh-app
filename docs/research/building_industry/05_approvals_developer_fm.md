# BUILDING INDUSTRY JOB MAP — PARTS (A) APPROVALS & LIAISON, (B) DEVELOPER / CLIENT SIDE, (C) HANDOVER, FM & OPERATIONS
### India primary. Every "GATE" line states who applies / who certifies / which licence / whether software may prepare but not submit.

**Two standing caveats, stated once:**
1. **NCO-2015 codes.** I could not reach a searchable NCO-2015 (DGE&T/Ministry of Labour) index in this session. NCO-2015 is structurally aligned to ISCO-08 at the 4-digit family level, so the 4-digit families below are reliable as *families*; the 8-digit occupation titles (NCO uses `XXXX.XXXX`) are **NOT VERIFIED** individually. Where I am not confident even of the family, I say **NOT FOUND**. Treat every code as "family, to be confirmed against the NCO-2015 volumes".
2. **Building permission, fire, lifts, trees, structural audit and society law are STATE subjects.** Nothing below is uniform nationally. I use Maharashtra/MahaRERA and MoHUA's Model Building Bye-Laws 2016 as the worked example because they are the best-documented, and flag where states diverge.

# (A) STATUTORY APPROVALS AND LIAISON JOBS

### A1. Liaison Officer / Liaison Manager (common Indian title: "Liaisoning Officer", "LO", "Govt. Liaison Manager")
- **NCO-2015:** family 3359 (regulatory government associate professionals n.e.c.) does **not** fit — the private-side liaison role is best mapped to **2422** (policy administration professionals) or **1219** (business services & administration managers n.e.c.). **NOT FOUND** as a discrete NCO title.
- **What they do:** Walks files through the Municipal Corporation / Development Authority / Town Planning office / Collectorate / Fire Brigade / MSEDCL / Water Dept. Books and attends the site inspections, tracks file movement on the OBPAS portal, collects shortfall letters, pays scrutiny/development/labour-cess challans, chases the Commissioner's signature, maintains the approval register.
- **Deliverables:** Approval tracker; file-movement register; challan receipts; inward/outward acknowledgements; shortfall-compliance letters; the approval set itself (IOD/Commencement Certificate/Plinth Checking/OC) delivered into the project's document vault.
- **Who employs:** Developer in-house (large developers keep a Liaison Dept.); or a liaison consultancy retained per-project; or the PMC.
- **THE GATE:** Liaison is **unlicensed** — this is the key point. A liaison officer may not sign anything. **Who applies:** the owner/promoter. **Who certifies drawings:** the registered Architect / Licensed Engineer / Licensed Surveyor. **Software agent:** may prepare, track, compile and even fill the portal form end-to-end; **cannot** be the credential-holder on an OBPAS architect login (those logins are issued against a Council of Architecture registration — Haryana HSVP is integrating the Architect Login directly with COA from 01.01.2026, https://obpas.hsvphry.org.in/). So: prepare — yes; submit — only under the licensee's own login, with the licensee's knowledge.

### A2. Permit / Approval Manager (titles: "Approvals Head", "DM–Approvals", "Statutory Approvals Manager")
- **NCO-2015:** 1323 (construction managers) family.
- **What they do:** Owns the *approvals critical path* — sequences ~25–60 permissions, owns the dependency map (e.g. AAI height NOC must precede final building plan sanction; EC must precede commencement; fire scheme approval precedes plan sanction; OC needs fire Form-A + lift licence + structural stability certificate), budgets premiums/FSI charges, and runs the condition-compliance register attached to each clearance.
- **Deliverables:** Approvals matrix/Gantt; pre-condition compliance registers per clearance; premium & fee computation; sanctioned drawing set register (revision-controlled); the OC dossier.
- **Who employs:** Developer; occasionally the PMC or the lender's technical agency (as a monitor).
- **THE GATE:** none of their own. They are the *orchestrator* of other people's licences. **Software agent:** this role is the single most automatable in the whole family — dependency graph, deadline tracking, condition registers, document assembly — with zero statutory signature.

### A3. Licensed Building Surveyor / Licensed Engineer / Licensed Supervisor empanelled with the municipal body
**This is the heart of your question, so here are the actual categories a city licenses.** MoHUA's **Model Building Bye-Laws 2016** (https://mohua.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/MBBL.pdf) puts qualification & competence of all professionals in **Appendix "E"**, and requires them to be *registered with the Authority* before they may submit plans. The MBBL category list (as reproduced in state adoptions) is:

| MBBL / state category | Typically licensed to do |
|---|---|
| **Architect** (COA-registered, Architects Act 1972) | Prepare + sign **all** building plans, any height; the only one who may use the title "Architect" |
| **Engineer** (degree, Corporate Member of Institution of Engineers / state register) | Plans for buildings up to a bye-law-specified size/height |
| **Structural Engineer** (PG in structures + experience; registered/empanelled with the ULB) | **Structural design, structural drawings and the Structural Stability / Structural Safety Certificate**; above ~10 m the structural engineer countersigns the plans |
| **Supervisor** (diploma/ITI + experience; Class I / Class II) | Supervise execution; sign the supervision undertaking; plans only for small/low-rise plots |
| **Town Planner** (recognised PG degree/diploma + ITPI membership) | Layout/sub-division & change-of-land-use submissions |
| **Landscape Architect / Urban Designer** | Landscape & urban-design submissions |
| **Geotechnical Engineer** | Soil investigation report (mandatory above stated heights) |
| **Fire Consultant / Licensed Fire Agency** | Fire-scheme drawings + fire compliance certificate |
| **Plumber (licensed)** | Water/drainage connection application + plumbing completion certificate; licensed after examination |
| **Electrician / Licensed Electrical Contractor** | Internal electrical installation + test report |
| **"Licensed Building Surveyor" (LBS)** | Chennai/Tamil Nadu & some ULBs: a distinct licensed class entitled to submit plans within stated limits; **Grade I / Grade II** by qualification |

- **NCO-2015:** Architect **2161**; Civil/Structural Engineer **2142**; Town Planner **2164**; Surveyor **2165**; Construction supervisor **3123**; Civil engineering technician **3112**; Plumber **7126**.
- **What they do:** Prepare and **sign and submit** the sanction set: site plan, layout, floor plans, sections, elevations, area statement/FSI computation, parking statement, services plan, fire scheme, structural design basis report, soil report, plus the statutory undertakings (ownership, supervision, no-deviation, stability).
- **Deliverables:** Drawing set on the ULB portal (DWG/DXF for auto-scrutiny); area/FSI statement; structural design calculations; Supervision Undertaking; **Structural Stability Certificate**; plinth-level certificate; completion certificate/"as-built" set for OC.
- **Who employs:** The owner/promoter appoints them (and must name them in the application); they are independent professionals, not municipal staff — but they hold a **licence/registration issued by the ULB** which can be suspended for false certification.
- **THE GATE — be exact:**
  - **Who applies:** the owner/promoter (applicant of record).
  - **Who certifies/signs the plans:** the registered **Architect** (any size) or the **Licensed Engineer/Surveyor/Supervisor within the class limits** set by that ULB's bye-laws.
  - **Who certifies structure:** only the **registered Structural Engineer** empanelled with that ULB.
  - **Licence required:** COA registration (architects); state/ULB empanelment (engineer, structural engineer, supervisor, LBS); ITPI/qualification (town planner).
  - **Submission mechanics:** on OBPAS systems only a **registered Architect / Technical Person login** can run auto-scrutiny and file a permit — Odisha SUJOG states it flatly: "Only registered Architect/Technical Persons can do drawing auto scrutiny and apply for building permits" (https://sujog.odisha.gov.in/Deshboard/images/OBPAS%20User%20Manual_Architect%20&%20Technical%20Person.pdf). UPSIDA requires a valid **COA certificate** to register (https://onlineupsida.com/pdf/User_Manual_UPSIDA_OBPAS_Architect.pdf).
  - **Software agent:** may draft drawings, run pre-scrutiny, compute FSI/area, assemble the dossier, pre-fill the form. **May not** sign, may not hold the login, may not certify. In **low-risk/self-certification** tracks (Haryana: "all Low Risk Category cases will be sanctioned under self-certification", https://ulbharyana.gov.in/WebCMS/Start/?Id=1449&DynamicTypeIdView=12605) the *self-certification is still by the owner + the licensed professional*, not by software.

### A4. RERA Compliance Manager
- **NCO-2015:** 1219 / 2411 (if a CA) family. **NOT FOUND** as a distinct title.
- **What they do:** Project registration and its annual renewal; quarterly QPR uploads (building-wise progress, inventory sold/unsold, cost incurred, approvals received); maintains the **designated/separate account** discipline; collects the withdrawal certificates; serves the agreement-for-sale in the prescribed model form; handles complaints before the Authority/Adjudicating Officer; files extension applications; registers the agents.
- **Deliverables:** RERA registration certificate & project page; quarterly progress reports; **Form 1 / Form 2 / Form 3** set for each withdrawal; **Form 5** annual report; annual audited project account; Form-4 (architect's project completion certificate in Maharashtra); allottee-wise ledger.
- **Who employs:** Developer (in-house compliance cell), or outsourced to a CA/CS/law firm.
- **THE GATE — the designated-account withdrawal, the cleanest licence gate in the whole industry:** RERA **s.4(2)(l)(D)** requires 70% of allottee receipts in a separate account in a scheduled bank, withdrawable only in proportion to completion. Under the **Maharashtra RERA Rules, Rule 5**, the promoter must place **three** certificates before the bank: **Form 1 by the Architect** (percentage of physical completion), **Form 2 by the Engineer** (actual cost incurred on work done), **Form 3 by the Chartered Accountant** (proportionate cost and the withdrawable amount) — official forms listed at https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/new-forms (Form 4 = architect's completion certificate; Form 5 = annual report on statement of accounts). MahaRERA Circular No. 39 restates that **"without which the scheduled bank shall not permit any withdrawal"** (https://credaimchi.com/pdf/E-Library/RERA/28-12-2022-RERA-Circular-No-39.pdf).
  - **Who applies (for withdrawal):** the promoter.
  - **Who certifies:** three *different* licensed professionals — Architect (COA), Engineer, and a **practising CA holding a valid ICAI Certificate of Practice** (Form 3; https://www.nagpuricai.org/seminar-presentations/FORMATS-OF-CERTIFICATES-OF-ARCHITECT-ENGINEER-AND-CHARTERED-ACCOUNTANT.pdf).
  - **Plus:** annual audit of project accounts by a practising CA within six months of FY-end (RERA s.4 proviso).
  - **Software agent:** may compute percentage completion from measurement books, build the cost-incurred statement, draft all three forms and the QPR, and compute the withdrawable amount. **Cannot** sign Form 1/2/3, cannot file them with the bank as the certifier, cannot upload QPRs under the promoter's authenticated RERA login without the promoter's authority. Registration itself is by the **promoter** (s.4), not an agent.

### A5. Legal / Title Due-Diligence Lawyer (titles: "Title Advocate", "Search Clerk + Advocate")
- **NCO-2015:** **2611** (lawyers).
- **What they do:** 30-year (often 60-year in Maharashtra practice) title search at the Sub-Registrar; reads 7/12 extract, mutation entries, Property Card, index-II, encumbrance certificate; checks tenancy/ceiling/agricultural-status/ULC, inams/devasthan, minors' interest, HUF, SARFAESI & CERSAI charges, litigation and lis pendens; drafts the **Title Certificate** that goes on the RERA page and into the lender's file.
- **Deliverables:** Search report; **Title Certificate / Legal Opinion** (mandatory annexure for RERA registration); list of title defects & cure plan; draft Development Agreement / Power of Attorney / Sale Deed; **public notice** in newspapers.
- **Who employs:** Developer; the lender/bank (separate panel advocate); the buyer; the landowner.
- **THE GATE:** The **Title Certificate** may be issued only by an **Advocate enrolled with a State Bar Council** (Advocates Act 1961) — banks additionally require a **panel advocate**. A search-clerk may extract the records; only the advocate certifies. **Software agent:** may do the index search, OCR the deeds, build the 30-year chain-of-title table, flag gaps — a genuinely high-value automation. **Cannot** issue the title certificate. Registration of any deed requires physical/biometric presence of the parties before the Sub-Registrar (Registration Act 1908).

### A6. Land Revenue / Land Records Consultant (titles: "Revenue Consultant", "7/12 consultant")
- **NCO-2015:** **NOT FOUND**; nearest 3359/4419.
- **What they do:** Mutation (ferfar) entries; **non-agricultural (NA) permission** / conversion; 7/12 and Property Card corrections; boundary demarcation and measurement (*mojni*) through the Land Records office; ULC/ceiling clearances; Collector NOC; Form 2/Form 7 entries; zone certificate / Development Plan remarks.
- **Deliverables:** Mutated 7/12; NA order; measurement map (k-prat / tippan); zone certificate; DP remark; Collector NOC; stamp-duty & ready-reckoner valuation note.
- **Who employs:** Developer, landowner, PMC.
- **THE GATE:** **Who applies:** owner. **Who certifies/orders:** the **Tahsildar / Sub-Divisional Officer / Collector** (statutory revenue officers) and for measurement the **District Inspector of Land Records / Taluka Inspector of Land Records (a licensed surveyor in some states)**. No private certification. **Software agent:** may prepare applications and read records; the order is a government act and cannot be automated on the sanction side.

### A7. Environmental Clearance Consultant (titles: "EC Consultant", "EIA Consultant")
- **NCO-2015:** **2133** (environmental protection professionals) / **2143** (environmental engineers).
- **What they do:** Screens applicability; prepares **Form 1 + Form 1A + conceptual plan** on PARIVESH; air/water/noise baseline; water balance, rainwater harvesting, STP sizing, solid-waste plan, traffic & parking study, energy conservation chapter, construction-phase dust/C&D-waste management plan; attends the **SEIAA/SEAC** appraisal; then does the six-monthly **compliance reports** and the Consent-to-Establish/Consent-to-Operate under the Water Act 1974 / Air Act 1981 from the State Pollution Control Board.
- **Deliverables:** Form 1/1A; EIA/EMP report where required; EC letter + its condition list; six-monthly compliance reports; CTE/CTO; **C&D Waste Management Rules 2016** plan; tree/landscape plan.
- **Who employs:** Developer; the EIA consultancy itself.
- **THE GATE — and this is the part that is UNSETTLED, flagged as you asked:**
  - **Baseline rule:** Item **8(a)** of the **EIA Notification, 14 Sept 2006** requires **prior environmental clearance for building & construction projects with built-up area ≥20,000 m² and <1,50,000 m²**; item 8(b) covers townships/area development. Appraisal is at **State level (SEIAA on SEAC's recommendation)** for these — i.e. "Category B".
  - **The 2016 dilution:** the **9 Dec 2016** amendment tried to replace EC with building-permission-stage conditions for 20,000–1,50,000 m², i.e. hand the job to the local body. The **NGT quashed it on 8 Dec 2017**, calling it "a ploy to circumvent" the 2006 notification and invoking non-regression (https://www.livelaw.in/ngt-quashes-centres-notification-diluting-provisions-environmental-clearance-construction-activities-read-judgment ; https://www.mondaq.com/india/Real-Estate-and-Construction/720470/National-Green-Tribunal-Order-On-Environmental-Clearances-For-Real-Estate-Projects).
  - **Current position:** In **Vanashakti v. Union of India, W.P.(C) No. 166 of 2025, decided 5 Aug 2025** (CJI B.R. Gavai, J. K. Vinod Chandran) the Supreme Court **struck down Note 1 to Clause 8(a)** of the amended schedule, which had exempted industrial sheds, schools, colleges and hostels from prior EC, while **upholding the 29 Jan 2025 notification and the accompanying Office Memorandum** otherwise — holding that large building/construction projects cannot be carved out of the EIA regime whatever their purpose (https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/supreme-court-strikes-down-centres-exemption-for-educational-buildings-from-environmental-clearance-299977 ; https://zeus.firm.in/judgment-alert-vanashakti-vs-union-of-india-writ-petition-c-no-166-of-2025/ ; https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/sc-strikes-down-educational-buildings-eia-exemption-notification-125080501664_1.html).
  - **UNSETTLED / NOT FOUND:** (i) I could **not** confirm whether the MoEFCC's appeal against the 8 Dec 2017 NGT order was ever stayed or finally decided — **treat the appeal status as unknown**; (ii) sources conflict on whether the 29 Jan 2025 notification's exemption ran "up to 1,50,000 m²" (Lexology) or only to the Note-1 categories — **verify against the gazette and the judgment PDF**; (iii) a **draft notification to amend item 8** (built-up area definition, thresholds) was out for consultation and I could not confirm it has been finalised (https://www.civis.vote/consultations/1059). Also separately unsettled: the ex-post-facto/retrospective-EC line of cases. **Do not treat any building-EC threshold as safely settled without a current gazette check.**
  - **Who applies:** the project proponent (promoter). **Who certifies:** no private licence certifies an EC — but the application must be made through an **EIA consultant organisation accredited by QCI/NABET** for Category A/B consultancy work, and the EC itself is **granted by SEIAA/MoEFCC**. **Software agent:** may prepare Form 1/1A, water balance, waste plans, compliance reports in full. **Cannot** be the accredited consultant and cannot file as proponent on PARIVESH.

### A8. Fire NOC Consultant / Licensed Fire Agency
- **NCO-2015:** 2149 (engineering professionals n.e.c.) for the fire engineer; **5411** firefighters is the service side, not this.
- **What they do:** Designs the fire scheme to **NBC 2016 Part 4 (Fire & Life Safety)** and the state Fire Act — occupancy classification, travel distance, exits/staircase widths, refuge areas, fire lift, pressurisation, compartmentation, static/underground tank + terrace tank capacities, wet riser/down-comer, sprinklers, hydrants, detection & alarm, PA, fire-lift lobby, fire-check floors for high-rise; obtains **provisional fire NOC at plan stage** and the **final NOC / compliance certificate at occupancy**; then runs the periodic re-certification.
- **Deliverables:** Fire scheme drawings; hydraulic calculations; provisional NOC; installation test reports; **Form "A" compliance certificate**; **Form "B"** half-yearly maintenance certificate; fire-drill records; fire-safety-officer appointment.
- **Who employs:** Developer at project stage; the society/owner/occupier thereafter.
- **THE GATE (Maharashtra as the worked example — Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act, 2006 + Rules 2009):** only a **"Licensed Agency"** licensed by the **Director, Maharashtra Fire Services** may carry out fire prevention and life safety work and may issue the certificates; the **compliance certificate is in Form "A"** and the **half-yearly maintenance certificate (January and July) is in Form "B"**, issued by that Licensed Agency (Act: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/16067/1/the_maharashtra_fire_prevention_and_life_safety_measures_act.pdf ; Rules: https://maharashtrahousingandbuildinglaws.com/the-maharashtra-fire-prevention-and-life-safety-measures-rules-2009/). The **NOC is granted by the Chief Fire Officer / Director of Fire Services**, and is a **precondition to the Occupancy Certificate** for buildings above 15 m and for specified occupancies.
  - **Who applies:** owner/occupier/promoter. **Who certifies:** the **Licensed Agency** (private, licensed) for Form A/B; the **Fire Officer** grants the NOC. **Software agent:** may do the full hydraulic and egress computation, generate the scheme and pre-fill Forms A/B. **Cannot** sign Form A/B — the licence is personal/agency-specific and the signature is the enforcement hook.

### A9. Utility Liaison — Electricity (sanctioned load, substation, HT connection)
- **NCO-2015:** **2151** (electrical engineers) / **7412** (electrical mechanics & fitters) for the contractor's men.
- **What they do:** Load calculation and diversity; applies for **sanction of load / HT or LT connection** under **Electricity Act 2003 s.43** (licensee's universal duty to supply); negotiates the distribution-transformer/substation space in the building (a bye-law requirement in most ULBs); gets the internal installation tested; arranges the **Electrical Inspector's approval before energisation** for HV/EHV installations under the **CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations, 2010**; DG set approval; meter commissioning and the common-area vs flat-wise metering plan.
- **Deliverables:** Load sheet & single-line diagram; application + estimate/"demand note" payment; substation layout approval; **Electrical Contractor's completion & test report**; **Electrical Inspector's approval/energisation permission**; sanction letter; meter installation report.
- **Who employs:** Developer; MEP consultant; the electrical contractor.
- **THE GATE:** **Who applies:** the owner/promoter (consumer of record). **Who certifies the installation:** a **Licensed Electrical Contractor + licensed Electrical Supervisor/Wireman** holding a licence from the **State Electrical Licensing Board** — in most states an installation drawing and completion certificate signed by the licensed contractor is mandatory. **Who approves energisation:** the **Electrical Inspector** (a statutory officer). **Software agent:** may compute load, draft the SLD, prepare the application and the test-report template. **Cannot** sign the contractor's completion certificate; cannot obtain energisation.

### A10. Utility Liaison — Water Supply, Drainage & Sewerage
- **NCO-2015:** **7126** (plumbers) for the licensed plumber; 2142 for the public-health engineer.
- **What they do:** Water-connection application sized to the ULB's per-capita norm; ferrule/tap size; drainage connection to the municipal sewer; septic-tank/STP approval where no sewer exists; rainwater-harvesting and dual-plumbing compliance; the **drainage completion certificate** needed for OC.
- **Deliverables:** Plumbing & drainage layout; water-demand calculation; connection application + deposit; **licensed plumber's drainage/plumbing completion certificate**; RWH completion certificate; STP commissioning report; water-tax assessment.
- **Who employs:** Developer; plumbing contractor; the society later.
- **THE GATE:** Most ULBs will accept a water/drainage connection application **only through a Licensed Plumber registered with that ULB** (MBBL Appendix E treats the plumber as a licensed category, licensed after examination), and the **drainage completion certificate for OC must be signed by that licensed plumber**; the **Hydraulic Engineer / Executive Engineer (Water Works)** sanctions. **Software agent:** prepare yes; sign/submit no.

### A11. Heritage Clearance Specialist (Heritage Conservation Committee / ASI / NMA)
- **NCO-2015:** 2161 (conservation architect, within architects) — **NOT FOUND** as a distinct code.
- **What they do:** Two distinct regimes. (i) **Centrally protected monuments — AMASR Act 1958 ss.20A/20B/20C:** construction is **prohibited within 100 m** of the protected limits and **regulated for a further 200 m**; permission in the regulated area is applied for to the **Competent Authority** and considered by the **National Monuments Authority** (upheld as constitutionally valid by the Karnataka HC, https://lawchakra.in/high-court/karnataka-high-court/karnataka-hc-amasr-act/ ; general framework: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Monuments_and_Archaeological_Sites_and_Remains_Act,_1958). **UNSETTLED/inconsistent sources:** whether the 200 m regulated belt runs *from the monument* or *beyond the 100 m prohibited belt* — sources conflict; one gives 300 m. Verify on the NMA site. A Parliamentary committee has recommended a review of AMASR including dropping permissions for minor works (https://prsindia.org/policy/report-summaries/creation-of-regulatory-framework-for-protection-of-historical-sites-and-monuments) — pending, so treat as in flux. (ii) **State/ULB heritage lists & precincts** (e.g. Mumbai's Heritage Regulations, Grade I/II/III): approval of the **Heritage Conservation Committee** before any work, including on an adjoining plot in a precinct.
- **Deliverables:** Heritage Impact Assessment; conservation plan; elevation/material/signage proposals; NMA/Competent Authority permission; HCC recommendation and the Commissioner's consequent approval.
- **THE GATE:** **Who applies:** owner. **Who certifies/recommends:** the **NMA/Competent Authority** (AMASR) and the **Heritage Conservation Committee** (state); the drawings are signed by the **registered architect**, in practice a **conservation architect**. **Software agent:** may prepare the HIA and documentation; cannot submit or recommend.

### A12. Airport Height Clearance Specialist (AAI NOC)
- **NCO-2015:** 2165 (surveyors) for the survey input; otherwise **NOT FOUND**.
- **What they do:** Establishes site coordinates and **site elevation in AMSL** by DGPS survey; checks the **Colour Coded Zoning Map (CCZM)** for the airport; files on **NOCAS** (now NOCAS 2) for the **height NOC** under the **Ministry of Civil Aviation's Height Restrictions for Safeguarding of Aircraft Operations Rules, 2015 (GSR 751(E))**; manages crane/temporary-structure heights during construction too.
- **Deliverables:** DGPS/topographic survey with lat-long and AMSL; CCZM extract; NOCAS application; **AAI height NOC** stating the permissible top elevation; obstacle-limitation compliance note; aviation warning-light/paint specification.
- **Who employs:** Developer; survey firm.
- **THE GATE:** **Who applies:** the owner/proponent on NOCAS. **The decisive technical input:** site coordinates + AMSL elevation, in practice certified by a **licensed/competent surveyor** (AAI requires the applicant to warrant the survey data; I found **no** rule naming a specific licensed professional who must sign — **NOT FOUND**, verify in the current NOCAS applicant guidelines, https://nocas2.aai.aero/nocas/AAI_Links/Guidelines.pdf). Where the proposed top elevation is **below the CCZM permitted top elevation**, the NOC is **auto-settled** by the system (https://nocas.aai.aero/nocas/) — CCZMs have been published for ~88 airports. **Software agent:** this is the most automatable approval in the list — read CCZM, compare AMSL, file. Still filed under the applicant's own NOCAS account, and wrong survey data voids the NOC.

### A13. Coastal Clearance Specialist (CRZ)
- **NCO-2015:** 2133 / 2143.
- **What they do:** CRZ demarcation on a 1:4000 map by an **MoEFCC-authorised agency**; classification (CRZ-I/II/III/IV and the NDZ); hazard-line overlay; files for **CRZ clearance** under the **CRZ Notification 2019**.
- **Deliverables:** CRZ demarcation map from an authorised agency; CRZ clearance application; **SCZMA recommendation**; CRZ clearance letter; condition-compliance reports.
- **THE GATE:** **Who applies:** proponent. **Who demarcates:** only an **MoEFCC-authorised CRZ mapping agency** (e.g. NCSCM/authorised institutions) — a private consultant's map is not accepted. **Who recommends:** the **State Coastal Zone Management Authority**; **who grants:** the State/MoEFCC depending on CRZ category (CRZ-I & IV broadly central, CRZ-II & III broadly state under the 2019 notification — **verify the current delegation**, I did not fetch the 2019 notification text in this session: **NOT VERIFIED**). **Software agent:** prepare only.

### A14. Tree / Environment-Local Permission Specialist
- **NCO-2015:** **NOT FOUND**; 2132/6113 (horticulture) adjacent.
- **What they do:** Tree census/enumeration on the plot; application to the **Tree Authority** for felling/transplantation; compensatory plantation undertaking and tree-bank/deposit; the **tree NOC that most ULBs make a precondition to plan sanction or to commencement**.
- **Deliverables:** Tree census report with girth/species/photographs; Tree Authority application; felling/transplantation permission; compensatory plantation plan and completion report; deposit receipt.
- **THE GATE:** Under the **Maharashtra (Urban Areas) Protection and Preservation of Trees Act, 1975** permission to fell is granted by the **Tree Authority** of the local body. **I could not retrieve the Act text or the current Tree Authority procedure in this session — NOT VERIFIED; the statute name and the Tree-Authority-grants-permission structure is well established in practice, but verify the sections and the compensatory-plantation ratio against indiacode and the ULB's circulars.** **Who applies:** owner. **Who certifies:** the Tree Officer/Tree Authority; the enumeration is usually by a horticulturist/arborist but I found **no** statutory licensing of that person. **Software agent:** prepare only.

# (B) DEVELOPER AND CLIENT-SIDE JOBS

### B1. Land Acquisition Manager (titles: "Land Head", "AGM–Land")
- **NCO-2015:** 1323 / 1219 family. **NOT FOUND** as a distinct title.
- **What they do:** Sourcing and aggregation; price discovery against the ready-reckoner; dealing with multiple co-owners/heirs, tenants and agriculturists; structuring as outright purchase / Development Agreement / JDA / revenue-share / redevelopment with a society; MoU → token → **Agreement to Sell** → due diligence → **Sale Deed / DA + irrevocable PoA**; land aggregation across survey numbers; stamp duty and registration.
- **Deliverables:** Term sheet; MoU; Agreement to Sell; Development Agreement; **irrevocable Power of Attorney**; consent letters of co-owners/tenants; registered Sale Deed; stamp-duty & registration computation; land-cost file for the feasibility model.
- **Who employs:** Developer; land-aggregator firms; the landowner's side.
- **THE GATE:** Deeds are **drafted by anyone, certified-as-to-title only by an advocate (A5), stamped per the state Stamp Act, and registered only before the Sub-Registrar with the parties present** (Registration Act 1908, s.32 — execution/presentation by party or a duly authorised PoA holder). **Software agent:** may draft, compute stamp duty, run the due-diligence checklist. **Cannot** appear before the Sub-Registrar, cannot be a PoA holder in practice (a PoA must be a person).

### B2. Feasibility Analyst / Development Feasibility (titles: "Business Development Analyst", "Feasibility & Underwriting")
- **NCO-2015:** **2413**/2412 (financial analysts/advisers) family.
- **What they do:** Buildable-area study from the DCR/UDCPR (FSI, premium FSI, TDR, fungible/ancillary area, setbacks, parking, open space), saleable-area and efficiency assumptions; absorption and price benchmarking; the development cash-flow (land, premiums, construction, marketing, finance, approvals, contingency), IRR/XIRR, NPV, peak-funding, sensitivity and break-even; the go/no-go note.
- **Deliverables:** Feasibility model (Excel); buildable-area statement; price & absorption benchmark; IRR/NPV/peak-funding deck; risk register; investment committee note.
- **THE GATE:** **None.** No statutory sign-off. (A **valuation** for a lender or for IND-AS/Companies Act purposes is different — that must be by an **IBBI-registered valuer** under the Companies (Registered Valuers and Valuation) Rules 2017, **NOT VERIFIED here**.) **Software agent:** fully automatable, including the buildable-area read from the DCR — this and A2 are the two highest-leverage automation targets on the developer side.

### B3. Development Manager (DM) (titles: "Project Development Head", third-party "DM" firms)
- **NCO-2015:** **1323** (construction managers).
- **What they do:** Owner's single throat-to-choke from land to handover: appoints and coordinates architect, structural, MEP, landscape, façade, PMC, contractors; owns the design-freeze, the BOQ/tender strategy, the approvals plan (A2), the cost plan and the master schedule; runs the change-control; signs off milestone payments.
- **Deliverables:** Project brief & design-freeze pack; consultant appointment letters; tender & award recommendations; master schedule & cost plan; monthly owner's report; change orders; handover dossier.
- **THE GATE:** none personally. A DM **cannot** certify drawings, structure, fire or RERA forms — only commission the licensed people who can. **Software agent:** may run the schedule/cost/change registers and draft everything; cannot hold the contractual authority to bind the owner.

### B4. Project Finance Manager (titles: "Head–Project Finance", "CFO–Real Estate")
- **NCO-2015:** **1211** (finance managers) / 2411 (accountants).
- **What they do:** Construction-finance/LRD/NCD raise; term-sheet negotiation; mortgage creation and charge registration (CERSAI/ROC CHG-1); the **lender's technical & legal due diligence**; the **RERA designated account** architecture (70% account + the 30% account) and the tri-party bank arrangement; drawdown requests against the Form 1/2/3 set; escrow waterfall; interest-during-construction; NOC for each buyer's registration and the final **no-dues/lender NOC** for conveyance.
- **Deliverables:** Term sheet & sanction letter; mortgage deed; charge filings; drawdown requests with the architect/engineer/CA certificates; escrow waterfall statement; buyer-wise lender NOCs; **annual audited project accounts (CA)**.
- **THE GATE:** The **drawdown/withdrawal gate is the RERA Form 1/2/3 trio (A4)** — architect, engineer, **practising CA with ICAI COP**; the bank cannot release without them. Charge creation is filed by the company but the **ROC/CERSAI filing is digitally signed** by a director/authorised signatory (and often certified by a **practising CS/CA**). **Software agent:** may prepare drawdown packs and reconcile the escrow; **cannot** sign the certificates or the DSC-bearing filings.

### B5. Sales Head / CRM Head for a Project
- **NCO-2015:** **1221** (sales and marketing managers); CRM executives **4222**/3339; real-estate agents **3334**.
- **What they do:** Pricing and inventory release strategy; channel-partner network; booking → allotment letter → **Agreement for Sale** → registration → demand letters as per the construction-linked plan → home-loan coordination → **possession**; maintains the allottee ledger; handles cancellations/transfers/nominations; the RERA-mandated disclosure of carpet area and the sanctioned plan at booking.
- **Deliverables:** Booking form & cost sheet; allotment letter; **Agreement for Sale in the state's prescribed model form** (RERA s.13 — no more than 10% advance without a registered agreement); demand letters; receipts & allottee ledger; **possession letter**; NOC set for registration; the carpet-area statement.
- **THE GATE:** **RERA s.13:** a promoter may not take more than **10% of the cost** as advance without a **registered agreement for sale**. The **agreement is executed by the promoter and the allottee and registered before the Sub-Registrar**. Any person selling on the promoter's behalf must be a **RERA-registered real estate agent** (RERA s.9) — this is a real licence gate, often ignored. **Software agent:** may generate cost sheets, agreements, demands, ledgers; **cannot** be a registered agent (registration is of a person/entity), cannot register the agreement.

### B6. Marketing Manager (project marketing)
- **NCO-2015:** **1221** / **2431** (advertising & marketing professionals).
- **What they do:** Brand, launch, digital/performance, site branding, sample flat, broker meets, exhibitions.
- **Deliverables:** Launch plan; creatives; media plan; lead reports; **every advertisement carrying the RERA registration number and the project website QR** (RERA s.11(2) obliges the promoter to put the registration number on all advertisements and prospectuses).
- **THE GATE:** advertising without the RERA registration number is itself an offence; the **promoter**, not the agency, is liable. **Software agent:** may produce the material; the compliance stamp is a promoter obligation.

### B7. Customer Handover / Possession Manager
- **NCO-2015:** **NOT FOUND**; 1219/4222 adjacent.
- **What they do:** Pre-possession snag walk with the buyer, snag-list closure, final demand & no-dues, handover kit, meter transfer, maintenance-deposit and advance-maintenance collection, society share application, fit-out rules and fit-out deposit, keys.
- **Deliverables:** **Possession letter**; snag list + closure sign-off; final statement/no-dues; **Occupancy Certificate copy to each allottee**; handover kit (warranties, as-built MEP drawings, O&M manuals, equipment list, AMC list, meter details); maintenance-deposit receipt; fit-out guidelines.
- **THE GATE:** Possession may only be offered **after the Occupancy Certificate** — the OC is issued by the **ULB** on the strength of the licensed architect's/engineer's completion certificate + fire Form-A + lift licence + structural stability certificate + drainage/water completion. RERA s.11(4) and s.19 give the allottee the right to the OC/completion certificate. **Software agent:** may run the whole snag/possession workflow; the OC cannot be obtained by it.

### B8. Legal Documentation Manager (Agreement for Sale, Conveyance)
- **NCO-2015:** **2611** (lawyers) / 2619.
- **What they do:** Maintains the model Agreement for Sale, allotment letter, nomination/transfer, mortgage NOC, deed of apartment/declaration (where the Apartment Ownership Act route is used), **conveyance deed** to the society/association, deed of rectification; stamp-duty and adjudication; e-registration.
- **Deliverables:** Registered Agreement for Sale; Deed of Declaration/Deed of Apartment; **registered Conveyance Deed**; indemnities; stamp-duty adjudication orders; registration receipts & index-II.
- **THE GATE — the conveyance gate:** **RERA s.17** requires the promoter to **execute a registered conveyance deed** in favour of the allottee together with the **undivided proportionate title in the common areas to the association of allottees**, within the state-specified period (Kerala RERA's hosted Act text: https://reraonline.kerala.gov.in/PDF/RERA%20Act,%202016.pdf ; UP RERA copy: https://up-rera.in/pdf/reraact.pdf). Maharashtra adds **MOFA 1963** and, on failure, **deemed conveyance** before the **District Deputy Registrar / Registrar of Co-operative Societies** as competent authority (https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15816/1/maharashtra_ownership_flats.pdf). **UNSETTLED in the secondary sources:** the deadline — some read **Maharashtra RERA Rule 9(2)** as *three months from OC or from full payment by 51% of allottees, whichever earlier* (https://www.sansalegal.com/post/how-to-apply-for-deemed-conveyance-of-a-housing-society-flat-in-maharashtra-process-and-documents); others cite **four months from society formation** under MOFA s.11. **Verify against the current MahaRERA Rules and MOFA — I could not settle it.**
  - **Who applies/executes:** promoter + allottee/association. **Who certifies:** no private certifier; the **Sub-Registrar** registers; the advocate drafts and the **title certificate** (A5) underpins it. **Software agent:** may draft and compute stamp duty; cannot execute or register.

### B9. Society Formation / Handover Manager
- **NCO-2015:** **NOT FOUND**; 1219 adjacent.
- **What they do:** Forms the legal entity of the buyers — **co-operative housing society** (state Co-operative Societies Act + Rules), or a **condominium/apartment association** (state Apartment Ownership Act), or a **company**; the Chief Promoter process, name reservation, Form A application with by-laws, share capital and entrance fee, registration; then the **handover**: common-area accounts, sinking fund and maintenance deposit transfer, as-built drawings, warranties, staff, AMCs, and finally conveyance (B8).
- **Deliverables:** Chief Promoter resolution; name-reservation letter; **Form A** registration application + model by-laws; **Society Registration Certificate**; share certificates; first AGM/managing committee election; handover schedule (documents, deposits, assets, staff, AMCs); defect-liability register handed to the society.
- **THE GATE:** **RERA s.11(4)(e)** obliges the promoter to **enable the formation of an association/society/co-operative society of allottees** within the period specified by state rules (the sub-clause text itself I could **NOT** retrieve verbatim — **NOT VERIFIED**; the duty is well established and mirrored in state rules). Maharashtra's rule is commonly reported as **within three months of 51% of allottees booking** (https://credaimchi.com/... / secondary blog http://sahakarsutra.blogspot.com/2018/08/builder-bound-to-form-society-if-51.html — **secondary, verify**). **Who applies:** the **Chief Promoter** (an allottee) with the promoter's cooperation. **Who registers:** the **Registrar/Deputy Registrar of Co-operative Societies** (or the Competent Authority under the Apartment Ownership Act). **Who certifies:** no private licence, though a **practising CA/CS or an "authorised society consultant"** usually prepares the file; society **accounts must be audited by a statutory auditor on the state's panel** post-registration. **Software agent:** may prepare the whole file and the handover register; cannot register.

### B10. Asset Manager (titles: "Asset Management – Real Estate", for rent-yielding/commercial assets)
- **NCO-2015:** **3334** (real-estate agents and property managers) / 1219 for the manager grade.
- **What they do:** For a retained asset: NOI and cap-rate management, rent roll, leasing strategy, tenant mix, lease renewals and escalations, CAM reconciliation, capex plan, repositioning, valuations, exit/REIT readiness.
- **Deliverables:** Rent roll; NOI and budget-vs-actual; CAM reconciliation statement; lease abstracts; capex plan; valuation pack; investment memorandum.
- **THE GATE:** none, except: **valuation for REIT/financial-statement purposes** must be by a **registered valuer** (SEBI REIT Regulations require an independent valuer; Companies Act requires an **IBBI-registered valuer**) — **NOT VERIFIED in this session**. **Software agent:** highly automatable; cannot sign a valuation.

### B11. Investor Relations (Real Estate)
- **NCO-2015:** 2412/1211 family. **NOT FOUND** as a distinct title.
- **What they do:** LP/AIF/NCD-holder reporting, drawdown and distribution notices, waterfall computation, quarterly asset reports, compliance with the fund documents, data room.
- **Deliverables:** Quarterly investor report; NAV/waterfall statement; drawdown & distribution notices; compliance certificates; data room.
- **THE GATE:** none in building law. If the vehicle is a **SEBI-registered AIF/REIT**, the statutory certificates are the **trustee's / compliance officer's / statutory auditor's**, and offer documents require **merchant-banker** involvement (SEBI regime, **NOT VERIFIED here**). **Software agent:** prepare yes; the compliance officer's certification no.

# (C) HANDOVER, FACILITIES AND OPERATIONS JOBS

### C1. Facilities Manager (FM) / Head–Facilities
- **NCO-2015:** **1219** (business services & administration managers n.e.c.) family — **the ISCO/NCO structure has no dedicated FM code; NOT FOUND** as a discrete title.
- **What they do:** Runs the built asset after handover: hard services (MEP, lifts, DG, HVAC, fire, pumps, STP, WTP), soft services (housekeeping, security, landscape, pest, waste), helpdesk, PPM calendar, vendor/AMC management, budget and the maintenance-charge recovery, statutory compliance calendar, BCP/emergency plan, tenant/resident relations.
- **Deliverables:** FM transition & handover acceptance report; **asset register**; **PPM (planned preventive maintenance) calendar**; SLA/KPI dashboard; statutory compliance register; annual FM budget; MIS; incident reports; vendor AMC register.
- **Who employs:** Society/apartment association; the developer's FM arm; an IFM company (JLL/CBRE/Knight Frank/Quess/BVG etc.); the corporate occupier.
- **THE GATE:** **No licence to be an FM.** But the FM is the **custodian of other people's statutory certificates** and is contractually/criminally exposed when they lapse. **ISO 41001:2018** (Facility management — management systems) is a **voluntary** certification audited by an **accredited certification body**, not a statutory licence, and certifies the *organisation*, not the person. **Software agent:** can run the entire compliance calendar, PPM scheduling, helpdesk, and vendor management — the strongest FM automation case; **cannot** hold any of the certificates in C8–C13.

### C2. O&M Manager (titles: "Operations & Maintenance Manager", "Technical Services Manager")
- **NCO-2015:** 1219 / **2142**–**2144** if engineering-graded.
- **What they do:** Owns the engineering plant: operating philosophy, shift rosters for technicians, log sheets, breakdown vs preventive balance, energy and water consumption, spares, OEM escalation, shutdown planning, obsolescence/replacement plan.
- **Deliverables:** O&M manuals (from handover); operating logs; breakdown analysis & MTTR/MTBF; spares inventory; shutdown plan; capex replacement plan; utility consumption reports.
- **THE GATE:** none personally; but every statutory item under them has its own licensed signer (electrical supervisor's licence for HT operation in many states, lift licence, fire Form B, boiler/pressure-vessel certificate if applicable). **Software agent:** scheduling and analytics yes; licensed operation of HT switchgear no.

### C3. MEP Maintenance In-charge (titles: "MEP In-charge", "Chief Engineer")
- **NCO-2015:** **3113** (electrical engineering technicians) / **3115** (mechanical engineering technicians) / **2151**/**2144** at engineer grade; HVAC technicians **7127**; electricians **7412**.
- **What they do:** HT/LT panels, transformer, DG, UPS, lifts interface, HVAC chillers/VRF/AHUs, pumps and plumbing, fire pumps and panels, BMS, lighting, earthing and lightning protection, thermography, insulation-resistance testing.
- **Deliverables:** Electrical test reports (IR, earth resistance, relay settings, thermography); HVAC performance/water-balancing reports; pump and fire-pump test records; DG load-bank test; BMS trend reports; **licensed electrical supervisor's log** where required.
- **THE GATE:** Operation and maintenance of **HV installations** requires persons holding the state **Electrical Supervisor / Wireman licence**, and any addition/alteration must be executed by a **Licensed Electrical Contractor** with the **Electrical Inspector's approval** (CEA Safety Regulations 2010 regime). **Software agent:** prepares, schedules, analyses; cannot be a licensed supervisor.

### C4. Housekeeping Manager / Soft Services Manager
- **NCO-2015:** **5151** (cleaning and housekeeping supervisors in offices, hotels and other establishments); cleaners **9112**.
- **What they do:** Manpower deployment and shift planning, cleaning SOPs and frequencies, consumables, machinery (scrubbers, high-rise façade), pest control, landscape, washroom and common-area standards, hygiene audits.
- **Deliverables:** Deployment chart & attendance; cleaning schedule & checklists; consumable consumption; pest-control service records; hygiene audit scores; **statutory labour compliance pack for the contract workforce**.
- **THE GATE:** The real gate here is **labour law, not building law**: the FM/housekeeping contractor needs a **licence under the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act 1970** and the principal employer a **registration**, plus EPF/ESIC/Minimum Wages/PoSH compliance. Pest control uses **CIB&RC-registered pesticides** applied by a licensed operator in many states. **NOT VERIFIED** in this session — verify the CLRA threshold applicable to the site. **Software agent:** rosters, checklists, audit scoring, compliance calendar — yes; the CLRA licence is held by the contractor.

### C5. Security Manager
- **NCO-2015:** security guards **5414**; the manager grade **NOT FOUND** (1219 adjacent).
- **What they do:** Access control, visitor and material movement, CCTV and control room, patrolling, parking, emergency response and evacuation marshalling, incident investigation, guard training.
- **Deliverables:** Security SOP & post orders; guard deployment; visitor/material gate passes; CCTV retention log; incident register; evacuation drill reports.
- **THE GATE:** A private security *agency* must be licensed under the **Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act 2005** by the state **Controlling Authority**, with prescribed guard training and verification. (Well established; **not re-verified in this session**.) **Software agent:** access-control logic, visitor management, patrol verification — yes; the agency licence no.

### C6. Snagging / De-snagging Engineer (titles: "Snag Engineer", "QA/QC–Handover", "Punch-list Engineer")
- **NCO-2015:** **3112** (civil engineering technicians) / 2142.
- **What they do:** Unit-by-unit and common-area inspection against the specification and the sample flat: tiling levels and hollowness, door/window operation and sealing, plumbing pressure and leak tests, electrical point testing and polarity, paint/finish, waterproofing of toilets and terraces, railing heights, fire-door closers; raises, tracks and verifies closure of the snag list before and at possession; runs the de-snagging crews.
- **Deliverables:** **Snag list per unit** (photo-tagged); de-snag closure sign-off; waterproofing & pressure-test records; sample-flat benchmark; handover-readiness report; **joint buyer sign-off at possession**.
- **THE GATE:** none statutory — it is contractual. But the snag process is the evidence base for **RERA s.14(3)** defect claims. **Software agent:** excellent fit (photo-tagged lists, closure workflow, buyer sign-off); no certificate involved.

### C7. Warranty & Defect Liability Manager (titles: "DLP Manager", "Post-possession Service Head")
- **NCO-2015:** 1323/3112 family. **NOT FOUND**.
- **What they do:** Runs the statutory and contractual warranty window: logs allottee complaints, classifies defect vs wear-and-tear vs misuse vs fit-out damage, routes to the responsible contractor under its DLP bank guarantee/retention, closes within the statutory period, maintains the warranty matrix for every system (waterproofing 5–10 yr, lifts, pumps, DG, façade sealant, anti-termite).
- **Deliverables:** Defect register with ageing; warranty matrix; contractor back-charge notes; retention/BG release recommendations; closure certificates to allottees; **the handover of the open-defect list to the society**.
- **THE GATE — the clearest statutory clock in handover:** **RERA s.14(3)** — any **structural defect or any other defect in workmanship, quality or provision of services** brought to the promoter's notice **within five years from the date of handing over possession** must be rectified by the promoter **free of charge**, failing which the allottee is entitled to compensation; and under the proviso to s.11/s.17 the promoter's responsibility for such defects **continues even after the conveyance deed is executed** (Act text: https://reraonline.kerala.gov.in/PDF/RERA%20Act,%202016.pdf ; https://up-rera.in/pdf/reraact.pdf). **Who claims:** the allottee/association. **Who is liable:** the **promoter** — not the FM, not the society. **Who certifies a defect:** no statutory certifier; in disputes the Authority appoints or accepts a **structural engineer's report**; a **structural** defect opinion in practice comes from a **registered structural engineer**. **Software agent:** may run the register, classify, draft notices and track the 5-year clock per unit — valuable, and may not certify a structural defect.

### C8. Asset & Space Manager
- **NCO-2015:** **3334** family; 2164 for space planning at the planner grade.
- **What they do:** Keeps the asset register and the space inventory accurate: tagging and barcoding of equipment, criticality classification, lifecycle/condition assessment and replacement reserve; occupancy and density, stacking and churn planning, chargeback of area to departments/tenants, BOMA/IPMS or carpet/built-up reconciliation.
- **Deliverables:** Tagged **asset register** with criticality and residual life; condition assessment; replacement reserve/sinking-fund model; space inventory and stack plans; occupancy & utilisation reports; chargeback statements; as-built/CAD floor plates kept current.
- **THE GATE:** none. (A society's **sinking fund** contribution is, however, mandated by the state co-operative by-laws, so the replacement-reserve number has a legal anchor — **verify the state by-law percentage**, **NOT VERIFIED**.) **Software agent:** fully automatable.

### C9. CAFM / FM Software Administrator (titles: "CAFM Admin", "IWMS Administrator")
- **NCO-2015:** **2522** (systems administrators) / 2511 (systems analysts).
- **What they do:** Configures and runs the CAFM/IWMS (helpdesk, PPM, asset register, meter readings, BMS/IoT integration, mobile checklists, QR-tagged rounds, SLA escalation, reports); data quality; integration with ERP and the access-control/visitor systems; user training.
- **Deliverables:** Configured asset/PPM/SLA masters; dashboards; data-quality reports; integration specs; audit trails; the digital O&M/asset handover repository.
- **THE GATE:** none statutory. **Note the compliance angle:** where the CAFM stores resident personal data, the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023** obligations attach to the society/FM as data fiduciary (**NOT VERIFIED in this session** — DPDP rules' commencement status should be checked). **Software agent:** this role *is* software administration; it is the natural host for an agent, which is why the compliance-certificate boundary above matters.

### C10. Energy Manager / Energy Auditor
- **NCO-2015:** **2149** (engineering professionals n.e.c.) family — **the specific NCO title is NOT FOUND**.
- **What they do:** Metering and energy accounting, baseline and benchmarking (EPI kWh/m²/yr), load and power-factor management, HVAC and lighting optimisation, ECBC/ENS compliance for new work, solar and demand-side measures, M&V of savings, the statutory energy audit and the returns to the State Designated Agency.
- **Deliverables:** Energy accounting and EPI reports; **energy audit report with recommendations and payback**; investment-grade proposals; M&V reports; **statutory Form-1/returns to the State Designated Agency/BEE**; ECBC compliance report for new construction.
- **Who employs:** The occupier/owner that is a **designated consumer**; ESCOs; FM companies; BEE-empanelled audit firms.
- **THE GATE — your specific question, answered precisely:**
  - Under the **Energy Conservation Act 2001, s.14**, the Central Government may require **designated consumers** to **(i) designate or appoint an Energy Manager** and **(ii) get a mandatory energy audit conducted by an accredited energy auditor**. BEE's own notice states designated consumers must designate or appoint an Energy Manager under clause 14(l), and that the **qualification is passing the National Level Certification Examination to be a Certified Energy Manager** (https://beeindia.gov.in/sites/default/files/KKC16thExam_advertisement.pdf).
  - **So: is a BEE Certified Energy Manager mandatory for a designated consumer? YES — for an entity that is a notified designated consumer, appointing a certified Energy Manager is a statutory obligation, not a good practice.** The distinction that matters: the **mandatory audit** must be conducted by an **Accredited Energy Auditor (AEA)** — per the BEE notice, a *Certified* Energy Auditor is **not** authorised to conduct the mandatory statutory audit; accreditation is a further step (same source; vendor sites get this wrong, so do not rely on them).
  - **Buildings specifically:** designated-consumer status is notified **sector by sector** under Schedule/notifications; commercial buildings have been brought in as a designated-consumer category with a **connected-load / energy-consumption threshold**. **I could NOT verify the threshold (the commonly quoted 100 kW connected load / 600 kVA contract demand for commercial buildings) from an authoritative source in this session — treat the threshold as NOT FOUND and check the BEE gazette notification for commercial buildings and your State Designated Agency's circular.** Also note the **Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act 2022** added carbon-credit and ECBC/"Energy Conservation and Sustainable Building Code" machinery — **not verified here**.
  - **Who applies/files:** the **designated consumer** (the occupier). **Who certifies:** the **Accredited Energy Auditor** (statutory audit) and the in-house **Certified Energy Manager** (returns/energy accounting). **Licence:** BEE certification by national examination + accreditation. **Software agent:** may do the entire energy accounting, the analysis, and draft the audit report. **Cannot** be the accredited auditor and cannot sign the statutory audit or the return.

### C11. Building Safety / Structural Stability Certifier
- **NCO-2015:** **2142** (civil engineers) — structural engineer sub-family.
- **What they do:** Visual and NDT-based condition survey (rebound hammer, UPV, carbonation, cover meter, half-cell, core tests), chloride and corrosion assessment, load and stability assessment, repair/retrofit design, and the **statutory structural stability certificate**; also the certificate needed at OC stage for new buildings, and pre-redevelopment dilapidation (C1/C2A/C2B/C3 in Mumbai practice).
- **Deliverables:** Structural audit report with NDT results; repair/strengthening specification and BOQ; **Structural Stability Certificate** to the Commissioner; dilapidation/category report; post-repair certificate.
- **THE GATE — exact, with a sourced example:** Under **s.353B of the Mumbai Municipal Corporation Act 1888** (inserted by a 2009 amendment), the **owner or occupier** of a building that has passed **30 years** (counted from the earliest of the completion certificate, the s.353A occupation permission, or physical occupation of at least half the built-up area) **must have it examined by a structural engineer registered with the Corporation**, and the resulting **Structures Stability Certificate** is submitted to the **Commissioner** — the first within one year of the 30-year mark and thereafter **every ten years** or earlier if the Commissioner directs; failure to complete the indicated repairs within six months is punishable under s.471 (text: https://lextechsuite.com/Mumbai-Municipal-Corporation-Act-1888-SECTION-353-B-Structures-Stability-Certificate-1 ; MCGM's public notice on compulsory structural audit: https://portal.mcgm.gov.in/irj/go/km/docs/documents/MCGM%20Department%20List/Public%20Relation%20Officer/Press%20Release/Public_Notice_compulsory%20structural%20audit%20of%20private%20buildings.pdf). **Commercial sites claiming "every three years" are wrong on the statute — flagged.** Other states/ULBs have their own cycles (commonly 15–30 yr first audit then 3–5 yearly) — **verify locally.**
  - **Who applies/is liable:** the **owner or occupier** (post-handover: the **society**). **Who certifies:** **only a structural engineer on that Corporation's register/panel.** **Software agent:** may compile the survey data, process NDT results, draft the report and track the 30-year/10-year clock; **cannot** sign the Structures Stability Certificate, and a certificate from an unregistered engineer is void.

### C12. Fire Audit Coordinator / Fire Safety Officer
- **NCO-2015:** **5411** (firefighters) for the service grade; the building Fire Safety Officer **NOT FOUND** (2149 adjacent).
- **What they do:** Keeps the fire system live and certified: weekly/monthly pump, hydrant, sprinkler, detector and alarm testing; hydrant pressure and jet tests; extinguisher refilling cycle; staircase pressurisation and fire-door integrity; evacuation drills and marshal training; refuge area kept clear; fire-system AMC; and the **periodic statutory fire certification**.
- **Deliverables:** Fire-system test logs; extinguisher/hydrant/sprinkler service records; **Form "B" half-yearly compliance certificate (January and July) from a Licensed Agency**; drill reports; fire-NOC renewal file; deficiency-closure report.
- **THE GATE:** Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act 2006 + Rules 2009: **Form "A"** compliance certificate and **Form "B"** half-yearly maintenance certificate **may be issued only by a "Licensed Agency" licensed by the Director of Maharashtra Fire Services**, and must be **filed with the Chief Fire Officer/Fire Services**; after handover the obligation sits on the **owner/occupier — i.e. the society — not the builder** (Act: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/16067/1/the_maharashtra_fire_prevention_and_life_safety_measures_act.pdf ; Rules: https://maharashtrahousingandbuildinglaws.com/the-maharashtra-fire-prevention-and-life-safety-measures-rules-2009/ ; a separate Form A is required for each building). **Software agent:** may run the test calendar, logs and the Form-B pre-fill; **cannot** sign Form A/B. **Note:** sources disagree on whether Form B goes to the Chief Fire Officer or the state Fire Services — **verify locally.**

### C13. Lift / Escalator AMC Coordinator
- **NCO-2015:** lift mechanics **7233** (machinery mechanics & repairers) family; the coordinator **NOT FOUND**.
- **What they do:** Lift AMC (comprehensive vs semi), OEM call-outs, entrapment response and rescue procedure, ARD/intercom, annual load and safety-gear tests, rope and brake inspection, machine-room housekeeping, **licence renewal**, escalator comb/handrail safety.
- **Deliverables:** AMC agreement and service visit logs; entrapment/incident register; annual test records; **permission to erect** (new installation) and the **operating licence/licence renewal** from the Inspector; modernisation proposals.
- **THE GATE (Maharashtra Lifts, Escalators and Moving Walks Act, 2017 as the worked example — lifts are a **state** subject, I found **no** national lift Act):** the owner must obtain **permission to erect** from the **Electrical Inspector (Lifts)** (valid one year); after installation must **notify the Electrical Inspector within one month** and obtain a **working/operating licence** from the **Chief Electrical Inspector**, renewable for up to five years at a time; **any person or firm erecting, maintaining or testing** lifts/escalators needs a **contractor licence from the Chief Electrical Inspector, and must already hold a valid electrical contractor licence** from the state Licensing Board under the Central Electricity Authority regulations; refusal must be in writing with reasons; escalators/moving walks installed before the Act had six months to apply (https://indiankanoon.org/doc/103980396/ ; https://www.ceimah.in/uploads/cie/gallery/media/The%20Maharashtra%20Lifts,%20Escalators%20and%20Moving%20Walks%20Act,%202017.pdf ; https://elevatorworld.com/article/maharashtra-lifts-escalators-and-moving-walks-act-2017-an-overview/). Older states run on the **Bombay Lifts Act 1939 / state Lifts Acts** with the same shape.
  - **Who applies:** the **owner** (society post-handover). **Who certifies/does the work:** only a **licensed lift contractor**. **Who licenses the lift:** the **Chief Electrical Inspector**. **Software agent:** may track licences, AMC, tests and renewals; cannot erect, test or license.

### C14. STP / WTP Operator (titles: "STP Operator", "Plant Operator")
- **NCO-2015:** **3132** (incinerator and water-treatment-plant operators) family.
- **What they do:** Runs the sewage treatment plant and water treatment/softening/RO: screening, aeration/MBBR/MBR/SBR cycles, sludge handling, chlorination/UV, dosing, DO/MLSS/SVI control, treated-water reuse to flushing and landscape, logs and lab testing; WTP: filtration, softening, RO recovery, tank cleaning and potability.
- **Deliverables:** Daily operation logs; **treated-effluent test reports against the SPCB/CPCB discharge or reuse standards** (BOD/COD/TSS/pH/faecal coliform); sludge disposal records; chemical consumption; water-balance and reuse report; tank-cleaning and potability certificates.
- **THE GATE:** The building's **Consent to Operate** from the **State Pollution Control Board** (Water Act 1974 s.25/26, Air Act 1981) carries the STP conditions; analysis must be from a **laboratory recognised/NABL-accredited and/or approved under the Water Act (Environmental Laboratory)** for the results to count. Operator certification itself is generally **not** statutorily licensed in most states — **NOT FOUND**; some SPCB consents require a named qualified operator. **Software agent:** may run SCADA logs, trend DO/MLSS, schedule sampling and draft the returns; **cannot** issue a test report (that is the accredited lab) and cannot hold the consent.

### C15. Solar O&M (Rooftop PV) In-charge
- **NCO-2015:** **3113** / **7412** family.
- **What they do:** Module cleaning cycles, string and IV-curve testing, thermography, inverter servicing, earthing and SPD checks, generation monitoring vs PR (performance ratio), **net/gross metering** reconciliation with the DISCOM, warranty claims.
- **Deliverables:** Generation and PR reports; string test and thermography reports; cleaning schedule; inverter service records; **net-metering settlement reconciliation**; warranty claim file; annual electrical safety test report.
- **THE GATE:** Installation and any alteration require a **Licensed Electrical Contractor**, and grid connection requires the **DISCOM's net/gross-metering approval** plus, in many states, the **Electrical Inspector's approval before commissioning** (state net-metering regulations under the Electricity Act 2003; **specific state regulation NOT VERIFIED here**). **Software agent:** monitoring, PR analytics, claim drafting — yes; energisation/metering approval — no.

### C16. Waste Management Operator / Coordinator
- **NCO-2015:** refuse workers **9612**; the coordinator **NOT FOUND** (1219 adjacent).
- **What they do:** Segregation at source (wet/dry/domestic hazardous/sanitary), **on-site composting or bio-methanation for bulk generators**, dry-waste baling and sale to authorised recyclers, e-waste and battery channelling to authorised recyclers, C&D waste during fit-outs, bin infrastructure, and the ULB returns.
- **Deliverables:** Segregation and collection SOP; **bulk-waste-generator registration/return to the ULB** (Solid Waste Management Rules 2016 place specific duties on **bulk waste generators** — segregation at source and in-situ processing of wet waste); composting logs and output; authorised-recycler receipts/manifests for e-waste and hazardous waste; C&D waste disposal challans (C&D Waste Management Rules 2016).
- **THE GATE:** SWM Rules 2016 duties fall on the **bulk waste generator (the society/owner)**; e-waste must go only to an **authorised dismantler/recycler** (E-Waste (Management) Rules); hazardous waste movement needs **Form-manifests** and an **authorised transporter/TSDF**. **Software agent:** may run the registers, the manifests and the returns; **cannot** be the authorised recycler or sign the generator's declaration. (Rules not re-fetched in this session — **NOT VERIFIED**, verify SWM 2016 bulk-generator clauses and the ULB bye-law.)

### C17. Society / Association Manager (titles: "Society Manager", "Society Secretary (paid)", "Association Manager")
- **NCO-2015:** **1219** / **3334** family. **NOT FOUND** as a discrete title.
- **What they do:** Runs the handed-over building's legal entity: maintenance billing and recovery, I-forms and the members' register, share transfers and nomination, notices and minutes for the **managing committee and the AGM**, statutory **audit** and returns to the Registrar, sinking and repair fund accounting, NOCs to members, tenancy/leave-and-licence intimation to the police, redevelopment resolutions, and the compliance calendar for C11/C12/C13.
- **Deliverables:** Maintenance bills and recovery statement; members' register and share certificates; AGM/committee notices and **minutes**; **annual accounts + statutory audit report** filed with the **Registrar of Co-operative Societies**; **Form O/annual returns**; sinking-fund and repair-fund ledgers; the statutory certificate register (fire Form B, lift licence, structural stability, DG/electrical).
- **THE GATE — the governing law:** a handed-over building is governed by (i) the **state Co-operative Societies Act and Rules + the model by-laws** (Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act 1960 + MCS Rules 1961 + the model by-laws for co-operative housing societies), or (ii) the **state Apartment Ownership Act** where a condominium/Deed of Apartment route was used, or (iii) **Companies Act** for a company. Under the co-operative route: **registration by the Registrar/Deputy Registrar**; **statutory audit by an auditor on the Registrar's panel** (the committee appoints from the panel at the AGM); annual returns to the Registrar; elections conducted under the state's **Co-operative Election Authority** rules for larger societies; disputes to the **Co-operative Court / Registrar**. **MOFA 1963** continues to govern the promoter's society-formation and conveyance duties in Maharashtra alongside RERA, with **deemed conveyance** before the **competent authority (District Deputy Registrar / Registrar of Co-operative Societies)** (https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15816/1/maharashtra_ownership_flats.pdf). **I could not verify the specific section numbers of MCS Act/Rules or the model by-law clauses in this session — NOT VERIFIED; the structure above is well established but cite the state Act before relying on clause numbers.**
  - **Who applies/files:** the **society through its Secretary/Chairman** (office-bearers are elected members, not employees — a paid manager cannot sign for the society unless authorised by resolution). **Who certifies the accounts:** a **panel/statutory auditor** (practising CA or a Registrar-panel certified auditor). **Software agent:** may prepare bills, registers, minutes drafts, accounts and the compliance calendar; **cannot** be an office-bearer, cannot sign the audit, cannot file as the society.

# THE APPROVALS MAP
### Every permission a typical Indian multi-storey residential building needs, land purchase → occupancy certificate. Ordered by dependency. Authority | signer | what it waits for.
*(Maharashtra/MoHUA-MBBL worked example; a given city will add or drop items. Items marked ◆ are conditional.)*

**STAGE 0 — Land & title**
1. **Title search & Title Certificate** — Sub-Registrar records | signed by an **Advocate (Bar Council enrolled)** | needs 30/60-yr deeds, 7/12, Property Card.
2. **Measurement / demarcation (mojni)** — District/Taluka Inspector of Land Records | signed by the **Land Records officer / licensed surveyor** | needs 7/12 + application.
3. **Mutation (ferfar) & record correction** — Tahsildar/Circle Officer | **revenue officer's order** | after purchase deed.
4. ◆ **Non-Agricultural (NA) permission / land-use conversion** — Collector / Planning Authority | **Collector's order** | needs title + zone certificate.
5. ◆ **Urban Land Ceiling / tenancy / ceiling clearances** — Collector | **Collector's order**.
6. **Registered Sale Deed / Development Agreement + PoA** — Sub-Registrar | **parties execute; Sub-Registrar registers** | needs stamp duty paid.
7. **Zone certificate / DP remarks (land-use, reservations, road widening, setbacks)** — Planning Authority/ULB | **ULB officer** | before design.

**STAGE 1 — Design-stage NOCs that gate the plan sanction**
8. ◆ **AAI height clearance NOC (NOCAS)** — Airports Authority of India under MoCA GSR 751(E) / Height Restrictions Rules 2015 | **AAI** (auto-settled if under the CCZM permitted top elevation); survey data warranted by the applicant/surveyor | **must precede the final building plan**, because it caps the height.
9. ◆ **Heritage Conservation Committee approval** — ULB HCC | **HCC recommendation + Commissioner** | precedes plan sanction in a listed precinct.
10. ◆ **National Monuments Authority / Competent Authority permission (AMASR ss.20A–20C)** — NMA | **NMA/Competent Authority** | prohibited within 100 m, regulated for a further 200 m.
11. ◆ **CRZ clearance (CRZ Notification 2019)** — SCZMA recommends; State/MoEFCC grants | demarcation only by an **MoEFCC-authorised agency**.
12. ◆ **Tree NOC (felling/transplantation)** — **Tree Authority** of the local body (Maharashtra Trees Act 1975) | **Tree Officer** | often a precondition to sanction or commencement.
13. **Fire scheme approval / provisional Fire NOC** — Chief Fire Officer / Director of Fire Services, to **NBC 2016 Part 4** and the state Fire Act | scheme drawn by the **Licensed Agency / fire consultant**, approved by the **Fire Officer** | **precedes plan sanction for high-rise**.
14. ◆ **Environmental Clearance (EIA Notification 2006, item 8(a), ≥20,000 m² built-up)** — **SEIAA on SEAC's recommendation** | application by the **proponent** through a **QCI/NABET-accredited consultant** | **must precede commencement of construction**. **STATUS FLAGGED AS PARTLY UNSETTLED — see A7.**
15. ◆ **Airport/Defence/Railway/Highway/Pipeline/Metro/Power-line proximity NOCs** — respective authority | authority's officer.
16. ◆ **Civil Aviation/other high-rise NOCs: Traffic police NOC, Garden/Tree, Storm-water, Hydraulic, Sewerage, Environment dept (ULB), Collector NOC for excavation/quarry** — ULB departments | departmental officers | bundled in most OBPAS single windows.

**STAGE 2 — Building permission**
17. **Building plan sanction / Intimation of Disapproval (IOD) with conditions** — ULB / Development Authority, under the **state building bye-laws (MBBL-2016 model)** via **OBPAS/BPMS** | **plans signed and submitted by the registered Architect** (or Licensed Engineer/Surveyor/Supervisor within class limits); **structural design and Structural Stability Certificate by the ULB-registered Structural Engineer**; **soil report by a geotechnical engineer**; filed only from a **registered Architect/Technical Person login**.
18. **Payment of development charges, premium FSI, labour cess (BOCW Act 1996 cess), scrutiny fees** — ULB / Labour Dept.
19. **RERA project registration** — State RERA Authority, **RERA s.4** | applied by the **promoter**; requires the sanctioned plans, the **advocate's title certificate**, the **architect's/engineer's cost and completion-schedule certificates**, and the **designated-account declaration (s.4(2)(l)(D), 70%)** | **must precede any advertising or booking**.
20. **Commencement Certificate (CC) / Building Permit** — ULB | **Commissioner/Building Proposal officer** | after IOD conditions + EC where applicable.
21. ◆ **BOCW registration of the establishment; Factories/Contract Labour registrations; crane & lift (construction) permissions** — Labour Dept.
22. **Electricity: temporary construction power; then sanctioned load / substation approval** — DISCOM under **Electricity Act 2003 s.43**; installation by a **Licensed Electrical Contractor**; **Electrical Inspector's energisation approval** under the CEA Safety Regulations 2010.
23. **Water & drainage connection** — ULB Hydraulic/Water Works | application and completion certificate through a **ULB-licensed Plumber**.

**STAGE 3 — During construction**
24. **Plinth-level checking certificate** — ULB | **Architect/Engineer certifies; ULB verifies** | work above plinth cannot proceed without it.
25. **Periodic RERA compliance: Quarterly Progress Reports; designated-account withdrawals** — the withdrawal gate is **Form 1 (Architect) + Form 2 (Engineer) + Form 3 (practising CA with ICAI COP)** under **MahaRERA Rule 5**; the bank may not release without all three.
26. **Annual audit of project accounts** — **practising CA**, within six months of FY-end (RERA s.4 proviso).
27. ◆ **Revised/amended plan sanctions; EC condition-compliance half-yearly reports; SPCB Consent to Establish** — as applicable.
28. ◆ **Lift: permission to erect** — **Electrical Inspector (Lifts)**; erection only by a **licensed lift contractor** (Maharashtra Lifts, Escalators and Moving Walks Act 2017).

**STAGE 4 — Completion → Occupancy**
29. **Lift operating licence** — **Chief Electrical Inspector**, after the owner's notice within one month of installation.
30. **Final Fire NOC / fire compliance certificate** — **Form "A" from a Licensed Agency** + **Chief Fire Officer's NOC** (mandatory precondition to OC for buildings above 15 m and specified occupancies).
31. **Structural stability certificate (completion)** — **ULB-registered Structural Engineer**.
32. **Drainage/plumbing completion certificate; water connection regularisation; rainwater-harvesting completion** — **licensed Plumber** + ULB Hydraulic Engineer.
33. **Electrical installation completion & energisation; DG set NOC; transformer/substation handover** — **Licensed Electrical Contractor** + **Electrical Inspector** + DISCOM.
34. ◆ **Lift, STP, solar, firefighting commissioning certificates; SPCB Consent to Operate** — contractors + SPCB.
35. **Completion certificate / as-built set & area statement** — **registered Architect** (in Maharashtra, the architect's **Form 4** project completion certificate for MahaRERA).
36. **OCCUPANCY CERTIFICATE (OC) / Occupation Permission** — **ULB (Commissioner / Building Proposal Dept.)**, e.g. MMC Act s.353A | granted on the strength of items 29–35 | **possession may not be offered before this**.
37. **Property-tax assessment; water/electricity meter regularisation; society/association formation (RERA s.11(4)(e) + state rules)**.
38. **Conveyance deed to the association (RERA s.17; MOFA in Maharashtra; else deemed conveyance before the Registrar of Co-operative Societies)**.
39. **Post-OC recurring: fire Form "B" every January and July; lift licence renewal (up to 5 years); structural stability certificate from the 30-year mark then every 10 years (MMC Act s.353B); statutory energy audit if a designated consumer; SPCB CTO renewal; society statutory audit.**

**Sequence truths worth stating plainly:** AAI height NOC → height decision → plans. Fire scheme → plan sanction. EC → commencement (not sanction). Plan sanction + title certificate + designated account → RERA registration → advertising/booking. Registered agreement for sale → more than 10% collection. Form 1/2/3 → every withdrawal. Fire Form A + lift licence + structural stability + plumbing + electrical → OC. OC → possession. OC + society → conveyance.

# PREPARE vs SUBMIT vs CERTIFY
**The operative rule across the whole family: the *application* is almost always the owner/promoter's act; the *technical certification* is almost always reserved to a named licensed professional registered with that specific authority; and *preparation* is unreserved. A software agent can own nearly all of column 1, none of column 2 as a matter of identity, and none of column 3 as a matter of law.**

| Approval / document | ANYONE or SOFTWARE may PREPARE | Only the OWNER / PROMOTER may SUBMIT | Only a LICENSED PROFESSIONAL may CERTIFY |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Title due diligence** | Index search, deed OCR, 30/60-yr chain-of-title table, encumbrance list, defect log, draft opinion | — (the certificate is the advocate's own instrument) | **Advocate enrolled with a State Bar Council** (bank panel advocate for lenders) signs the **Title Certificate** |
| **Sale deed / Development Agreement / PoA** | Drafting, stamp-duty computation, e-registration slot booking, document assembly | Execution and presentation before the **Sub-Registrar** (parties in person or a human PoA holder; Registration Act 1908) | No private certifier; the **Sub-Registrar** registers |
| **Mutation / NA permission / measurement** | Application forms, record extracts, plot plans | Owner files | **Tahsildar / SDO / Collector** orders; **Land Records officer** certifies measurement |
| **Zone certificate / DP remarks** | Request, plot identification | Owner/architect files | ULB planning officer |
| **AAI height NOC (NOCAS)** | CCZM lookup, AMSL comparison, coordinate sheet, full application pre-fill, crane-height study | Owner/proponent files from its own NOCAS account and **warrants the survey data** | **AAI** issues; the survey input in practice from a **competent/licensed surveyor** (no statutorily named signer found — **NOT FOUND**) |
| **Heritage / NMA (AMASR)** | Heritage impact assessment, documentation, drawings, photo survey | Owner files | **HCC / NMA / Competent Authority** approves; drawings signed by the **registered Architect** (conservation architect in practice) |
| **CRZ clearance** | Water balance, project report, condition compliance | Proponent files | **MoEFCC-authorised agency** alone may do the **CRZ demarcation map**; **SCZMA** recommends; State/MoEFCC grants |
| **Tree NOC** | Tree census with girth/species/photos, compensatory plantation plan | Owner files | **Tree Officer / Tree Authority** grants |
| **Environmental Clearance (item 8(a))** | Form 1 / Form 1A, conceptual plan, water & waste computations, traffic study, EMP chapters, six-monthly compliance reports | **Project proponent** files on PARIVESH | Application must come through a **QCI/NABET-accredited EIA consultant organisation**; **SEAC appraises, SEIAA grants**. *Legal position partly UNSETTLED — see A7.* |
| **Fire scheme / provisional & final Fire NOC** | Egress and hydraulic calculations, scheme drawings, equipment schedules, test-log templates, Form A/B pre-fill | Owner/occupier files | **Licensed Agency** licensed by the **Director of Fire Services** signs **Form "A"** (compliance) and **Form "B"** (half-yearly); **Chief Fire Officer** issues the NOC |
| **Building plan sanction / IOD / CC** | Drawings, area & FSI statement, parking statement, auto-scrutiny pre-run, dossier assembly, deviation check | Owner is the applicant of record; **filing happens only from a registered Architect/Technical Person login** | **COA-registered Architect** (any size) or **ULB-licensed Engineer / Licensed Building Surveyor / Supervisor within class limits**; **ULB-registered Structural Engineer** for structural design + **Structural Stability Certificate**; **geotechnical engineer** for the soil report |
| **Plinth checking** | Measurement data, drawings | Owner/architect files | **Architect/Engineer certifies**; ULB verifies |
| **RERA project registration** | The entire application, declarations, uploads, project page content, QPR data | **Promoter** (s.4) — authenticated promoter login, promoter's declaration | **Advocate** (title certificate), **Architect & Engineer** (cost/completion-schedule certificates), **practising CA** (accounts) |
| **RERA designated-account withdrawal** | Percentage-completion computation from the MB, cost-incurred statement, withdrawable-amount computation, all three forms drafted | **Promoter** places the forms before the scheduled bank | **Architect → Form 1**; **Engineer → Form 2**; **practising CA with a valid ICAI COP → Form 3**. Bank may not release without all three (MahaRERA Rule 5 / Circular 39) |
| **RERA annual project audit** | Books, reconciliations, schedules | Promoter | **Practising Chartered Accountant**, within six months of FY-end |
| **Agreement for Sale** | Drafting in the prescribed model form, cost sheet, carpet-area statement, demand schedule | Promoter + allottee execute; registered before the **Sub-Registrar**; **no >10% advance without it (s.13)**; sales by third parties only by a **RERA-registered agent (s.9)** | No private certifier |
| **Electricity sanction / energisation** | Load sheet, SLD, application, estimate check, test-report templates | Owner is the consumer of record | **Licensed Electrical Contractor + Electrical Supervisor** (state Licensing Board) certify the installation; **Electrical Inspector** approves energisation |
| **Water & drainage connection + completion** | Plumbing/drainage layout, demand computation, application | Owner files | **ULB-licensed Plumber** signs the completion certificate; **Hydraulic/Executive Engineer** sanctions |
| **Lift: erect + operate** | Shaft/machine-room compliance check, application, AMC and test calendar, renewal tracking | **Owner** applies and must notify within one month of installation | **Licensed lift contractor** (which must itself hold an electrical contractor licence) erects/maintains/tests; **Electrical Inspector (Lifts)** permits erection; **Chief Electrical Inspector** licenses operation |
| **Completion certificate / OC** | As-built set, area statement, the whole OC dossier, checklist of items 29–35 | Owner/promoter files | **Registered Architect** signs the completion certificate (MahaRERA **Form 4**); plus **Structural Engineer**, **Licensed Fire Agency (Form A)**, **Licensed Plumber**, **Licensed Electrical Contractor**; **ULB Commissioner** issues the **OC** |
| **Possession** | Snag lists, closure workflow, buyer sign-off, handover kit, no-dues statement | Promoter offers possession — **only after OC** | No certifier; the **OC** is the gate |
| **Society / association formation** | By-laws, Form A, members' list, share register, the entire file | **Chief Promoter** (an allottee) applies; the **promoter** has the statutory duty to enable it (RERA s.11(4)(e) + state rules) | **Registrar / Deputy Registrar of Co-operative Societies** registers (or the Competent Authority under the Apartment Ownership Act) |
| **Conveyance deed (s.17) / deemed conveyance** | Drafting, stamp-duty computation, annexure set, deemed-conveyance application bundle | **Promoter** executes; **society/association** accepts; registered before the **Sub-Registrar**; on default the **District Deputy Registrar / Registrar of Co-operative Societies** orders deemed conveyance | No private certifier. *Deadline UNSETTLED in sources — see B8.* |
| **Defect liability (s.14(3), five years)** | Defect register, ageing, classification, notices, back-charge computation, the 5-year clock per unit | **Allottee/association** gives notice; the **promoter** must rectify free of charge | No statutory certifier; a **structural** defect opinion in practice from a **registered Structural Engineer** |
| **Structural stability certificate (MMC Act s.353B)** | Condition survey data, NDT processing, report drafting, 30-yr/10-yr clock tracking, repair BOQ | **Owner or occupier** (post-handover: the **society**) submits to the **Commissioner** | **Only a Structural Engineer registered with that Corporation** — an unregistered engineer's certificate is worthless |
| **Fire Form "B" (half-yearly, Jan & Jul)** | Test logs, deficiency list, pre-filled form, calendar | **Owner/occupier (society)** files with the Fire Officer | **Licensed Agency** only |
| **Statutory energy audit / Energy Manager (EC Act 2001 s.14)** | Energy accounting, EPI benchmarking, full analysis, draft audit report, draft returns | **Designated consumer** files the returns | **Accredited Energy Auditor** conducts and signs the mandatory audit (a merely *Certified* Energy Auditor may not); the designated consumer must **appoint a BEE Certified Energy Manager** — **mandatory, not optional**. *Commercial-building threshold **NOT FOUND** — verify the BEE notification.* |
| **SPCB Consent to Operate / effluent reports** | Operation logs, water balance, sampling schedule, draft returns | **Occupier** applies and files | **Recognised/NABL-accredited environmental laboratory** issues the test report; **SPCB** grants the consent |
| **Waste (SWM 2016 / E-waste / C&D)** | Registers, manifests, returns, segregation SOP | **Bulk waste generator (society/owner)** declares and files | **Authorised recycler/dismantler/TSDF** issues the receipt/manifest |
| **Society statutory audit & returns** | Accounts, schedules, registers, minutes drafts, bills | **Office-bearers (elected members)** file — a paid manager needs a committee resolution | **Auditor on the Registrar's panel / practising CA** |
| **ISO 41001:2018 (FM management system)** | The entire management system, documentation, internal audits | The organisation applies | **Accredited certification body** — voluntary, certifies the organisation not the person; **not a statutory gate** |

**The one-line conclusion for an automation design:** in this family, software's ceiling is the *prepare* column, which is 70–80% of the labour and almost none of the liability. Every *submit* is tied to an authenticated identity of the owner/promoter or a licensee, and every *certify* is tied to a specific register — COA for architects, the ULB's own register for structural engineers and supervisors, the State Bar Council for the title certificate, ICAI's Certificate of Practice for Form 3, the Director of Fire Services' Licensed Agency list for Forms A and B, the state Electrical Licensing Board for electrical work, the Chief Electrical Inspector for lifts, NABET/QCI for EIA, an MoEFCC-authorised agency for CRZ demarcation, and BEE accreditation for the statutory energy audit. An agent that drafts those certificates and lets the licensee sign is useful and lawful; an agent that signs or files them is forgery.
