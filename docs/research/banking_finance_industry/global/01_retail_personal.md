# Segment 01 - Retail & consumer banking, personal finance and consumer protection (worldwide)

Research date: 22 Nov 2026. Scope: branch and contact-centre work, account opening, deposits, KYC and onboarding,
customer service, complaints and ombudsman routes, digital banks / neobanks, credit unions and co-operative banks,
Islamic retail banking, and the person's own money: budgeting, bills, subscriptions, savings, debt help, credit
scores, scam and fraud protection, personal tax, retirement and pension basics, home buying, students, seniors,
expats / migrants / NRIs, gig workers, and family / estate / inheritance paperwork.

How to read the tiers (from the brief):
- **A** = admin / prepare / explain / calculate / track. Safe for an AI employee working for the person or firm.
- **B** = the AI assists a named licensed or accountable human, who decides and signs.
- **C** = reserved for humans. Listed at the end with the reason.

Rules that apply to every role below (stated once instead of 70 times): the AI never moves money, never freezes
or closes an account by itself, never approves or declines an account, KYC, loan or claim, never files a report
on behalf of an institution, and never asks for a PIN, OTP, CVV, full card number or password. When money must
move or a card must be frozen, the AI says which button to tap in the person's own banking app, or prepares the
paper for the person to send.

Source honesty: items marked **[opened]** were fetched and read directly. Items marked **[search]** were read
from search-engine result extracts of the named page (the page title and URL are real, and the figures come from
the extract). Items marked **from memory - verify** were not checked in this session.

## 3. The AI employees (numbered)

Format per role: **What it does** · Hired by · Jobs-to-be-done · Tier and who decides · Data/rails · Regional notes ·
Existing products (evidence it is doable).

### Group A - Working for the bank, credit union or co-op (front office and contact centre)

**1. Contact-Centre Agent Co-pilot**
- What: listens to (or reads) the live customer call or chat and hands the human agent the answer, the policy, the next step and a draft wrap-up note.
- Hired by: bank, credit union, neobank, BPO running bank contact centres.
- Jobs: live transcript and intent; pull the product policy and fee table; prompt identity-check steps already in the script (never collect OTP itself); suggest compliant wording (vulnerability cues, complaint expression of dissatisfaction); auto-fill CRM case; draft after-call summary; flag a call that is legally a complaint; QA score against script; prepare follow-up letter/email.
- Tier: B - the human agent speaks and decides.
- Data/rails: CCaaS audio stream (Genesys/Nice/Five9/Twilio), CRM, knowledge base, core banking read APIs.
- Regional: UK DISP complaint definition + Consumer Duty outcomes; US Reg E/Z dispute triggers; India RB-IOS escalation timeline (30 days); call recording consent rules vary by state/country (check).
- Products: interface.ai Nexus "agentic CCaaS" for credit unions [search: cuinsight.com]; Posh (credit-union CUSO, phone bot, CoachQA oversight 2026) [search: businesswire.com].

**2. Customer Service Chat and Voice Agent (first line)**
- What: answers members/customers 24/7 in their language - balances and transactions they are already signed in to see, how-to, fees, card lost/stolen steps, branch hours - and hands to a human with context.
- Hired by: bank, CU, co-op bank, neobank, mobile-money provider.
- Jobs: authenticated FAQ; explain a transaction line; guide the customer to freeze a card in-app; book branch or callback; replacement-card request intake; travel notice; statement copy request; status of a dispute or complaint; warm hand-off with summary; vernacular/low-literacy voice.
- Tier: A (information) / B for anything that changes the account - the customer taps or a staff member acts.
- Data/rails: digital banking session, read APIs, ticketing.
- Regional: language mix (Hinglish, Arabic, Swahili, Tagalog, Portuguese); accessibility duties (UK Equality Act, US ADA) (check).
- Products: Bank of America Erica - 20.6 m users, ~700 m interactions in 2025, 3.2 bn+ since 2018 [search: newsroom.bankofamerica.com]; Klarna assistant did two-thirds of chats in month one, ~700 FTE equivalent (company claim) [search: klarna.com press]; Posh phone bot up to 97% containment (vendor) [search].

**3. Branch Concierge and Appointment Coordinator**
- What: books, prepares and follows up branch visits so the customer arrives with the right papers and the banker has the case ready.
- Hired by: bank, CU, co-op bank.
- Jobs: appointment booking; document checklist per purpose (account opening, locker, loan, nominee change, deceased claim); pre-fill forms; queue/token updates; post-visit follow-up; feedback survey; translate forms.
- Tier: A.
- Data/rails: appointment system, forms library, SMS/WhatsApp Business official API.
- Regional: India branch forms (nomination DA1/DA2/DA3, from memory - verify); EU multilingual forms.
- Products: branch scheduling tools (Coconut Software, from memory - verify).

**4. New-Account Application Preparer**
- What: walks a prospect through account opening, assembles a complete, consistent application and flags gaps before the bank's own checks.
- Hired by: bank, CU, neobank, co-op bank (also a consumer for themselves).
- Jobs: product-fit questions (basic/no-frills vs premium); document list by customer type (minor, NRI, senior, student, sole trader); data-consistency check (name/address/date across documents); pre-fill; explain fees and minimum balance; schedule video-KYC slot; track application status; draft "why was I declined" follow-up questions.
- Tier: A prepare; account approval is the bank's (C).
- Data/rails: digital onboarding stack, ID document capture (customer uploads), e-KYC rails (Aadhaar e-KYC, Singpass Myinfo, UAE Pass), CIP data.
- Regional: India basic savings account (BSBDA) right; EU Payment Accounts Directive right to a basic account; US Bank On certified accounts (from memory - verify).
- Products: digital onboarding vendors (Alloy, Onfido/Entrust, IDnow, Signzy; from memory - verify).

**5. KYC File Preparer and Checker (for the KYC officer)**
- What: assembles the KYC/CDD file, checks documents for consistency, expiry and obvious tampering signals, and writes the officer a summary; the officer decides.
- Hired by: bank, CU, NBFC, neobank, payments/e-money firm.
- Jobs: document completeness; OCR and cross-field match; expiry and re-KYC due dates; name screening pre-check summary (hits explained, not decided); source-of-funds questionnaire; risk-rating input sheet; missing-items letter to customer; audit trail.
- Tier: B - KYC officer / MLRO decides; AI never clears or rejects.
- Data/rails: CKYC (IN), sanctions/PEP lists, ID docs, registry lookups.
- Regional: FATF R.10; US CIP/CDD rule; EU AMLR; India RBI KYC Master Direction (V-CIP); GCC central bank AML rules.
- Products: KYC automation vendors (from memory - verify: Fenergo, Signzy, Sumsub).

**6. Periodic KYC Refresh / Re-KYC Outreach Assistant**
- What: finds customers whose KYC is due, explains plainly what is needed, and collects it through official channels so accounts are not frozen for lapsed KYC.
- Hired by: bank, CU, co-op bank, mobile-money provider.
- Jobs: due-list build; multilingual reminders; self-declaration "no change" forms where allowed; doc upload chase; branch/video slot; status board; escalation to staff; record of contacts.
- Tier: A.
- Regional: India RBI allows self-declaration for no-change re-KYC (from memory - verify); Nigeria BVN/NIN linkage drives; Kenya SIM/e-money tier upgrades.

**7. Deposit Operations Back-Office Clerk**
- What: prepares the paper and system entries for everyday account servicing - address change, nominee, standing instructions, interest certificates, closure requests - for a staff member to approve.
- Hired by: bank, CU, co-op bank.
- Jobs: read the request; check signatures-on-file match is flagged for a human (never decided by AI); prepare maker entry for checker; generate interest/TDS certificates; balance confirmation letters; closure final-statement calc; dormant-account reactivation pack; record retention.
- Tier: B (maker-checker: human checker).
- Regional: dormant/unclaimed rules (India DEA Fund after 10 years; US state escheat; UK Dormant Assets Scheme) (from memory - verify).

**8. Disputes and Chargeback Case Preparer**
- What: turns a customer's "I didn't make this" or "I paid but didn't get it" into a complete dispute case with the right reason code and deadlines.
- Hired by: bank, CU, card issuer, neobank.
- Jobs: classify (unauthorised vs merchant dispute vs error); collect evidence list; card-scheme reason-code mapping; regulatory clock (US Reg E 10/45 days, Reg Z 60-day notice; EU PSD2 unauthorised refund by end of next business day); provisional-credit eligibility note; letters to customer; merchant response pack review; status updates.
- Tier: B - dispute officer decides credit/denial.
- Data/rails: card processor, Visa/Mastercard dispute systems (VROL, Mastercom), core banking.
- Regional: UPI disputes via NPCI UDIR (India); Pix MED 2.0 (Brazil); PSR APP reimbursement (UK).

**9. Complaints Handler Assistant**
- What: registers, classifies, timetables and drafts responses for complaints, and keeps the handler inside the legal clock.
- Hired by: bank, CU, NBFC, payments firm, co-op bank.
- Jobs: detect a complaint in any channel; log and acknowledge; classify by product/root cause; draft investigation plan and evidence request; draft final response / summary resolution letter with ombudsman referral wording; redress calculation sheet (interest at statutory rate) for approval; deadline tracker; ombudsman case pack if escalated.
- Tier: B - complaints handler decides and signs.
- Regional: UK DISP 8 weeks (15 business days for payment services); India RB-IOS 30 days then ombudsman; UAE 15 days before Sanadak [search]; Australia RG 271 IDR 30 days (from memory - verify); US no fixed federal clock except CFPB portal 15/60 days (from memory - verify).
- Evidence of demand: FOS 305,726 complaints 2024/25, +54%; credit cards 60,364, current accounts 36,221; fraud & scams 35,416; 34% upheld [opened: financial-ombudsman.org.uk].

**10. Complaints Root-Cause and Conduct MI Analyst**
- What: reads all complaints, chats and call notes, finds patterns and drafts the board/regulator MI pack.
- Hired by: bank, CU, consumer-duty / conduct teams.
- Jobs: theme clustering; trend vs last period; root-cause hypotheses with examples; vulnerable-customer outcome tracking; draft Consumer Duty annual board report sections; regulator complaints return pre-fill (FCA complaints return; RBI data) for the human filer; remediation-population sizing.
- Tier: B - head of complaints / compliance files.

**11. Scam and Fraud Victim Case Assistant (institution side)**
- What: supports the fraud team in handling scam claims fairly and on time.
- Hired by: bank, CU, payment service provider.
- Jobs: claim intake script; evidence summary; reimbursement-rule check (UK £85,000 cap, 5 business days, consumer-standard-of-caution exception) drafted for decision; receiving-bank recovery request pack; customer updates; vulnerable-customer flag; post-case education note.
- Tier: B.
- Regional: UK PSR rules (first year £173 m reimbursed, ~188,000 in-scope claims) [search: thepaypers.com, psr.org.uk]; Singapore SRF; Brazil MED 2.0; Australia SPF (check).

**12. Vulnerable-Customer Support Assistant**
- What: notices signs of vulnerability (bereavement, illness, debt stress, coercion, low capability), records needs with consent and adapts the journey.
- Hired by: bank, CU, collections teams.
- Jobs: cue detection in chats/calls; consent-based needs register; adapt communication (plain language, larger text, third-party authority); referral list (debt charities, carers' groups, domestic-abuse financial support); follow-up reminders; outcome monitoring.
- Tier: B.
- Regional: UK FCA FG21/1 guidance on vulnerable customers + Consumer Duty; Australia Banking Code; US elder-abuse reporting rules (from memory - verify).

**13. Collections and Hardship Assistant**
- What: early, respectful outreach to customers who are behind, and preparation of affordable plans for a human to agree.
- Hired by: bank, CU, NBFC, BNPL, utility-like lenders.
- Jobs: pre-delinquency nudges; income-expenditure form; affordability calc using standard spending guides; plan options drafted; compliant letters (no threats; contact hours); hardship evidence pack; referral to free debt advice; broken-promise tracking.
- Tier: B - agreeing a settlement or write-off stays human.
- Regional: US FDCPA / Reg F (7 calls in 7 days, from memory - verify); UK CONC 7 and Standard Financial Statement; India RBI fair-practices code on recovery agents; Australia hardship notices under NCCP.
- Products: interface.ai Smart Collections agent [search: cuinsight.com].

**14. Deposit Product and Rate Explainer**
- What: compares the bank's own savings/term-deposit/current-account products for a customer and prepares the mandated disclosures.
- Hired by: bank, CU, co-op bank.
- Jobs: needs questions; product comparison with fees and rates; maturity calendar; auto-renewal warnings; Truth in Savings / Key Facts document generation; deposit-insurance cover explanation; record of what was shown.
- Tier: A (bank's own products, factual) - personal investment advice is out of scope.
- Regional: US TISA; EU PAD fee information document; UK FSCS cover; India DICGC (from memory - verify).

**15. Safe-Deposit Locker and Custody Admin Assistant**
- What: runs the paperwork of lockers: agreements, rent reminders, nominee/joint-hirer access, inventory, break-open notices.
- Hired by: bank, co-op bank.
- Jobs: agreement renewal tracking; rent dues; access log summaries; nomination; deceased-hirer claim checklist; notice drafting.
- Tier: A/B (access decisions human).
- Regional: India RBI locker agreement rules 2021-23 (from memory - verify); rare in US/UK (declining).

## 5. Never an AI employee (tier C) in this segment

| Job | Why it stays human |
|---|---|
| Teller / cashier handling cash, cheques, valuables | Physical custody of money and instruments; signature verification liability |
| Approving or declining an account, KYC, or a customer risk rating | Legal accountability of the regulated firm (FATF R.10, CIP, RBI KYC MD); brief's hard limit |
| Freezing, blocking or closing an account; placing holds | Brief's hard limit; due-process and consumer-protection rules |
| Filing a SAR/STR, CTR or regulatory return as the institution | Named MLRO / compliance officer duty; tipping-off offences |
| Deciding a dispute, chargeback or scam reimbursement | Firm's liability decision; regulators expect accountable decision-maker |
| Agreeing a debt settlement, write-off, or payment plan binding on the lender; making collection calls that pressure | Lender's credit decision; FDCPA/CONC conduct risk |
| Regulated debt advice (UK FCA debt counselling, Australian financial counsellors) and personalised investment/pension/mortgage advice | Licensing (FCA, ASIC AFSL, SEBI RIA, US RIA/FINRA); AI may prepare, a licensed human advises |
| Signing a tax return as a paid preparer / representing before the tax authority | PTIN/EA/CPA, registered tax agent, CA rules |
| Acting as attorney under a POA, trustee, executor, or guardian | Fiduciary appointment by law/court |
| Drafting/attesting a will, giving legal advice, probate or succession court filings | Reserved legal activities in many jurisdictions; witnessing |
| Notarisation, attestation, witnessing signatures | Physical/legal presence requirement |
| Issuing a Shariah ruling (fatwa) or Shariah certification of products | Shariah Supervisory Board authority |
| Ombudsman / ADR adjudication | Statutory adjudicator role |
| Moving money, paying bills, sending remittances "on the person's behalf" | Brief's hard limit - person taps in their own app |
| Identity verification that needs secrets (OTP, PIN, passwords) | Never requested by the AI |

## 6. Top 10 roles to build first, and why

1. **Money Safety Assistant (37)** - biggest, fastest-growing pain worldwide (FTC US$15.9 bn; UK APP +19%; GASA 57% met a scam); pure tier A; works in every country with a local pattern library; natural voice-call use ("is this call real?").
2. **Scam Recovery and Reporting Assistant (38)** - the minutes after a scam decide recovery (UK reimbursement, Brazil MED 2.0 80-day window, Singapore SRF); one assistant that writes every report and claim is rare and clearly valuable.
3. **Bank Complaint and Ombudsman Escalation Assistant (43)** - every region has an ombudsman with rising volumes; self-filers do better than paid representatives at FOS (37% vs 27%); pure drafting + deadlines.
4. **Credit Report Checker and Dispute-Letter Drafter (31)** - the single largest complaint category in the US (88% of CFPB complaints); clear statutory clocks; easy to measure success.
5. **Subscription Auditor and Bills Manager (23 + 22)** - universal, frequent, proven willingness to pay (Rocket Money), and federal US rule gap makes self-help more needed.
6. **Bereavement Money Administrator (64)** - high emotion, high paperwork, few good tools (Empathy proves demand); pairs with the institution-side role 65 for banks.
7. **Budget and Cash-Flow Coach with aggregation (21 + 26)** - daily-use anchor role that feeds every other role; open-banking rails exist (AA, CDR, OBIE, Open Finance Brasil) even if US 1033 is in flux.
8. **Contact-Centre Agent Co-pilot (1)** - the B2B entry for banks/credit unions; proven by Erica, Klarna, interface.ai, Posh; tier B keeps humans deciding.
9. **Personal Tax Organiser and Return Preparer (46 + 47 + 48)** - annual spike, huge market, proven AI uptake (H&R Block AI Tax Assist); prepare-and-review model fits tier A/B cleanly.
10. **Debt Help Navigator (30)** - rising arrears (StepChange), clear regulated hand-off point to free advice; builds trust and retention for the whole department.

Runner-ups: Expat/NRI Banking Assistant (58) and Remittance Comparer (59) for the global migrant audience; Senior Money Guardian (40) given FinCEN elder-exploitation data; Credit Union Member Services (17) as a 412 m-member B2B niche; Islamic Retail Banking Product Assistant (20) for GCC/Malaysia/Nigeria.
