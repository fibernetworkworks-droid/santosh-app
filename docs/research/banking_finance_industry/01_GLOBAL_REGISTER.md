## How to read it

- **Tier A** - she runs it for herself or her firm: it prepares, checks, explains, calculates, drafts, tracks and reminds.
- **Tier B** - it works for a named licensed or accountable person (adviser, credit officer, MLRO, auditor, CFO), who decides, signs or files.
- **Tier C** - never an AI employee (list below). The AI never moves money, places an order, approves a loan / claim / account / KYC, freezes an account, files as the institution, signs an opinion, or gives licensed advice.
- **Id** `03.2` = part 03, role 2 - open that part to read the role in full. *Also* = the same role found by another part (merged here).
- **Hoola today** - what Hoola already does for that role (Built / Partly / India only). Blank = not built.

## What the research found

- **Scams are the biggest pain on every continent.** GASA puts losses near US$1 trillion a year with ~4% recovered; the FTC logged US$15.9 bn of US losses in 2025 and UK APP fraud was £576 m (+19%). Refund rules now differ sharply by country (UK PSR, Singapore SRF, Brazil Pix MED 2.0, EU PSD3/PSR), so scam check before paying and the scam refund claim are the strongest tier A employees - with one pack per country.
- **Complaints and credit reports are huge, measured demand.** About 88% of the CFPB's 6.6 m complaints in 2025 were credit reporting; UK ombudsman cases rose 54% to 305,918 in 2024/25; India's RBI ombudsman took ~13.3 lakh. Every region has an ombudsman ladder that a letter-writer can follow.
- **Forced demand from new rules.** E-invoicing mandates (EU ViDA, Belgium / France / Poland / Germany 2026-28, Saudi, Malaysia, Nigeria, Vietnam...), T+1 settlement (US now, EU and UK 11 Oct 2027), ISO 20022 structured addresses (14 Nov 2026), DORA and CPS 230 third-party registers, the EU AI Act, and the US model-risk overhaul (SR 26-2) all create paperwork that has to be done by a date.
- **Small firms have no compliance team.** A 'Compliance Officer for One' for small fintechs, lenders, brokers and money services, and the bookkeeper / AP / credit-control stack for every SME, fit Hoola's existing users. 59% of US small businesses have invoices 30+ days overdue.
- **Proven paid markets for tier B.** Adviser meeting notes and suitability letters (Jump, Zocks), pitchbooks and models (Rogo ~US$2 bn), AML alert triage where 90-99% of alerts are false positives, chargebacks (261 m a year, US$33.8 bn), reconciliation and month-end close (Numeric, BlackLine, FloQast).
- **Inclusion gaps are large and global.** SME finance gap in emerging markets US$5.7 tn; farmers US$170-200 bn a year; 1.3-1.4 bn adults without an account; sending US$200 home costs 6.4% on average (8.8% into Sub-Saharan Africa); savings groups and mobile-money agents still run on paper.
- **The line not to cross is getting clearer.** FINRA's 2026 report flags AI agents' autonomy; some brokers now let AI place trades and one agri lender lets AI make the final credit call. Hoola stays on the prepare side of every one of those acts (tier C below).

## Never an AI employee (tier C)

| Act | Why it stays with a person |
|---|---|
| Moving money, paying, sending a remittance, releasing a settlement instruction | Money moves only on her own tap in her own app / bank; for a firm, a named payments officer releases |
| Placing, changing or cancelling an order; confirming a trade; setting margin | Broker / dealer licence; market-abuse liability |
| Approving or declining a loan, claim, account, KYC, benefit or a customer risk rating | The regulated firm or public body is accountable (FATF R.10 and every national KYC rule) |
| Freezing, blocking or closing an account; placing holds; deciding a dispute or a scam refund | Due process; the firm's liability decision |
| Filing a SAR / STR, regulatory, tax or statistical return AS the institution or for a client as their agent | Named MLRO / reporting officer / registered tax agent; tipping-off offences |
| Signing an audit opinion, valuation, actuarial or rating opinion, financial statements, a fairness opinion | Licensed professional's signature and liability |
| Personalised regulated advice - investment, pension, mortgage, debt counselling, insurance | Licensing (SEC/FINRA, FCA, SEBI, ASIC AFSL, MAS FAA...); Hoola explains and prepares, the adviser advises |
| Agreeing a settlement, write-off or binding payment plan; collection calls that pressure | Lender's credit decision; FDCPA / CONC-style conduct rules |
| Publishing research, issuing a rating or a listing / index decision | Research and rating-agency rules |
| Acting as attorney, trustee, executor, guardian; probate, will drafting and legal advice | Fiduciary or court appointment; reserved legal activities |
| Issuing a Shariah ruling or certifying a product | Shariah Supervisory Board authority |
| Ombudsman / ADR adjudication, regulator enforcement or licensing decisions, fund freezes | Statutory decision-makers |
| Cash, cheque and valuables handling, notarising, witnessing signatures | Physical custody and presence |
