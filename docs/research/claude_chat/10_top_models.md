## What it says

1. **Google's Gemini 4 leads almost every text field.** Gemini 4 Carbon is #1 in 12 of 19 fields and Gemini 4 Argon
   #1 or #2 in 15, both at Rs 4.22 a step: half the price of Claude Opus 5.5 (Rs 8.45) and a fifth of Claude Fable.
2. **Gemini 4 Carbon is refused on her OpenRouter account** (17 Nov: "is banned"). Argon is the safe top choice: it is
   within 1-11 points of Carbon everywhere, with 3-6x more votes. It has not been called from her account yet.
3. **Claude leads where work is built or read closely:** apps and websites (Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5), pictures and PDFs
   (Fable 5 / 5.1), long tool jobs (Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5). It costs 2-5x Gemini 4 Argon.
4. **Gemini 3.8 Flash is the top value.** Within 5-20 points of the leaders in maths, medicine, pictures and reading text
   in pictures, at Rs 1.58 a step.
5. **The cheap open models (GLM, DeepSeek, Qwen 27B) are not near the top** in any field: 30-60 points behind. They are
   the right workers for simple jobs, not the best performers.
6. **Media leaders are also cheap:** Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash for video, GPT-Image-2.5 at Rs 3.41 a picture.
