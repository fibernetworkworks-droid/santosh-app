# Hoola: hero demo script + 30 short-video scripts

For YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Google/YouTube ads. Audience: small business owners worldwide (English first).

**One promise, used everywhere:** *Describe your business in one line. Get your whole website, and an AI employee that runs it.*

**One call to action, used everywhere:** *Try Hoola free* (with the link in the bio or description).

## Rules before anything is posted

1. **Record the real product only.** Every screen in these scripts is something Hoola really does. Never fake a screen, a result, a customer or a review. Ad platforms ban fake demos and fake testimonials, and one exposed fake kills trust.
2. **Be honest about time.** The three full looks take a few minutes, not seconds. Show the real wait with a visible timer, then use an on-screen "3 minutes later" cut. Don't claim "in 10 seconds".
3. **Speed the waiting up, never the result.** You may speed up the waiting parts (marked "fast-forward" below). Never change what Hoola actually made.
4. **The free plan must exist before "Try free" goes live.** Until then, use "Join the waitlist".
5. **Disclose paid creator videos** with #ad / "Paid partnership" on every platform.
6. **Use real customers only after written permission.** Scripts marked [CUSTOMER] need a real customer and their written OK; until then, use your own demo businesses.

**Format for all shorts:** vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, captions burned in (most people watch without sound), the hook in the first 2 seconds, 15–45 seconds long.

### How to record it

- Screen-record the console at 1440×900, and the phone with real screen recording, not a mock-up.
- Use a clean demo account with no other projects visible.
- Light theme, browser zoom 110% so the text is readable on a phone.
- Add a soft music bed. Record the voiceover separately; a calm, confident voice works better than a hype voice.
- Export 16:9 for YouTube and the website, and 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts (crop to the panel being used).

## 30 SHORT-VIDEO SCRIPTS

Six series, five scripts each. Post 1–2 a day; reuse the winners as ads.

Each script lists: **Hook** (the first 2 seconds, on screen and spoken), **Shots**, **Voiceover / captions**, **Ending**.

### Series A: Before → After (the website magic)

**A1. "My bakery had no website"**
- Hook: "My bakery had NO website. Watch." (empty Google result for the bakery)
- Shots: type *"Home bakery in Toronto, custom cakes, order by WhatsApp"* → fast-forward → three looks → the chosen one live on a phone.
- VO: "No designer. No code. One line, three full designs, and I picked this one."
- Ending: "Link in bio to try yours."

**A2. "Same prompt, three websites"**
- Hook: "I gave AI ONE sentence. It gave me THREE websites."
- Shots: the three looks side by side, then a fast swipe through every page of each.
- VO: "Every page, three styles. Which one would you pick? Comment 1, 2 or 3."
- Ending: the comment prompt (it drives comments, which the algorithm rewards).

**A3. "Website for my dad's hardware shop"**
- Hook: "My dad has run his shop for 30 years with no website. Until today."
- Shots: dad's shop in real life (with permission) → typing → the live site on his phone → his reaction.
- VO: "One line, a few minutes, and he approved it himself."
- Ending: "Tag someone whose business needs this." [CUSTOMER / family, with permission]

**A4. "Dentist website, start to finish"**
- Hook: "A full dentist website. Start to finish. No skipping." (a real timer in the corner)
- Shots: an honest full run with the timer visible; fast-forward only the waiting, and show the timer.
- VO: "This is the real time it takes. Pages, booking, contact. Done."
- Ending: "Real time: X minutes." (the true number)

**A5. "From a napkin to a website"**
- Hook: "I wrote my business idea on a napkin…"
- Shots: a napkin with the idea → typed into Hoola → the live website.
- VO: "…and Hoola turned it into this."
- Ending: "What's your napkin idea? Comment it."

### Series B: The AI Employee (the "who runs it" story)

**B1. "I hired an AI employee for my Instagram"**

**B2. "My AI employee's first day"**

**B3. "AI answered my customer at 2 AM"**
